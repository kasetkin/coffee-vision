Ticket ML-29 · coffee-vision · what both models read

Canonicalization honours every colour declaration, reads HDR main images, and tries applying gain maps

Two goals, the owner's of 2026-11-14: read every photo correctly, and use as much of what a photo file carries as we can. The first is a set of fixes with no retrain: a file's colour declaration is honoured or refused, never guessed, in every accepted format, and an HDR main image is tone-mapped into today's canonical photo. The second is an experiment, judged by the owner before anything is adopted: applying the gain maps that 61% of the dataset's photos carry. This ticket absorbs ML-20 and OPS-24. Decided and planned (P1–P11); not implemented.

1. Summary

Today canonicalization takes a photo's colour from its ICC profile, or reads it as sRGB; a JPEG XL is read in two header enumerations only. A file that declares its colour another way is either refused (JPEG XL) or accepted and read wrong (HEIC, AVIF). Every second image in a file is ignored. After this ticket:

  • Part 1, read correctly. Every colour declaration is honoured or refused (ADR 0040); a 10- or 12-bit AVIF keeps its depth; a float JPEG XL is read; an HDR main image (PQ, HLG, values above white) is tone-mapped by one operator into the standard-range canonical photo. No dataset photo changes, so nothing is retrained.
  • Part 2, use more. A gain map is applied at canonicalization and brought back to standard range with the same operator. This changes 1,731 dataset photos, so it is checked in three steps with a stop for the owner, and adopted only past a bar fixed now (D12, D16).

The terms are GLOSSARY.md's: main image, second image, gain map, colour declaration, and the four meanings of HDR.

2. Background

All of it measured 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14 from the files' bytes; the tables, scripts and limits are in analysis/ml29/README.md.

  • How colour is read today. canonical.py, decode: JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF and BMP take Pillow's icc_profile, HEIC pillow-heif's; no profile, or one LittleCMS cannot use as RGB, means sRGB (ML-19 D16). RAW is developed into linear Rec.2020. _decode_jxl reads only the enumerations (sRGB, sRGB) and (BT2100, BT709), and refuses an embedded ICC profile and float samples.

  • The owner's darktable exports of one photo. Of 11 JPEG XL, only the four integer sRGB ones are read; linear Rec2020, Display P3, Adobe RGB (custom primaries with an explicit gamma), PQ, HLG and 32-bit float are refused. Of 5 AVIF and 6 HEIC, all are accepted, and three are read wrong because their only declaration is a coded tag: linear Rec.2020 as AVIF and as HEIC (65 of 255 levels off) and Display P3 as HEIC (2 levels off, up to 8). The 10- and 12-bit AVIFs are decoded at 8 bits, against ML-19 D36.
  • The dataset is read correctly today. 2,851 photos. Every HEIC and 1,715 of 2,556 JPEGs have an ICC profile; the 841 JPEGs without one carry no Adobe RGB tag; the 9 PNGs with legacy chunks carry sRGB's own values; no main image is PQ or HLG; every JPEG, HEIC and PNG is 8-bit.
  • What the dataset's files carry unused. A gain map in 1,731 photos (61%): 1,570 Ultra HDR JPEGs (Google 984, OnePlus 7 Pro 536, Samsung 50) and 161 iPhone HEICs; 208 of them also carry the ISO 21496-1 form. In every one the main image is standard-range. Also: depth or disparity 8, mattes and style maps 6, motion-photo video 27, a DNG local tone map 71, and capture metadata (subject distance 1,687, focal length 2,181).
  • What the gain maps ask for in the bean region. On 206 photos with a hand-checked mask: a mean boost of 0.43 stops, by brand 0.37 (Google), 0.43 (OnePlus 7 Pro), 0.86 (Samsung), 0.44 (Apple). On the Android phones the boost is one value over the whole region in about half the photos and varies little in the rest; only Apple's maps vary inside the region in every photo. Sony (455 photos), the other OnePlus (262) and every raw have no map: the Sony phone's HDR setting merges exposures into one standard-range JPEG and leaves nothing in the file.
  • Storage has the room. A canonical photo is already a 16-bit JPEG XL. A gain-map result needs about 12 bits; what would stop an HDR picture being stored is the canonical photo's standard-range colour encoding (ML-19 D7), which D2 keeps.

3. Before and after

Now

  • Colour comes from an ICC profile or is taken as sRGB; a coded tag is not read.
  • A JPEG XL is read in two enumerations; float and every other declaration are refused.
  • A linear Rec.2020 or Display P3 AVIF or HEIC without a profile is accepted and read wrong.
  • A 10- or 12-bit AVIF is decoded at 8 bits.
  • A PQ or HLG main image is refused (JPEG XL) or read as sRGB (HEIC, AVIF, PNG).
  • Gain maps and every other second image are ignored.

After

  • A colour declaration is honoured or refused: ICC profile, then coded tag or enumeration, then R03, then sRGB (ADR 0040).
  • Every JPEG XL header declaration is read, float included; an embedded ICC profile is refused with a message.
  • An AVIF keeps its bit depth.
  • A PQ or HLG main image, and values above white, are tone-mapped by one operator into the standard-range canonical photo.
  • If the owner adopts Part 2: a gain map is applied and brought back with that operator. If not: ignored by decision, recorded in .out-of-scope/.
  • Every other second image is ignored by rule, stated here.

4. What does not change

  • The canonical photo. Rec.2020 with the BT.709 transfer, standard-range, 16-bit lossy JPEG XL, read back as 8-bit sRGB (ML-19 D2, D7; ADR 0033). Only what is read into it changes.
  • Part 1 changes no dataset photo. The scan found none that the new rule reads differently, so there is no re-canonicalization and no retrain. A phase that finds otherwise stops and says so.
  • The format list. No format is added or removed (ADR 0034).
  • A photo with no declaration. Still read as sRGB (ML-19 D16).
  • All cameras equal. Part 2 is reported per camera and judged by the owner; it adds no camera-specific rule (ADR 0010).
  • The site, during Part 2's experiment. Untouched until adoption (D15).

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1A file accepted today is refused after Part 1. Only a file whose declaration cannot be converted: a coded tag with a reserved value, a JPEG XL with an embedded ICC profile (already refused). Legacy PNG and BMP fields are ignored, not refused (D7). The refusal names the declaration.
R2A profile built in code differs by rounding from a reference profile and moves pixels. ML-19 D46 measured 3% of 8-bit pixels moving one level. Here it touches only photos no model has seen. Tests pin the builder: each of the owner's exports lands within a stated tolerance of the sRGB export.
R3The tone-mapping operator is a matter of taste. D11 makes it a measurement: the owner's PQ and HLG exports against the sRGB export of the same photo. PQ and HLG as AVIF and HEIC are re-encoded here from those exports' pixels, and public same-scene HDR and standard-range samples add a second set (D25). The owner picks the operator from the report (plan P4).
R4Applying gain maps adds a brightness difference between camera brands. Measured: 0.37 to 0.86 stops by brand in the bean region, nothing for Sony, the other OnePlus and raws. D13: every result of Part 2 is reported per camera; D16 fixes the adoption bar; the owner decides.
R5The ISO 21496-1 reader is wrong. 208 dataset files carry both forms: the ISO result is checked against the vendor map's on them before it is trusted (D14).
R6Gain maps cost the front time and memory. Not measured. Only matters at adoption (D15); the adoption release measures it against the front's budget (ML-19 D22, D38).
R7The 16-bit AVIF decode needs a decoder the front does not have. To be verified in the first phase (D9); imagecodecs and pillow-heif are both installed already.

7. Scope and acceptance

Part 1: read correctly.

  • Each of the owner's 22 darktable exports is read, and its canonical photo lands within a stated tolerance of the 8-bit sRGB export's; the three files misread today (linear Rec.2020 AVIF and HEIC, Display P3 HEIC) are among them.
  • Files with the declarations darktable's unexported profiles would carry (linear Rec709, Rec709, PQ P3, HLG P3, linear ProPhoto), and PQ and HLG as AVIF and HEIC, are made here from the exports in hand (D25), read from their bytes and added to the table as made here.
  • Tests for the precedence: a file with an ICC profile and a coded tag, a coded tag alone, "unspecified", R03, PNG cICP and sRGB, legacy PNG chunks and BMP endpoints ignored, a reserved coded value refused with its message.
  • A 10- and a 12-bit AVIF reach the canonical photo at their own depth.
  • The tone-mapping research: its sources, its candidates, the measurement on the owner's PQ and HLG exports and on public samples, and the operator the owner chose.
  • Every dataset photo canonicalizes to the bytes it has today (scan_dataset.py's "read today as" against the new rule, then the canonical store's hashes).
  • openapi.json, llms.txt and the refusal message say what is read.
  • A darktable linear Rec2020 export gets an answer on the live site.

Part 2: use more.

  • The ISO 21496-1 reader agrees with the vendor map on the files that carry both (R5).
  • Step 1's report: per camera, the change in the bean region, and the crops the owner looked at.
  • Step 2's report: answers, masks and OOD verdicts that change with today's models. The owner's decision to run step 3 or stop.
  • Step 3, if run: the paired three-seed comparison, per class and per camera, against D16's bar, and the owner's decision.
  • Adopted: one release of store, front and model, with the front's time and memory measured (R6). Not adopted: .out-of-scope/ gains the idea and its measurements.

Out of scope

  • A canonical photo that keeps HDR, or models that read more than 8-bit sRGB (D2). It would amend ADR 0033 and re-canonicalize every photo; proposed again only if the owner wants the models' input changed.
  • A gain map given to the models as an extra input, and gain maps applied for one brand only (D10).
  • Depth or disparity as a segmenter hint: 8 photos carry one (D3).
  • The DNG local tone map (D18).
  • A decoder for a JPEG XL's embedded ICC profile; unblocked when imagecodecs returns it (D22).
  • JPEG XL animation and extra channels.
  • Capture metadata as a signal (ML-30).
  • Raising the 16 MP cap on RAW uploads.
