Owner request, 2026-11-02. Make a clean split between the web-page part of the server and the ML part that serves the model. The proxy has fast connections and very good stability but small RAM, low CPU and no GPU. Move the site from yappy to the proxy, so TLS terminates at the proxy; check the image there; send it to yappy through the tunnel and run all of /analyze on yappy.

1. Summary

Today yappy does everything: its nginx terminates TLS, serves the page and passes /analyze to gunicorn, and the proxy only passes TLS through to it. After this ticket the service has two parts (ADR 0031):

  • The front (the proxy): TLS, the page, the busy gate, the size cap, a check of the upload's format, the visitor-facing logs, and later analytics (OPS-3) and the queue (OPS-8).
  • The inference host (yappy): decoding, both models and the streamed answer, behind the tailnet only.

Both parts ship as one release, deployed and rolled back together. The work splits into the front's check service, the inference host's site, the front's site and page, the two-host deploy, the proxy's setup, and the cutover (plan).

2. Background

Read at d9212c6 and measured on both hosts on 2026-11-02.

  • Today's path. The proxy's nginx stream block reads the SNI and passes the domain's TLS connection to yappy:12346 over Tailscale. yappy's nginx terminates TLS, serves webapp/static/ from the release, and proxies /analyze with limit_conn 1, client_max_body_size 20m and proxy_read_timeout 30s (webapp/deploy/coffee-cv.nginx.conf.template). Another site on the proxy already uses the pattern this ticket wants: the stream map sends it to a TLS server on the proxy itself.
  • The tunnel. Tailscale between the proxy and yappy is relayed through a DERP server, not direct (tailscale ping). Measured with one raw TCP transfer each way: proxy→yappy 2.1 Mbit/s (21 MB in 81 s), yappy→proxy 8.5 Mbit/s (8.4 MB in 7.9 s). The owner rules the link as fast as it can be: yappy's ISP limits it (D4). A deploy's upload to yappy also goes over Tailscale, at about the same rate (14 MB a minute, deploy.md).
  • The proxy. Debian 12, 2 vCPU (AMD EPYC, AVX-512), 958 MB RAM with about 200 MB available and 440 MB of its 1 GB swap in use, 3.7 GB of disk free (81% used). It runs about 25 other services (databases, monitoring, VPNs, file sharing) and its nginx serves six other sites. No Docker, no uv; system Python 3.11.2. It holds the same wildcard certificate as yappy, valid to 2027-01-01. Its firewall is firewalld. SSH alias webproxy, as root.
  • The inference host. yappy: 4 cores, 7.7 GB, about 3.3 GB available beside the live worker. /analyze takes latency_ms 8.4–8.9 s on its GPU (OPS-7 P5).
  • Decoding. load_rgb_image decodes at full resolution with no early resize (coffeecv/photo_io.py): a 48 MP photo is about 144 MB of RGB, RAW goes through rawpy.
  • Photo sizes. In dataset/, every phone photo is under 5 MB (Pixel, OnePlus, iPhone HEIC); 180 of the 310 Sony camera photos are over. Of the deploy fixtures, only compare's (7.6 MB, Sony) is; smoke's is 1.8 MB (webapp/deploy/fixtures.txt).
  • Client IPs. docs/logging_plan.html §03-C excludes them "for now", and webapp/README.md says the logs hold none. The app does not read X-Real-IP today; behind the passthrough it would only be the proxy's tailnet address.

3. Before and after

Now

  • The proxy passes TLS through; yappy terminates it on a public port.
  • yappy serves the page; when it is down or mid-deploy, the page is down with it.
  • Every upload crosses the 2.1 Mbit/s link before anything checks it; up to 20 MB.
  • One host, one release, one flip.
  • No client IP in any log.

After

  • The front terminates TLS; yappy listens only on its tailnet address, plain HTTP.
  • The front serves the page, and answers "the classifier is offline" while yappy is unreachable.
  • The front refuses a busy request, a file over 5 MB or a file that is not an image before it crosses; the page refuses an oversized photo before uploading it.
  • One release across two hosts: staged on both, stopped, flipped and started on both, reverted on both.
  • Full client IPs in both hosts' logs, kept 30 days.

4. What does not change

  • The public contract. POST /analyze, its form fields and its NDJSON answer stay as openapi.json describes them, apart from the size cap (D15).
  • What the model sees. The front forwards the original bytes; decoding and every pixel the model sees stay on the inference host, in the code path training uses (D2).
  • Immutable releases and rollback (ADR 0008), now on two hosts.
  • One streamed answer, no state between requests (ADR 0023). The front is one more hop that must not buffer it.
  • The serving runtime and the models (ADR 0019): yappy's GPU, LiteRT, the warm boot.
  • The tunnel. Tailscale as it is configured (D4).
  • The DNS record. It already points at the proxy.

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1The front does not fit in the proxy's memory. About 200 MB available, swap already in use. The check service uses the standard library only (D7) and is measured on the proxy before it serves (plan P6); nginx's buffering of an upload is capped at 5 MB a request by D15 and the gate.
R2A deploy breaks the proxy's other sites. A reload of the proxy's nginx reloads all of them. The deploy touches only its own site file, release directory and unit, runs nginx -t before every reload, and never edits nginx.conf (D8).
R3Photos are slower to cross, not faster. The front holds an upload before forwarding it, where the passthrough relayed it as it arrived. The visitor's leg to the proxy is fast, so this adds little; the crossing, about 20 s for 5 MB, dominates and only fewer bytes help (ML-15). Accepted by the owner (D1, D4).
R4Camera photos are refused. 180 of 310 Sony photos in the dataset are over 5 MB, and most RAW files. Accepted by the owner until ML-15 (D15); the page says so before uploading.
R5A year of logs about visitors becomes personal data. Full IPs are kept 30 days only, in files of their own, apart from the 365-day logs (D13; plan Q1).
R6Half a deploy. One host flips and the other fails. Any failure after either is stopped reverts both (D14); a skew of seconds between the flips is harmless because the contract between them does not change.

6. Decisions

IDQuestionOptionsOwner decision
D1What is the split for? (a) The page stays up while yappy is down or mid-deploy. (b) TLS on the always-on box. (c) CPU off yappy. (d) Upload bandwidth to yappy. 2026-11-02: (a), (b) and (d) are the main goals, (c) minor. (d) is not met by this ticket: with the link fixed (D4) only fewer bytes help, which is ML-15.
D2Does the front change the photo's bytes? (a) Lossy re-encode. (b) Lossless re-encode. (c) Check only, forward the original bytes. 2026-11-02: (c). Decoding stays on yappy; re-encoding at the front is ML-15.
D3One release across two hosts, or two parts deployed apart? (a) One release ID, shipped to both hosts by one script, rolled back together. (b) Two parts with a stable contract, deployed apart. 2026-11-02: (a). The contract between them stays stable anyway. Takes in OPS-9's goal.
D4Which tunnel, and is it tuned? (a) The Tailscale link already configured, as it is. (b) Work on a direct Tailscale connection first. 2026-11-02: (a). Not to be tuned: it is as fast as yappy's ISP allows.
D5Does yappy stop facing the internet? (a) It listens only on its tailnet address, plain HTTP inside the tunnel, no public certificate. (b) It keeps a public TLS port. 2026-11-02: (a), plain HTTP or any protocol inside the tunnel.
D6What are the two parts called? "The front" and "the inference host". 2026-11-02: as proposed. The structure holds if either machine changes (D17).
D7What runs the upload check on the front? (a) nginx alone, no content check. (b) A standard-library Python service. (c) Lua in nginx. 2026-11-02: (b), with uv and Python 3.12 as everywhere else (ADR 0007), not the proxy's 3.11.
D8What may a deploy touch on the shared proxy? Its own site file, release directory and unit; or nginx.conf too. 2026-11-02: its own files only, nginx -t before every reload. The owner edits the stream map once, by hand.
D9Which user deploys to the proxy? (a) coffee-deploy with limited sudo and a release user, as on yappy. (b) root. 2026-11-02: (a). root stays the owner's alias, never the script's.
D10Does yappy keep nginx in front of gunicorn? (a) Yes, buffering the whole upload before gunicorn. (b) The front talks to gunicorn directly. 2026-11-02: (a). Over 2.1 Mbit/s, a sync worker reading the body itself would be held for the crossing.
D11Where is the busy gate? At the front, at yappy, or both. 2026-11-02: at the front, so a second upload never crosses; yappy keeps its own as a backstop. A queue with each visitor's place and wait is OPS-8.
D12What does the page say when yappy is unreachable? The front's own 503, kept apart from "busy". 2026-11-02: {"error": "the classifier is offline"}; the page shows it and stays up.
D13Client IPs in the logs? (a) None, as logging_plan.html §03-C has it. (b) Full addresses. (c) Truncated. 2026-11-02: (b), on both hosts, X-Real-IP still forwarded to yappy; for abuse handling; kept 30 days; the other logs keep 365 days and the daily counts carry no IP. Reverses §03-C.
D14How does a deploy switch two hosts? (a) Flip one, then the other. (b) Stage both, stop both, flip both, start both. 2026-11-02: (b). If either breaks, both revert. On the front "stop" is the check service only: the proxy's nginx serves its other sites, so it keeps running and serves the page with D12's 503 meanwhile.
D15The size cap. (a) 20 MB, as today. (b) Lower. 2026-11-02: 5 MB at the front until ML-15; yappy keeps 20 MB, since the limit is the link's. The page refuses a larger photo before uploading it, with its size and the limit.
D16The front's timeouts. Derived from the measured link. 2026-11-02: about 60 s to yappy (5 MB crosses in about 20 s, inference about 12 s), written beside the measurement. The page says "sending to the classifier" while the photo crosses.
D17Which tickets this changes. 2026-11-02: OPS-9 is closed, superseded by this ticket (D3). OPS-8 becomes a queue at the front. Analytics (OPS-3), if built, live at the front.
D18Where do the client IPs go, so that they are kept 30 days while the app log keeps 365? (plan Q1) (a) In nginx access logs only. (b) In each app line, scrubbed after 30 days. 2026-11-02: (a). The front logs $remote_addr, yappy logs $http_x_real_ip, each in a file of its own rotated at 30 days. The app log stays without an IP at 365 days, joined to them by request_id.
D19What serves the check service, with no dependencies allowed (D7)? (plan Q2) (a) The standard library's http.server. (b) A WSGI server, which D7 rules out. 2026-11-02: (a). ThreadingHTTPServer on loopback under systemd, behind the front's nginx. The gate admits one request at a time (D11), so it never needs more; OPS-8's queue revisits it.
D20How does the front learn the visitor's IP? (plan Q3) (a) A log of the proxy's stream block, per TLS session, for this domain only. (b) The PROXY protocol on the stream server, which every one of its eight targets must then accept. (c) Transparent proxying. (d) None. 2026-11-02: (a), after the proxy's configuration was read: one stream server on 443 routes by SNI to eight upstreams, five of them not nginx (Webmin, Home Assistant, the cameras, a Tailscale proxy, the Reality default), so (b) would break them. The visitor's IP, the session's time, length and bytes go to coffee-cv-front.sessions, rotated at 30 days, joined to the request logs by time. No request carries a visitor IP past the stream hop, so this replaces D18's per-request IP files on both hosts and D13's X-Real-IP to yappy; yappy's logs keep each request's detail.

7. Scope and acceptance

  • The domain's TLS terminates on the proxy, and yappy accepts nothing from outside the tailnet (its public port closed).
  • With coffee-cv-web stopped on yappy, the page loads and /analyze answers D12's 503.
  • A file over 5 MB is refused by the page before upload and by the front with a 413; a non-image is refused by the front with a 400; neither reaches yappy's log.
  • The smoke photo's answer through the domain matches the release's expected answer, streamed in two parts.
  • One deploy and one --rollback switch both hosts; a failure injected on either host reverts both (rehearsed locally).
  • The check service's memory on the proxy is measured and recorded.
  • Full client IPs appear only in files rotated at 30 days.
  • deploy.md, webapp/README.md, logging_plan.html, llms.txt, openapi.json and docs/inference_path.html say what is live.

Out of scope

  • Re-encoding or resizing photos at the front, and raising the 5 MB cap: ML-15.
  • A queue, batching or more workers: OPS-8.
  • Analytics: OPS-3.
  • Tuning the tunnel or getting Tailscale to connect directly (D4).
  • Docker on either host (.out-of-scope/containerised-serving.md).
