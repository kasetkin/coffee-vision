Ticket ML-3 · coffee-vision · dataset and class list

Country classes and the new photos: merge the farm regions of one country into one class, add Peru and Rwanda

The classifier's label changes from one coffee (a country plus a farm region, variety or grade, e.g. Brazil Cerrado or Kenya AA) to the country alone. Coffees from the same country become one class, so the two Brazil Cerrado coffees and the new Brazil Sul de Minas all train as Brazil. At the same time, the photos waiting in dataset_new_ignored/ join the dataset. They include two new countries, Peru and Rwanda, and a few photos of two new farm regions. classes.txt is rewritten to keep country and farm region apart from other information (D15). This is the data-merge ticket that ML-2 deferred (ML-2 D20). Terms follow GLOSSARY.md and D14. This ticket records the request, the data, the risks and the decisions. It has no implementation plan yet, and nothing is implemented.

Owner request, 2026-10-04. Expand the dataset and change the list of classes. Farm regions from one country are merged into one class, for example the two Brazils, Cerrado and Monte Cristo, become Brazil. The new class list is dataset_new_ignored/classes_short_map.txt; dataset_new_ignored/classes.txt lists the coffees behind it. The data in dataset_new_ignored/ moves into dataset/. A ticket only for now; planning and implementation come later.

1. Summary

Today each class is one coffee we photographed, named by its country and farm region, variety or grade (dataset/classes.txt, ten classes). Two countries have two coffees each (Brazil, Ethiopia), so the model must tell apart coffees from one country. The new list has ten countries. Fourteen coffees map onto them, four of them new.

The work splits into five parts. Each part is small, but they all meet in the head and the probe refit:

  1. Labels. Training, evaluation, the model card and the web page use the country. The farm region and the misc information stay in classes.txt as facts about each photo, never as labels (D1, D15).
  2. Data. 288 new photos (the set fixed on 2026-10-05, labels/ml3/new_photos.csv) from 7 capture folders move from dataset_new_ignored/ into dataset/, are DVC-tracked, and run through the segmenter crop (ML-2), then join the camera pools.
  3. Privacy. GPS and other private metadata are stripped from every photo in dataset/, new and old (D13).
  4. Models. The frozen DINOv3 head and the OOD probe are refitted on the new classes and data. The owner reviews the existing segmenter's masks on the new photos; a second fine-tune follows only if the owner decides the declines call for it (D8).
  5. Release. The web app serves country scores (D12).

The new photos look roasted. Most of today's data is green (§2, R1). The owner accepts that for now (D4).

3. Before and after

Now

  • Label = one coffee, 10 classes (dataset/classes.txt, Country,Name).
  • 938 photos, 9 sessions, 4 camera pools.
  • Live: allrigs_dino3b16_seg_s7, which shows ten scores such as "Brazil,Cerrado".
  • Confusions inside one country (Sidamo vs Kochere, Cerrado vs Monte Cristo) count as errors.

After

  • Label = country, 10 classes. Each class folder maps to its country through classes.txt; farm region and misc are facts about a photo, never labels (D1, D15).
  • 1226 photos (1,207 in the pools after the mask review), 16 sessions (random_date_raccoon included, D5), the same 4 camera pools (D6).
  • A refitted head and OOD probe on the segmenter pools. The site shows ten country scores (D12).
  • Confusions inside one country are no longer errors. Peru and Rwanda are new classes.

4. What does not change

  • Pipeline shape. Segmenter crop at both ends (ML-2), the frozen DINOv3 ViT-B/16 with cls_mean, a logistic-regression head with C fixed at 0.1 (ML-1 D3), no TTA.
  • Evaluation protocol (ML-1). Photos are pooled across capture folders and split 70/15/15 within each class, now the country (D7). Val patch macro-F1 selects, test is reported. Both are in-distribution, not a real-world estimate.
  • Shipped models keep working. Every model carries its own frozen .classes.txt (infer.classes_path_for). The live model and the ResNet fallback keep their ten old classes until a new release replaces them.
  • Class folder names. The raw class folders keep their names (D1). Their pixels do not change; their bytes do, through the metadata strip (D13, R8).

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1Roast becomes a shortcut to the class. If all of Peru and Rwanda are roasted and the old seven countries are green, roast colour alone separates them. From the sample (§2), 3 of 10 classes would be roasted only (Indonesia, Peru, Rwanda), and 7 green with a handful of roasted photos (Ethiopia, Brazil, Colombia, from random_date_raccoon). The in-distribution metric cannot see this: val and test inherit the same roast-to-class link. On the site, a roasted photo of Kenyan beans would be pushed towards Peru, Rwanda or Indonesia. Roast state is not recorded per photo and is not reported (D4), so this ticket does not measure the effect. The real fix is photos: the same coffees in the other roast state. Accepted for now by the owner (D4): one mixed class per country; more green captures or roast-split classes may follow later.
R2New numbers are not comparable with old ones. A country label removes the confusions inside one country by construction, so macro-F1 rises without the model learning anything new. Two new classes make it harder at the same time. Mitigation: the archived results of the live model, remapped to countries, are printed as an unpaired reference (D10). Remapping alone lifts its macro-F1 by about 0.01–0.02.
R3Class sizes and farm regions inside a class are uneven. Brazil has 194 photos and Ethiopia 172, against 90 for Kenya. Training draws 150 patches per class per camera pool (train_patches_per_class), so the patch count per country stays even. Within a country, however, each coffee gets a smaller share, and Brazil SulDeMinas (4 photos) and Colombia Antioquia (12, 8 after the mask review) are almost invisible. The split is by country (D7), so a small coffee lands in val or test only by chance.
R4The segmenter has not seen these photos. ft_s123 was fine-tuned on today's data: green, plus 126 roasted Java photos. The new photos are frame-filling, which the segmenter handles best (ML-2 P1: frame-filling masks match, tray photos fail). The hopper photo is the exception. The owner reviews the masks in the review tool, up to all 288 (D8); no judge calls. Declined masks show where the segmenter needs more training.
R5The OOD probe holdout is spent. The probe must be refitted, because the head and the data change. The holdout is recorded as spent: any probe change needs new holdout photos to be validated honestly. Owner decision (D11): refit, and use the spent holdout as a regression check only. New photos and a second refit come later, outside this ticket.
R6Data can't be backed up yet. The DVC remote has been down since 2026-09-24, and earlier pushes are still pending. The new raw photos, the re-hashed old ones (R8) and their crops would sit in the local cache and the VM cache only.
R7Still no generalization test. As ML-1 R2: val and test share bags and cameras with training. The new sessions are the same three cameras. random_date_raccoon (other dates, at least one other setting) would have been the nearest thing to a fresh-scoop check, but it joins the pooled split (D5), so nothing new measures this.
R8Stripping metadata changes every photo's file hash (D13). Pixels stay the same if the strip is lossless, but the bytes do not. Every session's .dvc pointer changes, and every crop/segcrop stage becomes stale and must run again. Its outputs should come out identical; that is the check that the strip touched no pixels. The segmenter masks are bit-identical only on the same machine and thread count (ML-2 P0), so they are rebuilt on the VM as before. Records that pin photos by sha256 must be re-keyed from old hash to new hash: the deploy fixtures (webapp/deploy/fixtures.txt), and ML-2's labels/ml2/photo_lists.yaml, verdicts.jsonl, defects/*.csv and base_points.yaml, and the DVC-tracked data/seg_labels/labels.csv, data/seg_labels/r{1,2,3}/index.csv and data/seg_masks/*/index.csv. The review decisions files key on path and mask sha256 and need no change (corrected 2026-10-05 from the plan and its review). seg_eval fails loudly on a missing verdict, so a missed re-key shows up. The OOD manifests reference photos by file name and are not affected. The old GPS-bearing copies remain in the DVC caches by owner decision (D13.3).

A live-model score on the new val/test would be inflated, because many of those photos were in its training split; that is why this reference uses the archived results instead. Both numbers are in-distribution near ceiling, so a gap of about 0.01 says little. ADR 0002.

D13.1What counts as private? Remove: GPS, device and lens serial numbers, maker notes, C2PA/JUMBF (its signing certificate carries a device-stable id), extended XMP (e.g. Google HDR+), embedded thumbnails, owner/artist fields. Keep: camera make and model, date and time, exposure settings, the orientation tag (the pipeline ignores it), the ICC colour profile. Accepted as listed, 2026-10-04. This is the 2026-09-30 ood_positives set plus owner fields. Camera model and date stay, because the dataset is organized by them and they are not location data.
D13.2Which folders? (a) Everything under dataset/: the 9 training sessions, the 7 new folders, and the non-training folders (2026-07-24__first_pictures with DNG and HEIF, 2026-08-06__box_pictures, classes_labels_only, ood_negatives, ood_positives_internet; ood_positives is already clean). (b) Training sessions and new folders only. (a), 2026-10-04: everything under dataset/. HEIC (iPhone) and DNG need a different tool than JPEG; DNG keeps the EXIF its raw decoding needs and loses only the private fields.
D13.3What happens to the old, GPS-bearing copies? The originals stay as objects in the DVC caches (here, on the VM, and on the remote once it is back), and older commits' .dvc pointers still name them. (a) Keep them: new commits are clean, history is not. (b) Purge them (dvc gc plus deletion on the remote): history is clean, but every past experiment loses its raw input and can no longer be reproduced from its commit. (a), 2026-10-04: keep them. New commits are clean; the DVC caches and older commits still hold the GPS-bearing originals, so every past experiment stays reproducible.
D14Terms (GLOSSARY.md). Was proposed: "origin class" and "lot". The owner replaced both. Owner, 2026-10-04. No "lot": the project has no division by bag or grade. "Region" is never used alone. Terms, written into GLOSSARY.md during implementation:
  • Country: the country a coffee was grown in, by name only (e.g. Ethiopia). Since ML-3 it is the class: what the model predicts and is scored on.
  • Farm region: the growing area inside a country, written Country, Region[, Subregion] (e.g. Ethiopia, Yirgacheffe, Kochere). It means the same as "origin". It may be unknown, leaving the country only. A fact about a photo, never a label. Avoid: region on its own, lot.
  • Misc: anything else known about a coffee, kept apart from country and farm region in classes.txt: grade (AA, Excelso), variety (PinkBourbon), species (Robusta), farm, brand or washing-station name (LaPastora, TataNahual, MonteCristo, Minca, Gisuma). Never a label.
  • Bean region (existing term, ML-2): always written as "the photo's bean region", never "region" on its own.
The 3-digit ids in classes.txt and in the folder names stay for now. They are not a term, and the project does not use them.
D15How does classes.txt describe each coffee? Today: id;Country,Name, where the name is a farm region, a variety, a species or a grade. New: country and farm region first, everything else after a second ;. Owner, 2026-10-04. Format id;Country[,Region[,Subregion]];misc, ASCII only. Farm regions were found by web research where the name allowed it (Tarrazu for La Pastora, Cerrado for Daterra Monte Cristo, Acatenango for Tata Nahual, Rusizi for the Gisuma washing station) and given by the owner for the rest.
