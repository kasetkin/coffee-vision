1. Summary

Stub. The deploy smokes a new release on the host, in production's own sandbox, while the live release keeps serving, and its preflight guards that with 5 GB of available memory. That held while the live worker ran on PyTorch. On the GPU a worker takes about 3.5 GB of the host's memory, so a second one no longer fits on webappremote. Either the deploy makes room, with a planned and announced outage, or the host does. Nothing is proposed yet.

2. Background

Measured 2026-10-18 during OPS-7's deploy, and from ML-7's record; to be checked when the ticket is written.

  • The preflight. MIN_MEM_GB (default 5) is checked against the host's MemAvailable before anything runs there (scripts/deploy_webapp.sh, preflight). The ML-6 deploy fell short once and was rerun at 4 GB (6842b08).
  • The smoke worker. The release runs as coffee-cv-smoke.service on port 8001, beside the live coffee-cv-web, capped by SMOKE_CPU and CAP_MEM (7G); it must prove the GPU in its boot record (ADR 0020), so it cannot move to the CPU. --compare also runs the release's models on the host; whether it meets the same limit is not checked.
  • The numbers. ML-7 P10: a GPU worker takes 3.5 GB of MemAvailable, its GPU buffers included (peak RSS 2.25 GB); the preflight's 5 GB held beside the PyTorch service (5.3 GB available). OPS-7's deploy: 3.6 GB available beside the live GPU worker (RSS 1.9 GB, about 2.1 GB shared memory), 6.6 GB with it stopped; the smoke worker booted in 17.7 s at a peak RSS of 1.8 GB. webappremote has 7.7 GB and 1 GB of swap.
  • What OPS-7 did. sudo systemctl stop coffee-cv-web at 02:35:01 UTC, then the deploy with DEPLOY_SKIP_CHECK=1 (its full check had passed on the same commit); the service stayed enabled, so the deploy restarted it after the flip. Photo answers were down until about 02:36:50. Had the deploy failed before the flip, the old release would have needed a start by hand.
  • Ideas raised, not assessed. A deploy option that stops the live service just before the smoke and starts it again on any failure before the flip, with the outage stated in deploy.md; the smoke worker beside the live one with a lower bar, swap and the risk of the kernel's OOM killer; a host with more memory.

Facts from ML-8's deploy (plan P8), measured on webappremote. MemAvailable 3.9 GB beside the live worker before the deploy and 3.6 GB after it (MemTotal 7.7 GB, swap 1 GB with 0.3 GB in use); 7.0 GB with the service stopped, so one GPU worker costs about 3.3 GB of MemAvailable (its cgroup's MemoryCurrent 3.24 GB: RSS 1.9 GB, of it 0.9 GB file-backed, and about 2.1 GB of shared memory for the GPU). A smoke worker beside the live one would leave about 0.3 GB, so lowering MIN_MEM_GB to 3 would let the preflight pass and the smoke run on swap or meet the OOM killer, whose likeliest victim is the larger live worker; 4 GB would still refuse at 3.6. The service was stopped by hand twice (photo answers down 06:09–06:21 and 07:07–07:13 UTC). New: the upload reuses a host file only at the same path, so a new model name re-sent all 780 MB (about 14 MB/s) though only head.npz differed; the first outage ended early by starting the service again and sending the files by hand with the script's rsync command, site up. An upload-only step, or reuse by content, would keep any outage to the smoke and the flip. Not researched, not written.
