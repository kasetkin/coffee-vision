1. Summary

Today the live segmenter is xl0_v7r_s123, fine-tuned on pass16's 602 training and validation positives (ML-16). The segmenter dataset has 888 now, and pass17 holds the owner's accepted label for every one. After this ticket, a segmenter fine-tuned on pass17 is compared with xl0_v7r_s123, and, if the owner adopts it, it re-crops the pool and ships with a refitted classifier. The parts: the recipe and the selection rule (D2, D3), the fine-tune, the comparison including ML-23's empty masks, and the release.

2. Background

  • pass17 (2026-11-17; python -m coffeecv.seg_review label --session pass17 --proposer xl0_v7r_s123 --redraw xl0_v7r_s123 --carry pass16): 888 positives accepted, 602 carried from pass16 and 286 new; of the new, 138 accepted at round 2, 131 at round 3, 15 at round 4, 2 at round 5, none at round 1: the owner corrected every proposed mask. 468 negatives keep their empty labels. By source: pool 646 (all 286 new among them), segmenter positives 234, internet positives 8 (seg_review status --session pass17).
  • The name: xl0_v7r_s123 was not in segment_beans.NAMED_SEGMENTERS, so the review page refused it by name; named on 2026-11-17 (42bdf63), as each adopted segmenter is.
  • The recipe today (ML-16, dvc.yaml): seg_ml16_finetune fine-tunes the pretrained XL0 on pass16 at seed 42 (xl0_v7_s42); seg_ml16_refine refines it at seeds 123 and 7 with --init models/seg/xl0_v7_s42.pt --lr 2e-5. ML-16 D8 chose seed 123 on validation scores, without a blind paired session on the test photos.
  • ML-23 (stub, 2026-11-08, superseded by this ticket): xl0_v7r_s123 left 11 photos without a mask in ML-16's re-crop against xl0_v6_s42's 5, six green photos of 2026-10-21__new_green among them, all declined from the pool (ML-16 D10); the page's Vietnam example lost its mask and left the page (ML-16 D12). seg_min_area_frac 0.028521 is XL0-v6's, kept (ML-16 D9).
  • ML-29: if its P11 adopts gain maps, the canonical photos of 1,687 gain-map photos change, by about 20 levels at the median (ML-29 P8); a segmenter trained before that reads today's (D1).
