Ticket ML-17 · coffee-vision · serving inputs

A photo's transparent pixels decode as the segmenter's fill grey, not as the colour hidden under them

The live site drops a photo's alpha channel when it decodes it. A cut-out photo then shows, in the preview and to the model, whatever the file keeps under its transparent pixels, which other viewers never show. The owner wants those pixels in the grey the segmenter already fills non-bean pixels with. Written 2026-11-03; not planned, not implemented.

1. Summary

load_rgb_image converts every non-RAW photo with Image.open(path).convert("RGB"), which discards the alpha channel. After this ticket it composites a photo with transparency over the segmenter's fill grey (ImageNet's mean, (124, 116, 104), ML-2 D3), so transparent pixels decode as that grey wherever the photo is read: the preview, the segmenter, the classifier and the training tools. The work is the change with its tests, the serving-path page, and a release.

2. Background

  • The decode. coffeecv/photo_io.py:load_rgb_image is the one decoder for serving (webapp/app.py:analyze, coffeecv/infer.py) and for the dataset tools (coffeecv/dataset.py re-exports it; seg_finetune.py, segcrop_session.py, seg_predict.py and others call it). Its non-RAW branch ends in .convert("RGB"), which keeps the colour under alpha 0 and drops the alpha. The preview is built from its output (webapp/app.py:_preview_part: "the pixels the model sees").
  • How it showed. The 9 top-level photos of dataset/2026-07-24__first_pictures are GIMP exports, 2209×2209 HEIF, 12-bit, with alpha 0 outside the lens circle. Under that alpha they keep the full photo: the jar and the tray. A viewer (Gwenview) shows the circle on white; the site shows the jar. On 2026-11-02 the photos were re-encoded as JPEG XL with cjxl, whose lossy default (--keep_invisible=0) replaced the hidden pixels with an encoder smear and black corners. On the site that copy got "No coffee beans found -- the whole photo is classified". Re-encoded with --keep_invisible=1 on 2026-11-03, the JPEG XL shows the jar on the site, as the HEIF does.
  • The fill grey. SegParams.fill_rgb = (124, 116, 104) (segment_beans.py), checked equal to ImageNet's mean rounded by tests/test_segment_beans.py:test_fill_is_imagenet_mean_rounded. mask_and_crop paints every non-bean pixel of the crop with it.
  • Transparency in the dataset (Pillow's has_transparency_data, 2026-11-03): 22 of 2,468 non-RAW photos. 18 are the folder above and its JXL/ copies, which no DVC stage reads (not in segcrop's list; coverage_report files it as pre-project). 4 are internet OOD negatives in the segmenter dataset. Two of those are opaque in fact (alpha 255 throughout). The other two, confusable_grain_012.png (34.8% transparent) and other_nuts_seeds_024.jpg (21.4%), both in the test split, are black under alpha 0.
  • The front. webapp/front/upload_check.py checks only a file's first bytes; it decodes nothing, so it is not affected. ML-15 (a stub) may add a re-encode at the front.

3. Before and after

Now

  • A transparent pixel decodes as whatever colour the file stores under it: a cut-away background, black, or an encoder's filler.
  • The preview shows it, the segmenter sees it, and with "Use full photo instead" the classifier samples it.
  • The segmenter's fill grey is a literal in SegParams.

After

  • A transparent pixel decodes as (124, 116, 104); a partly transparent one as decided in D2.
  • The preview shows the grey where a viewer shows transparency, and the model gets the same pixels.
  • One constant, photo_io.FILL_RGB, is both the decode's grey and SegParams.fill_rgb's default.

4. What does not change

  • Opaque photos. Nearly every photo, uploaded or in the dataset, has no transparency and decodes to the same array as before.
  • RAW files. rawpy's branch has no alpha.
  • The models. No retraining. No photo a classifier or the live segmenter trained on has transparent pixels (the two above are test-split negatives).
  • The front's upload check. It reads headers only.

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1A widened decode changes an opaque photo's pixels. The composite runs only when Pillow reports transparency data. A test holds an opaque photo's array equal to the old .convert("RGB") result (CODING_STANDARDS: a widened invariant is tested on its old cases too).
R2A re-evaluation of the segmenter's test split moves. Two test negatives decode grey instead of black where transparent. Recorded results stay as recorded (their stages are frozen); a new evaluation says that this ticket changed those two inputs.
R3The grey reads as beans, or as background, differently from the old colour. The segmenter already sees this grey around every crop it fills, never as a photo input. Check the 9 HEIF photos and their JXL copies through /analyze at full size, mask drawn, before the release.
R4A later re-encode at the front drops the alpha before the inference host sees it (ML-15). ML-15's ticket names this ticket: a re-encode keeps the alpha or composites over the same grey.

6. Decisions

IDQuestionOptionsOwner decision
D1What colour does a transparent pixel decode as? (a) The segmenter's fill grey, ImageNet's mean (b) White, as viewers show it (c) Keep the hidden colour, as now (a), 2026-11-03: "gray as when we apply segmenter mask".
D2How is a partly transparent pixel (a cut-out's anti-aliased rim) decoded? (a) Blended: colour × alpha + grey × (1 − alpha), as a viewer composites (b) Thresholded: grey below a cut-off, the colour above it Open. Recommended: (a).
D3Does the dataset tools' decode change with the serving one? (a) Yes, the one load_rgb_image changes (train/inference parity) (b) Serving only, behind a flag Open. Recommended: (a); the only training-side effect is R2.
D4When does it go live? (a) A release of its own after the merge (b) With the next release for another reason Open.

7. Scope and acceptance

  • load_rgb_image decodes a photo's transparent pixels as FILL_RGB and its partly transparent ones as D2 says; SegParams.fill_rgb defaults to the same constant.
  • Tests: transparency in HEIF, PNG (RGBA and a palette's transparent index) and JPEG XL decodes as the grey; an opaque photo decodes to the same array as before; the segmenter's fill equals FILL_RGB.
  • docs/inference_path.html says what step 1 does with alpha, in the flowchart's node and the card.
  • The 9 HEIF photos of 2026-07-24__first_pictures and their JXL copies, run through /analyze locally: grey outside the lens circle, mask drawn, opened at full size.
  • Released (docs/agents/deploy.md), and the same photos checked through the live domain.
  • ML-15's ticket names this ticket's constraint on a front re-encode (R4).

Out of scope

  • Using the alpha as a mask: excluding transparent pixels from the segmenter's region or from patch sampling, rather than painting them grey.
  • Showing transparency itself in the preview (a checkerboard, or the page's background).
  • Re-encoding the dataset's photos.
