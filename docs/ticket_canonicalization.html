Ticket ML-19 · coffee-vision · what both models read, in training and serving

Every photo both models read is a canonical photo: rotated, at most 50 MP, Rec.2020 JPEG XL q95, read as sRGB

The owner wants every photo turned into one form before either model sees it, in training and in serving: a lossy JPEG XL at quality 95 in Rec.2020, made on the front for a visitor's photo. Today each original is decoded as it comes, colour profile and orientation ignored, and photos cross the slow link as uploaded. This ticket comes before ML-16's retrain of both models, which then train on canonical photos. Let's think about it like image normalization process. So before retraining both models we should insert this conversion into preprocessing steps. I think we can store crops after segmentation using this format." In the grilling that followed: "the front memory is not a problem, we can use at least 500Mb of RAM"; "store JXL with Rec.2020 colorspace. Add tests so color conversion would be checked every commit and will not drift later."

1. Summary

Today load_rgb_image decodes each original as it is: colour profiles and EXIF orientation ignored, the main image only, at full resolution. The front forwards a visitor's bytes over a 2.1 Mbit/s link, and training saves its crops as JPEG q95, a lossy step serving never has.

After this ticket every photo is canonicalized (GLOSSARY.md) before either model reads it: the EXIF rotation applied, transparency over the segmenter's fill grey, at most 50 MP, colour-managed from its own profile into Rec.2020, its main image only, no metadata, encoded as lossy JPEG XL q95; the models read it decoded to 8-bit sRGB. A visitor's photo is canonicalized on the front, so fewer bytes cross the link; the inference host accepts canonical photos only. The dataset is canonicalized into a DVC store that everything reading photos for the models uses, and crops are stored as lossless JPEG XL. The segmenter's labels move to the canonical geometry in a new session, pass14, carried from pass13 without re-judging (ADR 0033).

The work splits into: the canonicalization function and its colour tests; the encoder and the front's measurements (D23–D25); the canonical store; pass14; the training stages reading it; the front and the host; the example photos; the baseline of today's models on canonical test photos. ML-15 and ML-17 are absorbed here; HDR gain maps are ML-20.

2. Background

Read from the code at 840bb87 and measured 2026-11-03; scripts and numbers in analysis/ml19/.

  • The decode. coffeecv/photo_io.py:load_rgb_image is the one decoder for training and serving: RAW through rawpy (8-bit sRGB out), everything else Image.open(path).convert("RGB"), which drops the ICC profile, the alpha channel (ML-17) and any second image, and ignores EXIF orientation.
  • Colour profiles in dataset/ (2,463 photos): Display P3 or DCI-P3 1,158 (all 148 HEICs among them), sRGB 487, Adobe RGB 7, other 14, none 797. Of the untagged, 657 say sRGB in EXIF ColorSpace, 1 says "Uncalibrated", 139 say nothing (112 JPEG, 20 PNG, 7 DNG). So 47% of the photos reach both models as P3 numbers read as sRGB.
  • Out of sRGB. In the 1,152 readable P3 photos 0.86% of pixels lie outside sRGB, 0.29% by more than 2%; 18 photos have more than 10% outside, mostly real-world negatives and label cards, the highest bean photo a Rwanda pool photo (58%).
  • Orientation. 662 photos carry a rotate tag (Rotate 90 CW 361, 180 281, 270 CW 20). Both models and every label mask use the stored, unrotated pixels.
  • Second images. 1,518 photos hold one (an HDR gain map, a depth map or a preview); the models read the main image only.
  • Over 50 MP. 7 photos (six 200 MP, one 84 MP, Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, 19–33 MB). Downscaled to 50 MP with Lanczos: 0.005–0.49% of the image's power lay above the new resolution limit; on the live models the same verdict and top-1 on all 7 (probability within 0.012), mask area the same to 3 decimals, bean pitch halved exactly with the scale; the slowest photo 34.3 s against 16.7 s.
  • Crops. segcrop_session.py saves each crop as JPEG q95 and its mask as a PNG; serving crops in memory and never encodes the crop, so training alone sees that lossy step.
  • The front. webapp/front/check_service.py is standard library only, under MemoryMax=100M; ADR 0031: "The front gets no ML code and no decoder", pending ML-15. The owner allows at least 500 MB (D1). The front's upload cap is 10 MB (OPS-16 D8); the link carries about 2.1 Mbit/s, so 10 MB takes about 40 s to cross.
  • The encoder. pillow-jxl-plugin, the JPEG XL encoder in use (scripts/make_example_photos.py), cannot set a colour encoding: it keeps the source profile or not (use_original_profile). A Rec.2020 JPEG XL needs another encoder (D25).
  • Labels. pass13 holds the owner's accepted masks for all 478 training and validation segmenter positives and the pool draw's, in the originals' stored pixels (ML-16).

3. Before and after

Now

  • Each original is decoded as it is: profile, orientation, alpha and second images ignored, at full resolution, up to 200 MP.
  • The front forwards a visitor's original bytes; 10 MB crosses the link in about 40 s.
  • Training crops are JPEG q95; serving's are never encoded.
  • Label masks line up with the unrotated originals.

After

  • Both models read canonical photos only, decoded to sRGB, in training and serving.
  • The front canonicalizes each upload and sends the canonical photo; the host refuses anything else.
  • Training crops are lossless JPEG XL of what serving computes.
  • pass14 holds pass13's masks in the canonical geometry; the labelling page shows canonical photos.
  • Tests on every commit hold the colour conversion fixed.

4. What does not change

  • The originals. dataset/ stays byte for byte, the private source of truth; every record keys a photo by its original's sha256 (ML-16 D2, ML-18).
  • The API. /analyze takes the same form from the visitor; the formats the front accepts stay those of today, RAW pending D24.
  • The models. Nothing is retrained here; ML-16 retrains both on canonical photos.
  • Earlier label sessions. pass1–pass13 stay as recorded; pass14 is new (D9).

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1Canonical photos change what today's models answer. Colour (47% of the dataset), rotation (662 photos), the cap (7) and a q95 generation all move the input. D14: today's models are re-run on canonical test photos as the paired baseline, so the retrain's comparison separates the input's change from the training's.
R2No encoder writes Rec.2020 JPEG XL where it must run. pillow-jxl-plugin cannot. The plan finds one that runs on the front, the inference host and the development machine with the same output, before anything else (D25).
R3The front runs out of memory or time. A 50 MP decode alone is 150 MB of RGB; RAW decodes cost more. Measured on the front's hardware against 500 MB and D22's 40 s before D23–D25 are decided.
R4The colour conversion drifts with a library upgrade. littlecms or libjxl may change their output. D8's tests run on every commit, against values computed independently and a committed golden file.
R5A rotated mask no longer fits its photo. Rotation is exact on a pixel grid, but the four EXIF rotations and the "Unknown (0)" tag (35 photos) each need a case. Tested per rotation; the owner looks at a sample of pass14's rotated masks on the page before the segmenter trains on them.
R6Rec.2020 in 8 bits bands. Lossy JPEG XL keeps its own precision; the decode runs at 16 bits or more and rounds to 8-bit sRGB only at the end (D7).
R7An example photo changes its answer. The examples become canonical photos (D17); OPS-16 P6 checks them on ML-16's models, and every deploy's --verify after.

6. Decisions

IDQuestionOptionsOwner decision
D1What is canonicalization for? (a) one consistent input for both models (b) fewer bytes over the link (c) both (c), 2026-11-03: "the front memory is not a problem, we can use at least 500Mb of RAM. So answer is both (a) and (b)." ADR 0033.
D2Which colour space do the models read? (a) sRGB, converted from each photo's profile (b) Rec.2020 (c) as today, profiles ignored (a), 2026-11-03: "for now let's use sRGB for models input."
D3Does training read what serving reads? (a) the dataset canonicalized once into a stored set (b) on the fly at each decode (c) only the crops (a), 2026-11-03. ADR 0033.
D4Full resolution, or a cap? (a) full resolution (b) a cap (b), 2026-11-03, after measuring: "maybe we can downscale them to 48-50 Mp without loosing image details". A photo over 50 MP is downscaled (Lanczos, aspect kept) to at most 50 MP; the measurement in §2 found no answer changed.
D5Orientation? (a) keep the stored pixels (b) apply the EXIF rotation (b), 2026-11-03: "Rotate image and stored masks now. And apply rotation from exif during live-time conversion at the front." Only canonical photos are rotated; the originals stay as they are. ADR 0033.
D6The step's name? "normalization" (taken: the input tensors' mean/std scaling) or another "Canonicalization", 2026-11-03; its result is the canonical photo. In GLOSSARY.md.
D7How is a canonical photo stored and read? (a) 8-bit sRGB (b) Rec.2020, read as sRGB (b), 2026-11-03: "store JXL with Rec.2020 colorspace". Lossy JPEG XL, quality 95; Rec.2020 primaries, D65, Rec.2020's own transfer curve (BT.709's). The decode runs at 16 bits or more, converts relative colorimetric with clipping, and rounds to 8-bit sRGB. ADR 0033.
D8How is the colour conversion held fixed? Tests on every commit 2026-11-03, the owner's request, in the fast tier: (i) synthetic patches of known colour (sRGB and P3 primaries, a P3 green outside sRGB, greys, white) through original → canonical → sRGB, against values computed in the test by independent colour maths; (ii) the canonical file's header declares D7's encoding and holds no orientation tag; (iii) a committed golden file of expected values.
D9The segmenter's labels in the canonical geometry? (a) a new session carrying pass13's masks rotated (b) rotate pass13 itself (c) re-judge (a), 2026-11-03: "re-use pass13 masks". pass14 carries pass13's accepted masks, rotated to each canonical photo, keyed by the original's sha256; no re-judging.
D10How are crops stored? (a) lossless JPEG XL (b) lossy JPEG XL q95 (c) JPEG q95, as today (a), 2026-11-03: the stored crop is exactly what serving computes in memory.
D11Which formats does the front accept? (a) today's, RAW included (b) without RAW 2026-11-03: measure first, then decide: D24. The front canonicalizes (ADR 0033, amending ADR 0031).
D12What does the inference host accept? (a) canonical photos only (b) anything, canonicalized there (a), 2026-11-03: one lossy generation per photo; local tools run the same canonicalization first. ADR 0033.
D13Transparency? (a) here: transparent pixels become the segmenter's fill grey (b) apart, in ML-17 (a), 2026-11-03. ML-17 is absorbed.
D14The retrain's baseline? (a) today's models on canonical test photos (b) today's numbers only (a), 2026-11-03: xl0_v6_s42 and dino3b16_segxl0v6_country_aug8_s42 re-run on canonical test photos as the paired baseline (ADR 0002); today's numbers stay the record of what is live.
D15Which ticket? (a) a new one (b) in ML-16 (a), 2026-11-03: this one. It absorbs ML-15 and ML-17, blocks ML-16's retrain, and ships in ML-16's release with OPS-16.
D16A photo with no colour profile? (a) taken as sRGB (b) refused (a), 2026-11-03; the one "Uncalibrated" photo is checked by hand and read as Adobe RGB if its EXIF interoperability index says R03.
D17An upload that is already canonical? (a) passed through unchanged (b) re-encoded (a), 2026-11-03. The example photos become canonical photos, made from their originals, and OPS-16 P6 checks them again.
D18The canonical store? A DVC stage per folder, read by everything that feeds the models 2026-11-03: canonicalize@<folder> over every folder a model reads (the pool sessions, segmenter_positives, the OOD negatives, the internet positives); segcrop, the segmenter's fine-tune and prediction and the OOD tools read canonical photos only.
D19What does the labelling page show? Canonical photos from pass14 on Yes, 2026-11-03: the masks the owner judges sit on the pixels the segmenter trains on.
D20HDR gain maps and other second images? (a) the main image only (b) the gain map applied, then tone-mapped (a) for today, 2026-11-03; applying gain maps is ML-20.
D21Metadata in a canonical photo? None 2026-11-03: none but the colour encoding; the front never forwards or stores the original upload (ADR 0013).
D22The front's time budget? (a) 10 s for 50 MP (b) none 2026-11-03: "for 10 MB photos time limit will be 40 seconds", on the front, for canonicalization.
D23The new upload cap, now that the cap no longer guards the link? From the front's measured memory and time Open: measured in the plan.
D24Does the front take RAW within 500 MB? (a) yes (b) no Open: measured in the plan (D11).
D25Which encoder, at which effort? One that writes D7's encoding on all three machines with the same output; the highest effort within D22 Open: measured in the plan. The effort holds for the canonical store too.

7. Scope and acceptance

  • ADR 0033 written; the glossary's "Canonicalization".
  • An encoder chosen, and the front measured: D23–D25 decided.
  • The canonicalization function (D4, D5, D7, D13, D16, D20, D21) and its tests (D8) in the fast tier.
  • The canonical store: canonicalize@<folder> for every folder a model reads, in DVC, pushed and checked on the remote (D18).
  • pass14: pass13's masks in the canonical geometry, a sample looked at by the owner; the labelling page on canonical photos (D9, D19).
  • segcrop on canonical photos, crops as lossless JPEG XL (D10); the segmenter's fine-tune and prediction and the OOD tools on canonical photos.
  • The front canonicalizes; the host accepts canonical photos only; the example photos are canonical photos (D12, D17); docs/inference_path.html and ADR 0031 say so.
  • Today's models on canonical test photos, paired against today's numbers (D14).
  • Live with ML-16's release, verified on the domain.

Out of scope

  • Retraining either model (ML-16).
  • Wide-gamut input to the models (D2: sRGB for now).
  • HDR gain maps (ML-20).
  • Keying records by hash (ML-18).
