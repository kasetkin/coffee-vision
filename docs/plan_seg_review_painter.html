Implementation plan: seg review painter

The order of work for ticket ML-11. Two strands: the server records strokes and paints them into the mask (P1), then the page gets the brush, its cursor, the radius field and a live preview (P2). The server comes first because it is what a label depends on, and its tests pin the geometry the page then imitates. The owner tries the brush in P3. No phase changes a model, a crop or an existing label.

Built from a read of the code at 014d1c1, not from the ticket alone. Four things the ticket does not cover turned up. They are folded in below, and the ones that need a decision are Q1–Q3 (§2).

  1. Finished sessions are DVC-tracked directories. data/ml5_labels/pass3–pass7 each have a .dvc file, and the fine-tune stages in dvc.yaml depend on them. A new strokes column must not rewrite them: readers take a missing column as no strokes, and no old session's labels.csv or index.csv is rewritten by this work. P1.
  2. The page has no mask, only a JPEG with the outline drawn in (view_image), so the outline cannot follow the brush. Q1, answered as a tint over the painted area (ticket D7), which needs no mask on the page.
  3. Undoing a carried stroke cannot be previewed. With D3, the shown mask already has the earlier rounds' strokes painted in; the pixels under an erased stroke are the model's, which the page does not have. Q2.
  4. A rechecking session already carries strokes. recheck_in copies the earlier round's index row whole into round 1, so a painted label being rechecked keeps its strokes, as it keeps its points and fence, once the row has the column. Nothing to change; a test pins it.

1. Phase order and why

P1 is server-side and fully testable without a browser: the stroke format, the painting, its records and its refusals. P2 builds the page on P1's routes and is checked by hand on a toy session. P3 is the owner using it.

3. P1 — Strokes on the server

StatusDone 2026-10-27: ac9f831; 11 new tests in tests/test_seg_review.py and tests/test_seg_point_probe.py; scripts/check.sh green; dvc status up to date, no session file changed. A 300-point stroke on a 50 MP mask paints in 0.07 s at r = 100 photo pixels and 3–4 s at r = 500, the page's largest (100 screen pixels on a 1600 px view of an 8000 px photo)
Blocked byNone

A stroke is {"mode": "erase" | "fill", "r": <photo pixels>, "path": [[x, y], ...]}, the path in fractions of the photo, mapped to pixels as points and the fence are, f * (side - 1). Painting sets (fill) or clears (erase) every pixel whose centre lies within r of the path, a single point being a disc. Strokes apply in order, after the model and the fence (ticket D3, D4).

  1. paint_mask(mask, strokes) beside fence_mask, each segment rasterised in its bounding box only, so a long stroke on a full-size photo stays quick.
  2. _strokes(body) validates the request as _fence does: a list of strokes, each mode one of the two, r positive and at most the photo's long side, a path of one or more [x, y] fractions; 400 otherwise.
  3. LabelSession.redraw takes strokes: a redraw needs a point, a fence or a stroke; "nothing new" compares strokes too; the mask is drawn, fenced, then painted. strokes(item, k) reads a row's, as fence does.
  4. A strokes column in INDEX_FIELDS and LABEL_FIELDS, in the verdict rows and in accepted_labels; a row without it reads as no strokes (finding 1).
  5. The page's info gains strokes and the photo's width and height (from the index row).
  6. seg_point_probe._ml5_session skips a painted label, as a fenced one (ticket R3).
  7. Tests in tests/test_seg_review.py: erase and fill paint exactly the pixels within r of the path, after the fence; a strokes-only redraw is accepted and calls the model with the points; the same strokes again are refused; strokes carry into the next round's row and the verdicts and labels.csv; an index row without the column reads as none; a recheck keeps a label's strokes; bad strokes are refused; the probe skips a painted label.
  8. Checks: scripts/check.sh green; no file under data/ml5_labels/pass3–pass7 changed.

4. P2 — The brush on the page

StatusDone 2026-10-27: aaa0578; scripts/check.sh green. No browser in the agent's container, so the hand check in a browser is not done: the page's script ran in node against a stub DOM instead (21 checks: the radius's conversion to photo pixels, path thinning, the field's keys, clamping and storage, undo with the D9 message, the strokes Enter sends, no painting before a decline). What only a browser shows (the tint, the cursor, the edge after Enter, a window resize) moves to P3
Blocked byP1

In LABEL_PAGE: a canvas over the photo shows the strokes not yet sent, each a round-capped line of the brush's width, see-through light green for w and light red for e, over the server's view and its outline (ticket D7). Holding e or w and dragging with the left button draws on it. On release the stroke joins the list, converted to the record's units at the view's scale at that moment: the path to fractions, the radius to r_screen × width / shown width photo pixels (ticket D1).

  1. e and w are hold keys beside g, r and b (D5); painting needs canPoint(), as points do (D4); a click while e or w is held does not open the zoom tile.
  2. The cursor, while e or w is held over the photo: the native one hidden, a circle of the radius in screen pixels, a thin border with a see-through inside, red for erase and green for fill as the points are; at radius 1 still drawn a few pixels wide.
  3. A number field on the right panel, 1–100 screen pixels, clamped, starting at 5 and kept in localStorage (Q3, ticket D8); keys typed in it never reach the page's handler (ticket R2), and Enter or Esc there returns focus to the photo.
  4. Path points kept only where the mouse moved a screen pixel or more, rounded to four decimals as points are (ticket R5).
  5. U and Backspace undo the last stroke in the shared history; a stroke drawn on this page is taken off the canvas, a carried one with the message "undone; Enter redraws to show it" (Q2, ticket D9). Enter sends strokes with the points and fence; the message counts strokes too.
  6. The key list on the page and the seg_review.py and seg_review_pages.py docstrings describe the brush; ADR 0015 gets its amendment (ticket D6): a label is the model's mask, fenced, then painted by the owner; a painted label is no reference for the point probe.
  7. Checks: on a toy session (as the tests build), by hand in a browser: erase and fill tints, Enter changes the mask where the tint was, to within a pixel of its edge, resizing the window keeps the recorded radius in photo pixels, a digit typed in the field declines nothing; scripts/check.sh green.
