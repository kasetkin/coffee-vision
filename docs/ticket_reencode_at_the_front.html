1. Summary

Stub. The question is how the front can send fewer bytes without changing the answers beyond what the owner accepts, and what cap that allows. Nothing is proposed yet.

2. Background

Read at d9212c6 and measured 2026-11-02; to be checked when the ticket is written.

  • The link. 2.1 Mbit/s proxy→yappy, 8.5 Mbit/s back, over a Tailscale DERP relay; the owner rules it fixed (OPS-15 D4). 5 MB crosses in about 20 s; /analyze's inference takes about 9 s.
  • The front's budget. About 200 MB of the proxy's memory available, 2 vCPU with AVX-512, no GPU (OPS-15 §2).
  • What is refused today. 180 of 310 Sony photos in dataset/ are over 5 MB, and most RAW files; no phone photo is (OPS-15 D15).
  • Parity. The model is measured on pixels decoded from the original file (load_rgb_image, at full resolution); a converted model is held to D7's bars on the serving host (ADR 0020). Anything lossy at the front is a departure from the parity rule in CODING_STANDARDS.md and needs the same kind of measurement. ADR 0003 bounds how far a photo can be downscaled.

3. Questions to research

  • Exact transcoding: JPEG to JPEG XL lossless (cjxl), rebuilt byte for byte on the inference host. The bytes saved on the dataset's JPEGs, and its memory and time on the front.
  • Lossy re-encoding and downscaling: bytes saved against answers changed on the 13 fixtures and a wider set, under D7-style bars; the smallest long side ADR 0003 allows.
  • What HEIC, AVIF and RAW gain, given that decoding them on the front costs about 150 MB or more.
  • The cap each option allows, and what it lets through that 5 MB refuses.
