1. Summary

Today a fine-tuned segmenter sees one box in training, the whole photo, as serving sends it, and learns to disregard it. After this ticket a fine-tune also trains on smaller boxes, and the review page passes the owner's rectangle to the model as a prompt, so a redraw can find beans inside it rather than only drop what lies outside.

2. Background

  • The measurement (analysis/ml9/box_prompt_probe.txt, 10 pool photos the owner accepted in pass3): given a box over the left half of the beans, XL0-v3 put a median 50% of its mask outside the box, and matched the left half of the owner's mask at IoU 0.50; pretrained XL0 put 0.5% outside, IoU 0.91. Both match the owner's mask from the whole-photo box (median IoU 0.995 and 0.994).
  • The same thing happened to points: ML-2's box-only fine-tune stopped following points, and ML-5 D15 fixed it by sampling points from the error region in some batches (ADR 0015). Boxes would get the same treatment.
  • Serving keeps the whole-photo box (ADR 0015). Box batches must not cost the whole-photo output its accuracy, which is what crops every photo.

3. Before and after

Now

  • The rectangle is a fence: it cuts the redrawn mask (ML-9 D7).
  • A fine-tune trains on the whole-photo box and sampled points.

After

  • Some fine-tune batches carry a smaller box around part of the label.
  • The rectangle is sent to the model as a prompt, and the redraw follows it.

4. What does not change

  • Serving's prompt, the whole-photo box, and checkpoint selection on it (ADR 0015).
  • The owner judges every label (ADR 0015).
