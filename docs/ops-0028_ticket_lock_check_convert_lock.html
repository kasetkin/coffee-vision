Ticket OPS-28 · coffee-vision · environments and locks

coffeecv.lock_check holds convert/uv.lock to the photo stack's versions

lock_check keeps the training, webapp and front locks at one version of every package they share, but does not read convert/uv.lock, the LiteRT conversion env's. Twice a pin the photo path enforces at import time broke the conversion only when a model was exported (ML-27: imagecodecs; ML-37 P8: numpy), and today it holds Pillow and pillow-heif at versions the other three moved away from. Written, not planned.

1. Summary

The conversion env imports the photo path: coffeecv.export_litert builds the models through coffeecv.infer, which imports photo_io, canonical and, since ML-37, readback. canonical refuses any imagecodecs but its own, and readback any numpy but its own, at import. lock_check compares only three locks (LOCKS in coffeecv/lock_check.py), so a pin moved in those three, or a pin added to the code, is caught in the conversion env only by the next export. After: lock_check reads the conversion lock as well, for the packages it must share with the others (D1), and the conversion lock is brought to them.

2. Background

  • ML-27 (3576b92): imagecodecs, which canonical pins, was missing from convert/pyproject.toml; declared at the pinned version.
  • ML-37 P8 (3861fbd): litert_export stopped on readback's numpy pin, 2.5.3 against the conversion lock's 2.5.4; numpy pinned. The live model's export rerun on the new lock gave byte-identical graphs and head.
  • The conversion lock today, against the other three (2026-11-28): of the 41 packages it shares with the training lock, 8 differ. Deliberately: torch 2.13 against 2.14 and torchvision (litert-torch needs torch below 2.14, convert/pyproject.toml's header) and ai-edge-litert 2.2.0 against 2.3.0 (litert-torch brings its own). Not deliberately: pillow 12.3.0 against 13.0.0 in all three, which OPS-26 moved for 43 advisories and did not carry here; pillow-heif 1.8.1 against 1.8.0; and typing-extensions, markupsafe, filelock.
  • The D7 check (export_litert finish) runs in the training env, not the conversion env, so the fixtures it reads are decoded by training's libraries; whether the conversion step decodes any photo, and so whether Pillow's version there reaches a graph, is not known.

3. Before and after

Now

  • lock_check: three locks.
  • The conversion env: Pillow 12.3.0, pillow-heif 1.8.1; found broken only at an export.

After

  • lock_check reads convert/uv.lock too, under D1's rule; the pre-commit hook and scripts/check.sh refuse a mismatch.
  • The conversion lock at the others' versions where D1 says so; one export rerun to show the graphs unchanged, as ML-37 P8 did.

4. What does not change

  • The conversion env's own torch and ai-edge-litert. litert-torch needs them (ML-7 D6).
  • The three locks' rule. They stay at one version of everything they share (ML-19 D40, D41).

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1Pillow 13 in the conversion env changes a graph. Unknown (§2). Mitigation: rerun the live model's litert_export after the move and compare its files with the committed ones byte for byte; a changed graph stops for the owner.
R2litert-torch refuses a package version the others hold. Its resolver would say so at uv lock; such a package joins D1's exceptions with its reason.

6. Decisions

IDQuestionOptionsOwner decision
D1Which shared packages must the conversion lock hold at the others' versions? (a) every shared package but a named list of exceptions, each with its reason (torch, torchvision, ai-edge-litert) (b) only a named list: the photo stack (numpy, imagecodecs, pillow, pillow-heif, rawpy and the Pillow plugins) Open. Recommended (a): a package the photo path starts to import is covered without anyone adding it.

7. Scope and acceptance

  • lock_check reads convert/uv.lock under D1's rule, with a test that a mismatch in it fails.
  • convert/pyproject.toml and its lock at the others' versions; convert/.venv synced.
  • The live model's litert_export rerun: graphs and head byte-identical, or the owner told; the other export stages committed on the new lock.

Out of scope

  • Moving the conversion env to training's torch (blocked on litert-torch).
  • Upgrading any package beyond the versions the other three locks hold.
