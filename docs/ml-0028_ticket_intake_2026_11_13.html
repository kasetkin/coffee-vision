2. Background

  • CR3 is ready end to end. It is a row of coffeecv/data/accepted_formats.csv (rawpy), so it is in canonical.RAW_EXTENSIONS and in strip_metadata.PHOTO_EXTENSIONS. canonical.recognise and canonical.decode read two of the CR3s here (under 1 s each). No CR3 has been stripped before: labels/ml3/strip_manifest.csv lists none.
  • What the CR3s carry. Body serial, internal serial and lens serial; no GPS (the 90D has no GPS unit). The strip's -*serialnumber= and its audit's SerialNumber deny rule cover all three.
  • The JXLs are the CR3s' twins. Each of the 19 has a CR3 of the same stem. Each is a bare JPEG XL codestream (no container, so no Exif, XMP or JUMBF box: no metadata at all), 6984×4660, the 90D's full raw area against the CR3's 6960×4640 default crop: a development of the raw. Class 013's are landscape and about 20 MB, class 014's portrait and about 3.7 MB: two export settings.
  • JPEG XL is accepted but not intake's. .jxl has a row (imagecodecs.jpegxl) and decodes, but strip_metadata.PHOTO_EXTENSIONS does not name it, and find_duplicates and seg_dataset read that list: the duplicate check here skipped all 19, and the segmenter dataset would leave them out. ADR 0034 says intake's list is narrower on purpose; GIF joined it by ML-25 D6.
  • Two files of one stem share a crop name. segcrop writes <stem>__cropped.jxl, so of X.CR3 and X.jxl, or X.jpg and X.dng, the second crop overwrites the first (ML-25, Background "Twins", and R2: no guard was decided).
  • GPS. All 34 Pixel 9 Pro and OnePlus files carry GPS; the 13 G8441 files and the JXLs carry none. The strip removes it, as for every batch.
  • The duplicate check (find_duplicates, threshold 0.97, the 82 bean files against their three class folders):

The trial strip of a copy of 20261113_172701_Canon_EOS_90D_Brazil.CR3 failed and left the copy untouched: exiftool 13.25 says "Can't delete MakerNotes from CR3", and about 330 Canon maker-note tags survive, ML-3 D13's deny list failing every one. Of those tags, the identifying ones are InternalSerialNumber, ImageUniqueID, OwnerName (empty here) and TimeZoneCity with TimeZone, a region set in the camera's menu. 5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1The first CR3 strip fails or changes pixels. No CR3 has been through strip_metadata; exiftool writes CR3, and the strip's own audit compares decoded pixels before and after. Run it on one copy first; a failure leaves the originals untouched.
R2A kept twin's crop overwrites its sibling's. Section 2: 21 pairs (19 CR3/JXL, 2 JPG/DNG), all kept (D1, D2). D5 renames one of each; no shared stem, checked before segcrop runs.
R3One shot twice in the pool, across splits. Accepted by the owner in D1–D3 (2026-11-13): the twins differ in colour, the bursts are distinct exposures. The segmenter dataset groups shots 3 s apart on one clock (ADR 0029). The first build split 8 of the 19 CR3/JXL pairs: a CR3's EXIF time has a UTC offset, its JXL has no EXIF and is timed by its name, and two clocks are never compared. Each of the 19 pairs is now one unit in labels/ml5/split_units.yaml (ML-6 D11's rule for one scene on two clocks); rebuilt, every pair, the JPG/DNG ones included, is in one split. The classifier's own split is not checked here.
R5A 12-bit JPEG XL is canonicalized too dark. Found 2026-11-13: 17 of the 19 JXLs D18 fallbacks with an empty mask, their CR3 twins cropped. imagecodecs gives a 12-bit JPEG XL's samples as 0–4095 in a uint16, and canonical._decode_jxl took the uint16's 65535 as white. Fixed, test first: white is the header's bit depth. The fix changes canonical.py, a dependency of every canonicalize and segcrop stage, so all are re-run, as ML-25 did after OPS-22; outputs other than the 19 JXLs' must come back byte-identical. The live front reads JPEG XL uploads the same way: Production impact above.
R4A new crop the owner would decline trains the classifier. The owner's crop review, as in ML-25; a declined crop goes to labels/ml3/pool_exclude.csv.
