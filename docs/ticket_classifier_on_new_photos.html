Owner request, 2026-11-11. “Create new branch and ticket for training new classifier model with new data after ML-25 and ML-26. Keep segmenter model as it is now in the release.”

1. Summary

Today the site serves dino3b16_segxl0v7r_country_aug8_s42 (exp280, ML-16), fitted on a pool of 1,493 crops. Since then ML-25 brought 188 accepted crops: about 102 Ethiopia, 42 Colombia, 39 Brazil, 4 Costa Rica and 1 Peru. After this ticket a classifier fitted on the 1,681-crop pool is trained at seeds 42, 123 and 7, compared paired with exp280–282, and, if the owner picks it, shipped with a refitted OOD probe and its LiteRT conversion, then released. The segmenter stays xl0_v7r_s123, so there is no re-crop.

The work splits into: a check that the pool and the stages are current; the recorded run; the paired comparison and the owner’s pick; the ship, probe refit and conversion; the release.

2. Background

  • The segmenter. params.yaml seg_decoder is models/seg/xl0_v7r_s123.pt, the release’s (ML-16, live 2026-11-08). Every crop in data/segcropped/pool, ML-25’s included, was made with it. Keeping it means no step of docs/agents/experiments.md’s “Changing the segmenter”.
  • The new crops. ML-25’s three batches: 146, 43 and 17 photos. Of the bean photos’ crops, the owner accepted 188 and declined 4 (labels/ml3/pool_exclude.csv, 35 rows). merge_segpool reports 1,681 crops.
  • No new label. Classes 016 (Ethiopia), 017 (Peru) and 018 (Costa Rica) are new coffees in existing countries. The class is the country (ADR 0012), so the head keeps exp280’s 10 classes.
  • The recipe. exp280–282 used fit_frozen_head with frozen DINOv3 ViT-B/16 and head augmentation aug8 (ADR 0006, 0025, 0027). Each seed took about 3 h 10 min (ML-16, 2026-11-07). exp282 is the last experiment, so this run starts at exp283.
  • Splits move. The photo split is seeded on the sorted (folder, name) list (ML-1 D1(b)). New photos in Ethiopia, Colombia, Brazil, Costa Rica and Peru redraw those classes’ splits, so test sets differ from exp280–282’s, as in ML-16 D11. analysis/ml16/common_photos.py scored the photos in both arms then.
  • The probe. ML-16 D13 shipped exp280’s probe without a refit and left the refit “at the next refit”: this one.
  • The live input. Since OPS-22 the front canonicalizes visitors’ photos with cheaper settings than the store, which training reads (ADR 0036). The ADR keeps training on the store’s photos.

4. What does not change

  • The segmenter and the crops. xl0_v7r_s123 and seg_min_area_frac stay as the owner asked, and params.yaml’s segmenter keys are not touched.
  • The class list. 10 countries (ADR 0012).
  • The canonical store and the front. Training reads the store’s canonical photos, and the front keeps OPS-22’s settings (ADR 0036).
  • The models in models/. They are kept, with exp280 as the rollback.

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1The paired test deltas mix the model with the redrawn test sets. ML-16 D11: exp280–282 tested −0.0080 below their baseline, on test sets that were not the same photos. Mitigation: report both the full test sets and the photos common to both arms (analysis/ml16/common_photos.py), with per-class F1 (D2).
R2The new data is lopsided toward Ethiopia. About 102 of the 188 crops are Ethiopia, and most are Sidamo from one session. Per-class patch caps (600 train) bound its weight in the head. Per-class F1 shows any shift in other classes.
R3A stale stage trains on an old pool. The driver refuses stale stages and a dirty tree (experiments.md). Check dvc status for the segcrop and merge_segpool stages first, and diff the run’s config against exp280’s key by key.
R4The live input differs from training’s. ADR 0036: the front’s canonical photo has less fine detail. This ticket does not change that; the release’s --compare and --verify score the front’s path on the examples.
