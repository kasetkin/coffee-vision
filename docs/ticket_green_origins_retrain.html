1. Summary

Today the live models are the XL0-v3 segmenter (models/seg/xl0_v3.pt, ticket ML-6) and the classifier dino3b16_segxl0v3_country_aug8_s123 (exp271, ticket ML-8). Neither has seen a photo of 2026-10-21. After this ticket both are retrained on every new photo (D1), compared with the live ones, and, on the owner's go, deployed.

The parts: the new photos into the segmenter dataset (D2) and a label pass the owner judges (ADR 0015); a new XL0 decoder fine-tuned (D3) and compared with XL0-v3 (D4); every session re-cropped if it is adopted, its new fallbacks judged; the classifier at seeds 42, 123 and 7 with its OOD probe, compared as D5 says; the LiteRT export and the deploy (D6).

2. Background

  • What arrived on 2026-10-21 (all stripped of private metadata, ADR 0013, and in DVC): dataset/2026-10-21__new_green, 41 Pixel 9 Pro photos of green beans: 21 of a new coffee, 015;Indonesia; (region unknown, not 010's Java), and 20 of Peru Minca (class 011, the roasted Peru's coffee); 7 roasted Colombia Excelso photos in random_date_raccoon (class 014); 3 segmenter positives; 1 negative in ood_negatives/user_realworld; 2 label cards in classes_labels_only.
  • The crops. Both new sessions were cropped by XL0-v3; the 68 older random_date_raccoon crops came back byte-identical. The owner accepted all 48 new crops (D1), so the pool holds 1,299 photos from 17 sessions. One, IMG_7317.HEIC (Excelso), is a D18 fallback: the segmenter found no beans, so the whole photo trains, cups and hopper included (R3).
  • The segmenter dataset holds 923 photos since the ingest: 149 segmenter positives, 149 pool, 613 negatives, 12 internet positives. Its pool side is drawn to match the positives in number (ML-5 D12), so of the 48 new pool photos it holds none; the draw took 3 older pool photos (D2). Shots are grouped within 3 s, not 120 s, since the ingest (ADR 0029).
  • Labels are the owner's: pass3 (ML-6) holds the accepted masks. A new session started with seg_review label --carry pass3 shows only the photos new since.
  • The fine-tune recipe moved on. seg_ml6_finetune is frozen with XL0-v3's own augmentation; params.yaml's seg_ft.augment now takes the groups shared with the classifier (ADR 0027), which replace XL0-v3's brightness and contrast with the colour group (D3).
  • Roast state (status.md): Indonesia now has 126 roasted and 21 green pool photos, Peru 168 and 20. Rwanda stays roasted only, Kenya and Costa Rica green only.

3. Before and after

Now

  • The live classifier has seen Indonesia and Peru roasted only.
  • XL0-v3 was fine-tuned without the 2026-10-21 photos.
  • The segmenter dataset holds 7 photos of the ingest (3 positives, 1 negative, 3 older pool photos the draw took), with no labels yet.

After

  • The segmenter dataset holds the new photos as D2 decides, and each training and validation one has an owner-judged label.
  • A new segmenter (or XL0-v3, if the owner keeps it, D4) crops every session.
  • A classifier trained on every new photo, with its refitted probe, reported per class and on the green photos of 2026-10-21 alone, converted and live on the owner's go (D6).

4. What does not change

  • The country is the label (ADR 0012): class 015 trains as Indonesia, beside 010; green and roasted beans of a country stay one class (ML-3 D4).
  • The classifier's recipe: frozen DINOv3 ViT-B/16 with a linear head at C = 0.1 (ADR 0006), 8 augmented views per patch (ADR 0025). New data is the change.
  • The owner judges every segmenter label (ADR 0015) and reads every comparison (ADR 0002); nothing passes on a numeric gate.
  • A converted model serves only within ML-7 D7's bars (ADR 0020).
  • The label cards in classes_labels_only are a record of which coffee is which, not training data.

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1Green Indonesia and Peru come from one session: one phone, one day, one light. Their patches in docs/patch_samples.png are warmer and browner than the older green ones (mostly iPhone). A model can learn the session for those two countries, not the beans, and the split, by single photo within the pool, cannot see it: the test photos share the session. Mitigation: report that session's test photos on their own (roast state is not recorded per photo, ML-3 D4, but every photo of the session is green); a held-out session of green beans would measure it (ML-4 D3, never decided).
R2New photos re-draw the pooled split, so no run is paired with exp270–272. The test split changes with the pool (ML-4 §4). D5 asks how the candidate is compared: the live model can be scored on the new test split, as a baseline on the same photos.
R3IMG_7317.HEIC trains as a whole photo. A D18 fallback: its patches are cups, the hopper and the counter, labelled Colombia. The owner accepted it (D1). A new segmenter re-crops it; if that finds the beans, the fallback is gone. Reported either way.
R4A new segmenter can differ from XL0-v3 in two ways at once. At params.yaml's recipe the fine-tune changes data and augmentation (ADR 0027), so the comparison cannot say which one moved a result. D3.
R5The owner's time. Every training and validation positive without a pass3 label needs a verdict (ADR 0015): 23 at D2 (c), 18 of them from ML-8's appends of 2026-10-17 and 18, never labelled. The comparison then judges 76 test positives. XL0-v3's fine-tune took about 7 h at 8 threads (ML-6 D15).

7. Scope and acceptance

  • The photos of 2026-10-21 stripped, in DVC and pushed; both sessions cropped; every new crop in the pool (D1); the segmenter dataset appended at 3 s (ADR 0029).
  • A label session carried from pass3, every training and validation positive without a label judged by the owner (D2 (c): the dataset as appended). Done 2026-10-21: pass4, 22 accepted and 1 dropped of 23, 233 labels in all.
  • A new XL0 decoder fine-tuned in its own DVC stage with D3's augmentation (and, by D8, a second at lr 3e-5), and compared with XL0-v3 on the test split; the owner's choice and threshold recorded (D4).
  • If a new segmenter is adopted: every session re-cropped, its new fallbacks shown to the owner, the DINOv3 fixture regenerated (experiments.md).
  • The classifier at seeds 42, 123 and 7 as recorded runs, reported as ranges with per-class F1 and the green photos of 2026-10-21 alone, compared as D5 says; its probe refitted and measured with ood_eval.
  • The chosen models converted, within ML-7 D7's bars on the serving host (ADR 0020), and deployed if D6 says so; docs/status.md, the README and docs/inference_path.html updated for the live models.

Out of scope

  • New countries, and splitting a country's classes by roast state (ML-3 D4).
  • A held-out session of a new bag or a new phone (ML-4 D3), though R1 would want one.
  • The grouping rule (ADR 0029) and the classifier's recipe.
