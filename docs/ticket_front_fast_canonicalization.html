Ticket OPS-22 · coffee-vision · the front's canonicalization

The front canonicalizes with 8-bit colour, effort 3, quality 90 and a 15/18 MP cap, the models and the store unchanged

The owner's decision (D1): the front's check service canonicalizes visitors' photos faster and smaller, and the canonical store, training and both models stay as they are. A 50 MP photo now waits about 20 s on the front and 18 s crossing to yappy; measured here, the new settings take about 3 s and make a 1–3 MB file. This departs from ADR 0033 and the coding standards' train/inference parity, on purpose (R1). Every decision taken; live since 2026-11-10 (20261110T1117Z-42ba447); the live numbers (§7) not yet read.

1. Summary

Today the front makes the same canonical photo the canonical store holds (ADR 0033): float LittleCMS into Rec.2020, libjxl effort 7, quality 95, kept at full size up to 60 MP and over that area-averaged to 50 MP. After, the front makes its own, cheaper one: LittleCMS's optimized integer transform, effort 3, quality 90, kept up to 18 MP and over that area-averaged to at most 15 MP. Everything else about the canonical photo stays: Rec.2020, D7's header, upright, no metadata, the same check at the inference host. The front no longer passes an already canonical upload on as it is (ML-19 D17): it re-encodes every photo, the page's examples included (D3). The store, its stages, training, both models and the inference host's code do not change. The work: the front's settings as parameters of the shared module with training's as their defaults, the front passing its own; an ADR recording the departure; a release, checked by --verify's examples alone (D5).

2. Background

  • The front's time is the work itself. Its log (/var/log/coffee-cv-front/requests.jsonl, 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-10) shows 0.34–0.44 s per megapixel for every photo: 12 MP 4.3–5.4 s, the nine 50 MP photos 17.4–20.8 s; swap from 0 to 104 MB made no difference to the rate. Request cfe99dda (a 50 MP Pixel 9 Pro JPEG, 2026-11-10): canonicalization 19.6 s, crossing 17.8 s for its 5.36 MB, yappy's decode 5.8 s, 66 s from selection to answer.
  • Where the front's time goes (ML-21's measurements, a 12 MP photo on this machine): libjxl at effort 7 3.3 s, the float colour conversion 2.55 s, serially. Encode time does not depend on quality: at effort 3, 2.04 s at q95, 2.02 s at q90, 1.94 s at q85. Quality changes the size: q90's files are 28–36% smaller.
  • What effort 3 leaves out (libjxl 0.12.0's encode_effort.md and encoder source): the variable block-size search (8×8 DCT only), the adaptive quantization field, chroma from luma, Gaborish and the per-block edge-preserving filter strength. Patches and splines, effort 7's own additions, are already skipped in our chunked (streaming) encode.
  • The 8-bit colour path: LittleCMS with its default optimization, from the source's integers to Rec.2020 uint16: 0.29 s against 2.4 s for a 12 MP photo, within 31/65535 of the float path before the encode.
  • The proposed pipeline, measured 2026-11-10 on this machine (Ryzen 5 5600U) held to 2 CPUs by taskset, as the front's 2 vCPU; the front ran ML-19's pipeline in about 0.73× this machine's time:

  • Fidelity, against the photo taken straight to 8-bit sRGB without JPEG XL, at full size (ML-21's measurements): mean error 0.64 → 1.02 levels (12 MP) and 0.77 → 1.63 (50 MP) at effort 3 q90 against the live settings; pixels more than 2 levels off 2.5% → 7.5% and 3.1% → 22%; the error in fine detail (luma minus its 3×3 mean, relative to its energy) 0.025 → 0.057 and 0.028 → 0.193. Effort 3 at q95 alone stays at the live error (0.65, 0.79). The 15 MP cap removes, besides, about 70% of a 50 MP photo's pixels.
  • What the models were trained on: canonical photos at q95, effort 7, float colour, up to 60 MP: the segmenter dataset's 50 MP Pixel photos (36 in pass13 alone, ML-19 D27) and every 12–17 MP photo at full size.

3. Before and after

Now

  • The front makes the store's canonical photo (ADR 0033): float LittleCMS, effort 7, q95 (ML-19 D7, D31, D50).
  • Kept at full size up to 60 MP; over 60 MP area-averaged to 50 MP (ML-19 D27).
  • An already canonical upload, as each of the page's examples is, passes the front as it is (ML-19 D17).
  • A 50 MP photo: about 20 s on the front, 18 s crossing, 5.7 s decoding on yappy.

After

  • The front makes its own canonical photo: LittleCMS optimized integer transform, effort 3, q90.
  • Kept at full size up to 18 MP; over 18 MP area-averaged (OpenCV INTER_AREA, as now) to at most 15 MP.
  • Every upload re-encoded, an already canonical one included: a picked example goes the visitor's way (D3).
  • A 50 MP photo: about 3 s here (less on the front), about 4 s crossing, a 15 MP decode on yappy (about 1.7 s by ML-21's rate).
  • The store, training and the inference host as they are; the inference host cannot tell the two apart (D7's header is the same).

4. What does not change

  • The canonical store and training. data/canonical/, its DVC stages, the crops, both models: D1. canonical.py's defaults stay training's, and the golden-file test (ML-19 D8) holds them.
  • The canonical photo's format. Rec.2020, D7's encoding as an enumeration, 16-bit lossy JPEG XL, upright, no metadata: the inference host's is_canonical accepts both, and its code does not change.
  • The inference host's decode (ML-21): the same 16-bit decode and NOOPTIMIZE conversion to 8-bit sRGB.
  • The upload cap (40 MB, ML-19 D23), unless D2 says otherwise.

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1The models serve photos unlike the ones they were trained and evaluated on, so the reported metrics stop describing the live site. Departs from ADR 0033 ("training and serving read the same canonical photo, so a change to canonicalization ... needs a re-canonicalization and a retrain") and from CODING_STANDARDS.md's train/inference parity ("an accurate method offline with a cheap one live is rejected, because it inflates every reported metric"). Accepted by the owner in D1, and unmeasured by D5: no check of the models' answers beyond --verify's examples. Mitigation: an ADR amending ADR 0033, and docs/status.md saying the live input differs from training's and that no metric was measured on it.
R2Less bean texture: q90 and the 15 MP cap both remove fine detail, which the classifier's patches read. §2's fidelity numbers: detail error 2–7× at q90; a 50 MP photo keeps about 30% of its pixels. The classifier samples patches by the measured bean pitch, so a downscaled photo gives patches from fewer pixels, as a 12 MP photo's already are. Not measured (D5); the live site's answers are the only evidence.
R3Re-encoding the 50 MP grinder example fits the front's memory with little room. Its canonical JPEG XL decodes at 16 bits, whole: 300 MB of pixels. Measured here on 2 CPUs, 2026-11-10: 3.45 s, 1.06 MB, peak RSS 467 MB, under the unit's MemoryMax=600M. The released front's peak_mb for the example is read and recorded (§7). A second lossy generation (q95, then q90) is accepted by D3: a visitor's JPEG is one too. P2, 2026-11-10, through the code as committed: 3.79 s, 1.06 MB, peak 468 MB (VmHWM; this machine cannot hold a process to MemoryMax), analysis/ops22.
R4A change to the shared module changes the store. The front imports coffeecv.canonical, which built the store. The front's settings are parameters passed by check_service.py; the module's defaults stay training's; the golden-file test and the store's own tests run unchanged.
R5The 8-bit colour path is measured only on 8-bit sources. HEIC (10-bit), RAW (16-bit), 16-bit PNG and photos with transparency take other branches today. D4.

7. Scope and acceptance

  • An ADR amending ADR 0033: the front's canonical photo differs from the store's, by D1; ADR 0033 links it. GLOSSARY.md's "Canonicalization" names both.
  • canonical.py and jxl_encode.py take the colour path, effort, quality and the two thresholds as parameters, training's as defaults; check_service.py passes D1's. ML-19 D8's tests and the golden file pass unchanged.
  • Tests: a photo over 18 MP is stored at most 15 MP and one at 18 MP is kept; the front's settings give D7's header; an already canonical upload is re-encoded, not passed on (D3), and its log line says canonicalized; each D4 source decodes within one 8-bit level of the float path's conversion before the encode.
  • The check service's log line names the settings it canonicalized with, so the timelines tell the releases apart.
  • Released, --verify passed; the front's canonicalize_ms, canonical_bytes and crossing_ms, and yappy's decode_ms, read from the live logs for 12 MP and 50 MP photos, and the grinder example's peak_mb (R3), recorded here.
  • docs/status.md and docs/inference_path.html say the live input differs from training's, and how.
