1. Summary

Since ML-37 (D15, ADR 0043) the front makes the page's preview from its own model picture of the photo, and the host's models read the host's model picture of the same canonical JPEG XL. D15 promises the two are the same picture; D22 (answer (b)) checks it at every release: a small script, run from each host's own release over the SSH the deploy already uses, reads one fixture back to its model picture (coffeecv.canonical.load_canonical), and the deploy compares the hashes and the largest difference. Today --verify has no such step. The work: the script, the step in verify, its line in the deploy's output, and the runbook.

2. Background

  • ML-37 D22, 2026-11-26: "The deploy runs a small script from each host's release on one fixture, over the SSH it already uses, and compares the model picture's hash and largest difference: at most 1 level is accepted and recorded, more stops for the owner. No endpoint: the API is open to any origin (ADR 0035)." Only the plan's P8 names it; no phase's code added it (git grep finds no model-picture check in scripts/ or webapp/deploy/).
  • The by-hand check at P8's release, 20261128T1411Z-75d5dd9 (the ML-37 plan's P8 Activity): the smoke photo (PXL_20260825_193325070.jpg, 12 MP) canonicalized on the front with FRONT_SETTINGS by its release, that JPEG XL read back on the front, on yappy and on the training machine. The three hashes all differ; every pair at most 1 level apart: front and host on 431 of 12 M pixels, front and here on 74, host and here on 357. So an exact-hash check would fail every release; the largest difference is the check, the hashes are recorded beside it.
  • The front's release holds coffeecv/ (canonical, readback, preview and their imports) and a .venv with numpy and imagecodecs, but not webapp/deploy/; the host's release holds the whole tree, and its fixtures live in /opt/coffee-cv/fixtures/. verify already runs commands on both (vm, fe) and copies files to both (to_vm, to_fe).
  • A model picture is 36 MB at 12 MP: the comparison needs both pictures in one place, or a per-row digest from each host compared row by row, or the front's picture sent to the host.

3. Before and after

Now

  • --verify checks the preview is a JPEG at the mask's size, not that it was made from the host's picture.
  • A libjxl, numpy or CPU change on one host that moves the model picture goes unseen until an answer changes.

After

  • --verify prints both hashes and the largest difference, and fails over 1 level (D1).
  • docs/agents/deploy.md names the check.

4. What does not change

  • No endpoint. D22 ruled one out: the API is open to any origin (ADR 0035).
  • The read-back. A difference over the bar is reported, not fixed here.

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1The front's memory. The front runs under MemoryMax=600M and its photos peak at 537 MB (ML-37 P4). The check runs outside the check service, as the deploy user, on a 12 MP photo (the by-hand run did, without trouble); it must not run while the service holds a photo, or should use the smallest fixture that still has a gain map or wide gamut.

6. Decisions

IDQuestionOptionsOwner decision
D1A difference over 1 level at a full deploy's verify, after the flip? (a) a failed check: the deploy rolls both hosts back, as a wrong example answer does (OPS-16 D14) (b) a warning: the release stays live, the owner decides Open. Recommended (a): D22's "stops for the owner" read as the deploy's own stop.
D2Which fixture? (a) the smoke photo, already on the host (b) a fixture with a gain map or wide gamut, which exercises more of the read-back Open.

7. Scope and acceptance

  • A script in webapp/deploy/, run from each host's release, that prints a fixture's model picture hash (and whatever the comparison needs), with a test here.
  • verify runs it on both hosts and checks the largest difference against D1's rule; the hashes and the difference in its output.
  • Rehearsed with scripts/rehearse_deploy_local.sh, then one --verify on the live release.
  • docs/agents/deploy.md's "Deployed" paragraph names the check.

Out of scope

  • Making the three CPUs agree bit for bit.
  • Checking every fixture, or the store's settings.
