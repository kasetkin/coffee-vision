Owner request, 2026-10-08. Rename ood_positives to segmenter_positives and change what it is used for. Today it only gives the OOD guard's calibrated threshold, and the release uses 0.5 regardless. It now holds a good selection of different photos, mostly not frame-filling, for segmenter fine-tuning. Rework the whole segmentation dataset and how photos are taken for it: take all of this folder, an equal number of random photos from the main dataset, the OOD negatives and ood_positives_internet, and recreate the segmenter's train/validation/test sets. Rewrite how OOD is defined and measured. Switch from efficientvit_sam_l0 to efficientvit_sam_xl0. Then train a new segmenter and a new classifier.

1. Summary

Today the segmenter is fine-tuned L0 (ft_s123), trained on ML-2's lists (labels/ml2/photo_lists.yaml) with labels the Claude judge accepted. After this ticket it is fine-tuned XL0, trained on a new ~410-photo dataset whose labels the owner judged, in two passes: XL0-v1 from labels made with today's models, XL0-v2 from labels made with XL0-v1. The OOD guard is the linear probe at a fixed 0.5 after every classification. The classifier is retrained on the new segmenter's crops.

2. Background

  • The folder after the owner's 2026-10-08 additions holds 128 photos: the 58 in its manifest plus 70 new ones, all byte-identical copies of photos elsewhere in dataset/ (22 from random_date_raccoon, 17 from 2026-07-24__first_pictures, 31 from the dated capture sessions). All 89 photos added that day went through coffeecv.strip_metadata: 31 had private tags, 0 failed a check (labels/ml3/strip_manifest.csv).
  • The guard's threshold is not calibrated in practice. The shipped probe holds 0.5; its conformal calibration gave 0.5982 and the owner set 0.5 (ML-2 plan, 2026-10-03). On the 2026-10-02 holdout it caught 56/56 negatives and refused 2/26 genuine photos at 0.5.
  • Today's segmenter misses the segmenter positives. ft_s123 returns an empty mask on 6 of the 25 in ML-2's pos_seg_eval, and 8 of 25 fall below the tiny-mask threshold (seg_min_area_frac 0.083, ML-2 D18), against a 97.9% judge pass rate on pool photos.
  • The box-only fine-tune broke point prompts (measured 2026-10-08, 63 accepted ML-2 masks drawn by pretrained L0 from a box plus stored points). Replaying the same points on ft_s123 reproduces the mask (IoU ≥ 0.9) on 17–35% and returns an empty mask on 35–48%; simulated corrective clicks make its masks worse, not better. Pretrained L0 reproduces 100% and improves with each click, but its box alone selects the tray. SAM keeps point-following by training with points sampled from the error region (SAM paper, appendix A); ML-2's fine-tune trained the box only.
  • Sources for the new dataset. Pools: 1,248 photos in 16 session folders. OOD negatives: 142 in three batches. Internet positives: 12.
  • EfficientViT-SAM's own recipe (arXiv 2402.05008): focal and dice at 20:1, AdamW with cosine decay, horizontal flips, box or points at random, 512 px for L and 1024 px for XL. ML-2's fine-tune matches it apart from the prompts.

3. Before and after

Now

  • dataset/ood_positives: "photos the shipped model has never seen", for guard calibration; its dev split also feeds the segmenter (ML-2 D26).
  • Guard threshold calibrated by split-conformal on independent-day photos (ADR 0005), then overridden to 0.5.
  • Segmenter: L0, decoder fine-tuned on the box prompt only, labels judged by Claude (ADR 0011).
  • Segmenter lists from ML-2: pool split at seed 239, OOD dev positives and negatives.

After

  • dataset/segmenter_positives: a curated selection for the segmenter; no "never seen" claim.
  • The probe at a fixed 0.5 after every classification, fitted on training and validation negatives, measured on test (new ADR).
  • Segmenter: XL0 at 1024 px, decoder fine-tuned on box and point prompts, labels judged by the owner (new ADR).
  • One append-only segmenter dataset file: segmenter positives, as many pool photos, all negatives and internet positives, split 60/15/25.

4. What does not change

  • What a mask is and when it is accepted. ML-2 D2, D10 and D13: the bean region, coffee beans only, the same acceptance rule (labels/ml2/accept_rule.md), now applied by the owner.
  • The segmentation stage's shape: the fixed whole-image box prompt at serving, the crop rule (ML-2 D4), the same segmenter at training and serving, decoder-only fine-tuning (ML-2 D7).
  • Metadata stripping (ADR 0013): every photo in the new dataset is already stripped.
  • The classifier's recipe (ADR 0006): only its crops change.
  • ML-2's record. labels/ml2/, its verdicts, base masks and planted-defect results stay in git; nothing is rewritten.

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1A photo lands on both sides of the split. 70 segmenter positives are byte copies of pool photos, and bursts are shot seconds apart. Byte-identical files and shots within 120 s form one group (D13); the pool draw skips photos already in segmenter_positives (D12). Tested in P5.
R2No comparison with today's segmenter. Accepted (D6). The test split holds pool photos as well as segmenter positives, so a regression on ordinary tray photos shows in the per-source pass rates.
R3The guard loses its unseen-photo calibration. Accepted (D2). The new ADR measures it on the segmenter test split instead (D16).
R4Point corrections on pretrained L0 cost many clicks. Its box alone selects the tray, so a declined photo starts from the tray mask; ML-2's point-placed masks needed a median of 6–13 points. Accepted (D19): ft_s123 still proposes round 1, so only declined photos need points.
R5XL0-v1 may not follow points either. Point prompts in its training (D15) are SAM's own remedy. The point probe (D19) checks it before pass 2; if it fails, pass 2 corrections use pretrained XL0.
R6XL0 is slower on the VM's CPU. 3.4× L0's compute per the paper (185 vs 35 GMACs, 117 M vs 35 M parameters). No limit (D18); measured in P4, before labelling.
R7Segmenter-trained photos are in the classifier's test split. Not new: ML-2's segmenter did the same. Crops on those photos may be slightly better than on new photos.

D6What comes after the new dataset? — 2026-10-08: a new segmenter, then a new classifier on its crops.
D7What are the photo kinds called? — 2026-10-08: positive, pool photo (not "main dataset"), segmenter positive (not "OOD positive"); judge now means the owner or a Claude model. In GLOSSARY.md.
D8What are the splits called? (a) train/cal/test. (b) train/validation/test. (b), 2026-10-08, written out in full ("validation", not "val"). Validation only chooses the fine-tune checkpoint.
D9What is the OOD guard? (a) The probe at 0.5 on every photo. (b) The probe only when the segmenter fails. (c) The segmenter's empty mask alone. (a), 2026-10-08: the linear probe at a fixed 0.5 runs after every classification, on the crop or, when the segmenter fails (empty or tiny mask) or is switched off, on the whole frame. The segmenter's empty-mask rate on test negatives is reported beside it.
D10Where does a "segmentation off" checkbox in the webapp go? (a) This ticket. (b) Its own ticket. (b), 2026-10-08: OPS-8, later. Found while writing it: the "Use full photo instead" button already classifies the whole photo with the probe applied; OPS-8 decides whether it becomes a checkbox.
D11What happens when photos are added later? (a) Frozen assignments, new photos assigned on arrival. (b) Re-split everything. (a), 2026-10-08: append-only. The pool draw tops up by the same count, also assigned on arrival.
D12How are the pool photos drawn? (a) Proportional to folder size. (b) Equal per folder. (c) Equal per camera. (a), 2026-10-08: as many as segmenter_positives holds (128), skipping photos already in it.
D13Split ratios? 70/15/15 or 60/15/25. 60/15/25 train/validation/test within each source, 2026-10-08. Byte-identical files and shots within 120 s form one group and never straddle two splits.
D14Who judges the masks? (a) The Claude judge, as ML-2. (b) The owner. (b), 2026-10-08: the owner accepts or declines every training and validation label and every test mask (about 750 verdicts). Opus costs too much of the session limit and Sonnet is too sloppy. On a decline the owner places points: hold g and left-click for an include point, r for an exclude point; the mask is redrawn and judged again, up to 3 rounds; still declined means dropped, reason logged. Negatives get empty labels. No Claude recheck and no planted-defect test of the judge; a Claude recheck only if the metrics are bad, on the owner's call.
D15The fine-tune recipe? — 2026-10-08: ML-2's decoder-only recipe, one seed (123; ML-2's three seeds were indistinguishable). Plus point prompts in training: some batches add 1–3 points sampled from the error region of the box-only prediction, the box always kept (SAM's method). Serving and checkpoint selection stay box-only.
D16Do all negatives and internet positives join, and how is the guard measured? — 2026-10-08: all 142 negatives and 12 internet positives, split per D13 within each (batch, tag); the old dev/holdout labels retire. The probe is fitted on training and validation negatives and measured on test negatives and positives.
D17How are the changed decisions recorded? — 2026-10-08: two new ADRs, one superseding ADR 0011 (XL0 without a comparison, owner-judged labels, and why), one superseding ADR 0005 (D9, D16).
D18Which EfficientViT-SAM? — XL0 at 1024 px, the owner's decision, 2026-10-08, without a comparison against L0. No latency limit (ML-2 D8 stands); its time per photo on the VM is measured before labelling.
D19How are the labels made? — 2026-10-08, two passes. Pass 1: ft_s123 proposes round 1; a declined photo is redrawn by pretrained L0 from the box plus the owner's points (ML-2 D25's path; ft_s123 does not follow points, §2). XL0 fine-tuned on these labels is XL0-v1; the owner judges its test masks. Pass 2: XL0-v1 proposes; a point probe (replayed and simulated clicks against pass-1 accepts) decides whether XL0-v1 or pretrained XL0 redraws on a decline. The owner reviews every training and validation photo again, dropped ones included; a pass-2 verdict replaces the pass-1 label. XL0 fine-tuned again is XL0-v2; the owner judges its test masks. The review page encodes each photo once before a session, so a click reruns only the decoder.
D20Which segmenter goes on, and what does the classifier report show? — 2026-10-08: XL0-v2 by default; the owner picks after seeing v1 against v2 (test pass rate per source, empty-or-tiny rate on test negatives). The classifier is retrained with seeds 42/123/7 and reported as ML-2 D24 printed it against the shipped model (per seed validation and test patch macro-F1, paired differences, per-class F1), plus the D16 probe numbers. No numeric gate; the owner decides whether to ship.
D21The tiny-mask threshold? — 2026-10-08: re-derived as ML-2 D18 did (half the smallest bean region on a genuine test photo), on the new test split with the chosen segmenter's accepted masks; positives below the old and new values reported.

7. Scope and acceptance

  • The 2026-10-08 data is committed to DVC, pushed by explicit target, and every object verified on the remote.
  • dataset/segmenter_positives exists with a manifest row for every photo and a README for its new role; nothing live still reads dataset/ood_positives.
  • Two ADRs supersede 0005 and 0011, and the guard code follows the new one.
  • The segmenter runs XL0 at 1024 px at training and serving; its time per photo on the VM is reported.
  • The segmenter dataset is one committed, append-only file: every photo, its source, split and group; no group straddles two splits.
  • The review page takes g/r point clicks and redraws the mask.
  • XL0-v1 and XL0-v2 fine-tuned, their test masks judged by the owner, and one chosen.
  • The tiny-mask threshold re-derived; the pools re-cropped; the classifier retrained and reported; the owner decides whether to ship.
