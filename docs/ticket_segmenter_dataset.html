Owner request, 2026-10-08. Rename ood_positives to segmenter_positives and change what it is used for. Today it only gives the OOD guard's calibrated threshold, and the release uses 0.5 regardless. It now holds a good selection of different photos, mostly not frame-filling, for segmenter fine-tuning. Rework the whole segmentation dataset and how photos are taken for it: take all of this folder, an equal number of random photos from the main dataset, the OOD negatives and ood_positives_internet, and recreate the segmenter's train/cal/test sets. Rewrite how OOD is defined and measured. Then train a new segmenter and a new classifier.

1. Summary

Today the segmenter trains on ML-2's lists (labels/ml2/photo_lists.yaml): 554 main-dataset photos in seg_train, 15 OOD dev positives and 56 OOD dev negatives, with append-only eval lists. After this ticket it trains on a new dataset built from four sources, split from scratch, with test photos (positive or negative) never used for training. The work splits into: committing the 2026-10-08 data; the rename; a new ADR for the OOD guard; building and splitting the new dataset; labelling it with the ML-2 judge; fine-tuning the segmenter; re-cropping and retraining the classifier.

2. Background

  • The folder after the owner's 2026-10-08 additions holds 128 photos: the 58 in its manifest plus 70 new ones. All 70 are byte-identical copies of photos elsewhere in dataset/ (22 from random_date_raccoon, 17 from 2026-07-24__first_pictures, 31 from the dated capture sessions), so they are photos the classifier trained on. All 89 photos added on 2026-10-08 (these 70, 18 in random_date_raccoon, 1 in classes_labels_only) went through coffeecv.strip_metadata the same day: 31 had private tags, 0 failed a check (labels/ml3/strip_manifest.csv).
  • The guard's threshold is not calibrated in practice. The shipped probe (models/allrigs_dino3b16_seg_country_s123.ood_probe.json) holds 0.5; its conformal calibration on 28 dev positives gave 0.5982, and the owner set 0.5 (ML-2 plan, 2026-10-03). The folder's other use is the only false-refusal measurement on unseen photos (2/26 genuine refused at 0.5 on the 2026-10-02 holdout).
  • ML-2's lists overlap the new folder: 18 of the 70 copies are in them (13 seg_train, 2 seg_val, 3 seg_eval, one of those also in audit_sample), and 40 of the original 58 are in pos_seg_train / pos_seg_eval. Rebuilding from scratch (D3) retires those lists, so the overlap stops mattering.
  • Sources. Main dataset: 1,248 photos in dataset/*/class_* across 16 folders. OOD negatives: 142 in three batches (86 dev, 56 holdout). Internet positives: 12 (7 dev, 5 holdout).
  • Labels. ML-2's judge loop (coffeecv/seg_labels.py, ADR 0011) makes the training masks with no hand annotation. Verdicts in labels/ml2/verdicts.jsonl are keyed by the mask's hash, so an existing verdict is reusable only for an identical mask.

3. Before and after

Now

  • dataset/ood_positives: "photos the shipped model has never seen", for guard calibration and evaluation; its dev split also feeds the segmenter (ML-2 D26).
  • Guard threshold calibrated by split-conformal on independent-day photos (ADR 0005), then overridden to 0.5.
  • Segmenter lists from ML-2: main-dataset split at seed 239, plus OOD dev positives and negatives; holdouts never listed.

After

  • dataset/segmenter_positives: a curated selection for the segmenter; no "never seen" claim.
  • A new ADR supersedes 0005: what the guard is, its fixed threshold, and how it is measured.
  • A new segmenter dataset: all of segmenter_positives, an equal number of random main-dataset photos, the OOD negatives and the internet positives, split train/cal/test from scratch.
  • A segmenter fine-tuned on it, the pools re-cropped with it, and a classifier retrained on the new crops.

4. What does not change

  • What a mask is and when it is accepted. ML-2 D2, D10 and D13 stay: the bean region, coffee beans only, the same acceptance rule in the judge's prompt.
  • The segmentation stage itself (ADR 0011): EfficientViT-SAM-L0, the fixed whole-image box prompt, the crop rule, the same at training and serving.
  • Metadata stripping (ADR 0013): every photo in the new dataset is already stripped.
  • The classifier's recipe (ADR 0006): only its crops change.
  • ML-2's history. labels/ml2/ and its verdicts stay in git as the record; nothing is rewritten.

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1A photo lands on both sides of the split. 70 photos in segmenter_positives are byte copies of main-dataset photos, and bursts are shot seconds apart. The split groups byte-identical files and near-duplicate shots and keeps each group on one side; the random main-dataset draw skips photos already in segmenter_positives.
R2No comparison with today's segmenter. The new test split is new, and the owner deferred scoring ft_s123 on ML-2's lists (D5). A regression on ordinary tray photos could show only in the classifier's numbers. Mitigation: the test split includes main-dataset photos (D3), so the new segmenter is measured on that population.
R3The guard loses its unseen-photo measurement. Accepted by the owner (D2). The new ADR says what replaces it.
R4Fewer positive training photos than ML-2. About 256 positives instead of ML-2's 554 + 15, before the split. The owner's choice (D3): coverage of setups over volume. Fine-tune results on cal and test show whether it is enough.
R5Segmenter-trained photos are in the classifier's test split. Not new: ML-2's segmenter trained on photos from the classifier's test split too (seed 239 is no head seed). Crops on those photos may be slightly better than on new photos.

7. Scope and acceptance

  • The 2026-10-08 data is committed to DVC, pushed by explicit target, and every object verified on the remote.
  • dataset/segmenter_positives exists with a manifest row for every photo and a README for its new role; nothing in code or docs still reads dataset/ood_positives, apart from history.
  • A new ADR supersedes ADR 0005, and the guard code follows it.
  • The new segmenter dataset is one committed file: every photo, its source, its split; no group straddles two splits; no test photo is in train or cal.
  • Every train and cal positive has a judge label or a logged drop reason; negatives have empty labels.
  • A segmenter fine-tuned on the new train split, checkpoint chosen on cal, scored on test.
  • The pools re-cropped with it and the classifier retrained; results reported to the owner, who decides whether to ship.

Out of scope

  • Deploying the new models: a release goes through docs/agents/deploy.md as its own step, on the owner's call.
  • Comparing the new segmenter with ft_s123 on ML-2's lists (D5).
  • New photos for the pools (ML-4).
