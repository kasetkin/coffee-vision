Ticket OPS-6 · coffee-vision · webapp

The upload preview shows the photo's bean region: everything else tinted, with the crop box and a mask outline on top

When a user picks a photo, the web page shows the segmenter's mask on it. The photo's bean region stays as it is, and everything else gets a translucent tint. The point is that the user sees what the classifier will look at, and can switch to the full photo if the mask is wrong. Today the page dims only what lies outside the crop box, and the mask itself is thrown away. Written after a grilling session with the owner (D1–D12); planned in plan_webapp_mask_overlay.html, not implemented.

1. Summary

Today the preview after an upload shows the photo with everything outside the D4 crop box dimmed. After this ticket it shows the photo's bean region untouched, everything else under a 55% black tint, the crop box as a white outline, and a thin magenta line just outside the bean region's edge that a checkbox can hide. The segmenter already runs for /crop; only the mask has to reach the browser. The work splits into: /crop returns the mask over the whole photo at the thumbnail's resolution; the deploy's smoke check compares that mask with a tolerance; the page draws the overlay; the deploy.

2. Background

Read at a290ce4.

  • /crop already segments. It calls segment_bean_region and answers cropped, box (fractions of the full image) and needs_review (webapp/app.py, crop()). It is advisory: /classify segments again on its own and never takes a box from the client.
  • The full photo's mask never leaves the segmenter. segment_and_crop predicts it, and mask_and_crop keeps only its cut to the D4 box (None on a fallback); that cut is the mask segment_bean_region returns and /crop drops. The diagnostics it also returns go into classify_one's entry, which release_probe classify writes out, so they are no place for a mask (coffeecv/segment_beans.py, coffeecv/infer.py).
  • The page shows the box as a spotlight. #crop-overlay dims everything outside the box with a 55% black box-shadow, clipped to the photo's rect by #image-mask, which is a CSS clipping box, not a segmentation mask. "Use full photo instead" sends skip_crop=1, and then the segmenter does not run at all (webapp/static/index.html).
  • The classifier sees the mask, the fill and the crop. Non-bean pixels inside the crop are filled with ImageNet mean gray (124, 116, 104) (ML-2 D3), and the crop keeps 95% of bean pixels (ML-2 D4), so up to 5% of the bean region lies outside the box. An empty or tiny mask means the whole original photo, unfilled (ML-2 D18); /crop then answers cropped=false and the page shows nothing.
  • The thumbnail and the mask come from the same pixels. /preview and the segmenter both decode with load_rgb_image, which ignores EXIF orientation on purpose (the "Invariance over normalization" rule in CODING_STANDARDS.md; coffeecv/crop_tray.py). Both work on the stored pixels, so they line up, and a phone photo stored sideways with an Orientation tag shows sideways in the preview today. The thumbnail's long side is at most 1024 px (PREVIEW_MAX_DIM).
  • One request at a time. One sync gunicorn worker, a server-wide cap of one request in flight in nginx, and a 30 s timeout. The page calls /preview, then /crop, in sequence.
  • The deploy compares /crop exactly. release_probe.py compare-smoke walks the whole /crop body: numbers within 1e-3, strings equal. The expected answers are computed on the devcontainer and the served ones on the VM, and their masks differ: AVX2 and AVX-512 give different mask hashes, in about 1e-6 of the pixels (ML-2 P0, analysis/ml2_p0/README.md). A mask string would fail it.
  • The page is cacheable. nginx sets no Cache-Control on index.html, so a browser may keep a page from one release while the server runs another, after a deploy or a --rollback.
  • The review tool's outline. coffeecv/review_masks.py draws the mask's edge as a thin magenta line, (255, 0, 255) in coffeecv/seg_overlay.py, and Space toggles it.

3. Before and after

Now

  • /crop answers cropped, box, needs_review.
  • The preview dims everything outside the crop box.
  • A mask that falls back (ML-2 D18) shows nothing, with no word to the user.
  • The smoke check compares the /crop box.

After

  • /crop also answers mask (a 1-bit PNG of the whole photo, long side ≤ 1024 px, non-bean pixels transparent, base64, or null) and seg_fallback.
  • The preview tints everything outside the photo's bean region, outlines the crop box in white, and draws a magenta line just outside the bean region's edge, behind a "Show outline" checkbox that starts ticked.
  • A fallback shows no tint and a note that the whole photo will be classified.
  • The smoke check also compares the mask, by IoU ≥ 0.999.

4. What does not change

  • /classify. Same input, same answer; the overlay is display only.
  • The model and the segmenter. Same release model, same mask, same crop rule. The serving code gains a way to hand /crop the full photo's mask it already computes; what classify_one returns is bit-identical before and after.
  • The number of requests per upload. Still /preview then /crop; no extra segmenter pass.
  • /preview. It still returns the plain JPEG thumbnail; the overlay is drawn in the browser.
  • The request log. No new fields; /classify already logs mask_area_frac and seg_fallback.

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1The preview is not pixel for pixel what the model gets. The tint is translucent black, the model's fill is opaque gray (ML-2 D3). Accepted in D4: the contract is where the fill goes and where the crop cuts, not the colour.
R2The smoke check fails on a correct mask. Masks differ in about 1e-6 of their pixels across CPUs (ML-2 P0). Mitigated by comparing the decoded mask by IoU ≥ 0.999 instead of the string (D10). Not IoU = 1: the deploy compares a devcontainer mask with a VM mask, which ML-2 P0 found to differ, and one boundary pixel flipped by the thumbnail's area average and 0.5 cut would then fail a correct release. A real fault (the mask shifted by one thumbnail pixel, transposed, resized wrongly or empty) moves the IoU by about a percent or more, far below 0.999. P1 measures both margins on the smoke photo.
R3The mask is misaligned with the thumbnail. Both come from load_rgb_image's stored pixels and share the decoded image's frame: the page stretches the mask to the photo's displayed rect, as it places the box by fractions (D7), so a size that differs from the thumbnail's by a pixel is under a pixel on screen. What can still go wrong is width and height swapped, or one of the two rotated. Covered by a non-square photo, and by a photo with an Orientation tag that guards against a later change rotating one of the two and not the other (D10).
R4A larger /crop answer on a phone. A 1-bit PNG at ≤ 1024 px is a few KB, plus a third for base64. Measured on the smoke photo in P1 and written in the plan.
R5Exposing the full mask changes /classify. The change sits in coffeecv/infer.py and coffeecv/segment_beans.py, the code training and serving share. Mitigated by keeping the mask out of the diagnostics and checking classify_one bit-identical on the fixtures before and after (P1).
R6A cached page meets a server without mask. After a --rollback a browser can hold the new page while the server answers as before. The page treats a missing mask as "box only" (P3).
R7An answer from an earlier upload lands on the next photo. The file input stays enabled while /preview and /crop run, and nothing ties an answer to the upload that asked for it: a photo picked while the first one's /crop is in flight gets the first photo's mask drawn over it. Today the same race draws a wrong box. Mitigated by D13.

6. Decisions

IDQuestionOptionsOwner decision
D1What is the overlay for? (a) Show what the classifier looks at. (b) A nicer picture, loosely tied to the model's input. (a), 2026-10-07. Drawn on the original photo it is also nicer to look at, so (b) comes with it.
D2What stays untouched? (a) The photo's bean region, the segmenter's mask as is (gaps and shadows between beans included). (b) Single beans only, which needs per-bean masks. (a), 2026-10-07: the mask from the segmenter, unchanged.
D3What happens to the crop box? (a) Replaced by the mask. (b) Both: the tint for the mask, the box as an outline. (c) A switch between the two. (b), 2026-10-07. The box keeps its white outline and loses its dimming shadow; the tint replaces it. Bean region outside the box stays untinted.
D4What does the fill look like? (a) Opaque ML-2 D3 gray. (b) A translucent tint. (c) Opaque gray with a switch. (b), 2026-10-07: 55% black, as the spotlight today.
D5What if the mask is empty or tiny (ML-2 D18)? (a) Nothing, as today. (b) No tint, and a note. (c) Draw the tiny mask anyway. (b), 2026-10-07. The note says no coffee beans were found and the whole photo will be classified. It is not a refusal (ML-2 D9).
D6What does "Use full photo instead" do to the overlay? (a) Hide the tint, the box and the outline; switching back shows them, with no new request. (b) Leave them. (a), 2026-10-07. The overlay always shows what Classify will send. The button's label is unchanged.
D7How does the mask reach the browser? (a) A mask field in /crop's answer: a 1-bit PNG at the /preview thumbnail's size, base64. (b) A new /mask endpoint (a second segmenter pass). (c) Drawn into the /preview JPEG. (a), 2026-10-07. Advisory, like the box: /classify never takes it from the client. /crop also answers seg_fallback, for D5. Amended 2026-10-07 after the web review: the mask covers the whole decoded photo, scaled to long side ≤ PREVIEW_MAX_DIM (1024 px) with its aspect kept, by /crop's own rounding; it need not equal the thumbnail's size, since the page stretches it to the photo's displayed rect (R3). It is a 1-bit palette PNG, index 0 (not bean) transparent and index 1 (bean) opaque, for D14. /preview is not touched.
D8What if the release has no segmenter (crop_method not segment)? (a) mask: null and today's spotlight. (b) Not designed for. (b), 2026-10-07: an old model is served with its own old web app; a release ships its page and its model together (ADR 0008).
D9Does the bean region's edge get an outline? (a) No. (b) Yes. (c) Yes, behind a checkbox. (c), 2026-10-07: "let's try it". A thin magenta line, as in the review tool (seg_overlay.py, (255, 0, 255)), behind a "Show outline" checkbox that starts ticked. It hides the outline only, not the tint; it keeps its state across uploads within one page visit and is not saved.
D10How is it checked? As recommended: an app test on a tray photo (the mask has the thumbnail's size, is not empty, and agrees with the full-resolution mask scaled down); an EXIF-rotated photo (mask and thumbnail line up); the fallback (mask: null, seg_fallback: true); release_probe smoke compares the mask by IoU ≥ 0.98. As recommended, 2026-10-07. Amended 2026-10-07 after review: the photo with an Orientation tag checks that the mask has the thumbnail's size, both from the stored pixels, since load_rgb_image applies no rotation; the full-resolution agreement is checked independently of the resize helper, on a non-square photo; the smoke comparison takes IoU ≥ 0.999 (R2). The owner asked about IoU = 1; kept at 0.999 because devcontainer and VM masks differ (R2). Amended again 2026-10-07, with D7: "the thumbnail's size" becomes "long side ≤ 1024 px and the photo's aspect within one pixel", on both the tray photo and the photo with an Orientation tag.
D11What is out of scope? Editing the mask or box; a "model's view" switch; mask statistics in the /crop log; per-bean outlines. All four, 2026-10-07. See Out of scope.
D12Is the deploy part of the ticket? (a) Yes, a last phase that stops for the owner's go. (b) No. (a), 2026-10-07. The deploy follows deploy.md; "live" means checked on the live site with the smoke photo and one frame-filling photo.
D13What happens to an answer that arrives after the user has picked another photo (R7)? (a) A token per upload: each pick starts a new upload, and an answer for an earlier one is ignored. (b) Nothing, as today. (a), 2026-10-07. The token is checked after every wait in the upload's chain (the /preview answer, the /crop answer, the mask's decode), so a stale answer neither replaces the preview nor draws a box, tint, outline or note. Amended 2026-10-07 after the code review: on the live site nginx's server-wide cap of one request in flight refuses a photo picked while the first one's /crop runs (503), and the page shows "Could not read this photo." instead of the second photo's overlay. The owner accepts this: no stale answer lands on the page, and the user picks the photo again. The nginx settings stay as they are.
D14How does the page draw the tint and the outline from the mask? (a) Read the decoded PNG's pixels back with getImageData, find and widen the edge in JavaScript, and write one overlay image at the mask's resolution. (b) Composite: the mask's non-bean pixels are transparent (D7), so the tint is a 55% black fill with the mask cut out (destination-out), and the outline is the mask drawn at 16 offsets of radius 2 CSS px, minus the mask, filled magenta; both on canvases sized to the photo's displayed rect in device pixels. (b), 2026-10-07. No pixel readback, so the noise that Brave, Safari private browsing and Firefox private windows add to it cannot reach the overlay; the line is a crisp 2 CSS px at any screen density, redrawn on resize. The line lies just outside the bean region, over the tint, so no bean pixel is covered (D2); this differs from the review tool's band centred on the edge. The part of the ring beyond the canvas is cut off, so the photo's own border never gets a line. "Show outline" shows or hides the outline's canvas, with no redraw.

7. Scope and acceptance

  • /crop answers mask (1-bit palette PNG of the whole photo, long side ≤ 1024 px, index 0 transparent, base64, or null) and seg_fallback (D7).
  • The D10 tests pass: tray photo, photo with an Orientation tag, fallback, and the smoke comparison on a near and a far mask.
  • classify_one is bit-identical on the fixtures before and after (R5).
  • The page tints outside the bean region (D4), outlines the crop box (D3), draws the magenta line just outside the bean region by compositing, with no pixel readback (D14), behind a ticked "Show outline" checkbox (D9), shows the fallback note (D5), and hides it all on "Use full photo instead" (D6).
  • An answer for an earlier upload is ignored: picking a second photo while the first one's /crop is in flight never shows the first photo's overlay on it. On the dev server it shows the second photo's overlay; on the live site, where nginx refuses the second photo, the read error (D13, amended).
  • The owner has looked at the overlay on the local dev server and accepted it.
  • Deployed and checked on the live site (D12).

Out of scope

  • Letting the user edit the mask or the crop box. The TODO(crop editing) in index.html stays.
  • A switch to the opaque gray "model's view" (D4 (c)).
  • Logging mask statistics on /crop.
  • Per-bean outlines; they need per-bean masks (ML-2 D2 (b)).
  • Supporting a release without a segmenter (D8).
  • Sending each photo once instead of three times (/preview, /crop, /classify), and what the backend and the page pass to each other: OPS-7, a stub.
