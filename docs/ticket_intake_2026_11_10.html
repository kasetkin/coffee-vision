1. Summary

177 new photos arrived: 166 bean photos and 11 OOD negatives. 176 had private metadata and were stripped; the 177th, a GIF, had none. 31 of the bean photos were JPGs whose DNG (30) or GIF (1) twin of the same shot is also in the data; the owner took them out (D2, D4). The other 146 are in the data: 76 in random_date_raccoon, 57 in tasty_coffee, 2 in massimo, and 11 negatives in user_realworld.

All 146 are in the segmenter dataset, which grew from 1,504 to 1,650 photos with no entry removed or changed: the pool from 567 to 702, the negatives from 615 to 626. The classifier's pool grew from 1,493 to 1,628 crops. The owner's 467 renames went through every record that names a photo (D1).

On 2026-11-11 a second batch: 40 bean photos in random_date_raccoon (26 in class_001__Ethiopia_Sidamo, 3 in a new class_008__Ethiopia_Yirgacheffe, 7 in class_014__Colombia_Excelso, 4 in a new class_018__CostaRica) and 3 negatives. All were stripped, none is a copy, none a D18 fallback. The segmenter dataset grew to 1,693 photos (pool 742, negatives 629), the classifier's pool to 1,668 crops.

The same afternoon a third batch: 23 iPhone 15 Pro Max and 16 Pro photos in class_001__Ethiopia_Sidamo. 6 were copies and went to tmp/ as in D2 and D4: 2 HEICs byte for byte, and 4 "-1" files named .DNG that are JPEGs (a JPEG header; LibRaw refuses them), each the JPEG of the DNG shot in the same second. The other 17 were stripped; none is a copy; one, the 48 MP 20261111_173722 (a grinder's hopper in a café), is a D18 fallback. The segmenter dataset grew to 1,710 photos (pool 759), the classifier's pool to 1,682 crops. The owner's classes.txt gave 002 its washing station: 002;Kenya,Nyeri,Gatomboya;AA.

2. Background

  • The new folders. tasty_coffee holds class_001__Ethiopia_Sidamo (53) and class_008__Ethiopia_Yirgacheffe (4); massimo holds class_016__Ethiopia and class_017__Peru, one photo each. random_date_raccoon's new photos are in class_013__Brazil_Decaff (36), class_014__Colombia_Excelso (36) and class_013__Brazil_SulDeMinas (4).
  • Classes 016 and 017. The owner's two new classes.txt lines had a ; too many, and the loader refused the file; written in D15's format, 016;Ethiopia,Guji,SukeQuto;Daannisa and 017;Peru,Amazonas;Monteverde (D5). The class is the country, so neither is a new class to the classifier. Nor is the owner's 018;CostaRica; of the second batch, a Costa Rica of unknown region beside 004's Tarrazu.
  • Twins. A JPG and a DNG of one shot share a file name but for the suffix, and so share a crop name: the second crop overwrote the first, and 11 photos had no crop of their own. find_duplicates scored 20 such pairs above its threshold and 10 below it; it never compared the GIF with its JPG, as GIF was not an intake format (D6).
  • The canonical store. Every canonicalize and segcrop stage was stale on coffeecv/canonical.py since OPS-22 changed it; all were re-run, and every output that existed came back byte-identical (ADR 0033).
