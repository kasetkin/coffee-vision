1. Summary

Today the live models (segmenter XL0-v3 and classifier allrigs_dino3b16_segxl0v3_country_s42, ticket ML-6) were trained on data frozen on 2026-10-11 to 2026-10-13. Since then 20 pool photos have arrived without a crop and 27 negatives have joined the segmenter dataset. Training reads four camera pools, the manifests carry a camera column and scenario tags, and the classifier trains on unaugmented patches.

After this ticket: every photo available on 2026-10-19 (D6) is in the data; every session is cropped once by XL0-v3 and today's code (D1, D3); training reads one pool with no camera or rig logic (D2, D8); the classifier is fitted on 8 augmented views per patch generated per run and held only in RAM (D4), through a builder that refuses an argument the pinned library does not know; no manifest has a camera column or a scenario tag (D2, D9); the owner has judged the 20 new crops (D11); the model drops allrigs from its name (D10); its probe is refitted on unaugmented embeddings (D12), its LiteRT export passes the gates, and it is live (D7).

Eight phases (the plan): P1 commit the photos of 2026-10-18, the last to join (D6); P2 the strict builder; P3 no camera column, no tags; P4 one re-crop, measured against ML-6's, and the owner's judgement of the 20 new crops; P5 one pool with the new budget; P6 augmentation in the head fit; P7 the classifier at seeds 42, 123 and 7, its OOD reference and probe; P8 the LiteRT export and the deploy.

2. Background

Photos without a crop. ML-6 P8 re-cropped every session with XL0-v3 on 2026-10-13 (0675f40; 1,256 photos). Since then dataset/random_date_raccoon has gained 20 photos (d88533d and the ingest of 2026-10-18): 5 in class_013__Brazil_SulDeMinas and 15 in class_014__Colombia_Excelso. Neither the crop folders nor the pools have them, so no classifier has seen them. They are the two thin farm regions of ML-4, which now hold 12 and 27 photos. 14 are Pixel 9 Pro photos, 3 iPhone 15 Pro Max and 3 OnePlus KB2005, a session that merge_segcam_pixel merges whole into cam_pixel.

Crops stale against the code. dvc status reports every segcrop stage changed: segment_beans.py and sam_loader.py changed after P8, in ML-7 P8 (c434d11, 0f7617a) and OPS-7 P2 (6f88104). ML-7 measured the port: the mask moves 1–17 pixels on 165 of 782 photos, with no fallback or crop-box change, and left the stages built from the segmenter "not rerun or recommitted until the owner decides". Training stays on PyTorch (ML-7 D3).

Camera and rig logic today. Since ADR 0010 no camera is held out and the label is the class alone, but the camera still shapes training:

  • four pools, data/segcropped/cam_{pixel,sony,oneplus,iphone}, one merge_segcam_* stage each, named in dataset.CAPTURES and params.yaml train_capture_dirs;
  • the patch budget is per class per pool (params.yaml: 150 training, 40 validation, 40 test), so a class missing from cam_iphone gets three quarters of the others' patches: exp267 drew about 5,400 training patches and 1,440 each for val and test;
  • a pool's position in CAPTURES (capture_idx) seeds its patch boxes;
  • coffeecv.coverage_report counts photos per class and pool.

The five manifests (segmenter positives, internet positives, the three negative batches) also have a free-text camera column. Its one reader is seg_dataset.shot_time: for a photo with no EXIF capture time, it says whether the file-name time is UTC (pixel) or a local clock, for the 120 s grouping. On 2026-10-19, 21 of the 2,046 photos seg_dataset.scan reads took that path (16 Pixel photos without an EXIF time, among them the 8 renamed 20260910_*, and the 5 IMG_20260505_*); the other 2,025 have an EXIF time, and EXIF names their clock. Rebuilt with the column ignored and every file-name time read as one local clock, no group changed and the rebuild equalled the committed file; with file-name times not used at all, 14 photos lost their burst mates (the committed file still unchanged, since listed entries never move).

Augmentation today. Only the segmenter's fine-tune augments, with albumentations 2.0.8, drawn per sample as it trains and storing nothing (ADR 0018). The classifier applies none: fit_frozen_head.py gives the training split the eval transform ("frozen arms get no photometric augmentation"), the backbone is frozen and there is no TTA (ADR 0006). Its config still carries the ResNet era's in-house knobs (color_jitter_strength, brightness_jitter_strength, illum_gradient_strength, zoom and patch-scale), all off. The head is a convex logistic-regression fit on embeddings computed once per run (18.5 s for exp267), not an epoch loop. Embedding took 146.5 ms a patch on this host, about 13 minutes per seed for one view of every training patch; one embedding is 1,536 float32 values, 6 KB. The patches themselves are held in RAM as 448×448 uint8 arrays (602 KB each), one split at a time.

The library. albumentations 2.0.8 (2025-05-27, MIT) is the package's last release; every later version, 2.2 included, is the package albumentationsx (2.5.1 on 2026-10-11), AGPL-3.0-only or commercial, which collects usage statistics by default and installs under the same import name (PyPI, 2026-10-19). The library's online guide is written for the newer versions, and 2.0.8 does not reject their argument names: ColorJitter(brightness_range=(0.5, 0.6)) only warns "Argument(s) 'brightness_range' are not valid" and runs with the default brightness=(0.8, 1.2). seg_finetune._transform catches exceptions, not warnings; the live seg_ft.augment builds with no such warning (all 9 transforms, checked 2026-10-19), so XL0-v3 trained as configured.

Names. fit_frozen_head archives runs as allrigs_dino3b16_frozen_clsmean_… and the shipped models carry the same allrigs_ prefix, a rig word.

The negatives' tags. Every negative has a scenario_tag. Three things read it: the segmenter dataset splits newly added negatives within each (batch, tag) (seg_dataset.stratum); the probe fits only on ood_eval.CLEAN_NEGATIVE_TAGS, which leaves out wrong_bean_type; and ood_eval and seg_eval report per tag. Despite its name, wrong_bean_type holds no other bean species but three Wikimedia Commons photos of coffee-cherry material, in dataset/ood_negatives/2026-09__internet_proxy/wrong_bean_type/: wrong_bean_type_009.jpg (dried coffee cherries spread on a tray, close to a bean-tray capture), wrong_bean_type_010.jpg (a close-up of dried cherry husk) and wrong_bean_type_011.jpg (cascara, dried cherry skins). The segmenter dataset holds them as negatives with empty labels, 009 and 010 in its training split and 011 in test, so XL0-v3 was trained to give 009 and 010 no mask; only the probe skips them.

The live numbers. exp267–269: val macro-F1 0.9702–0.9815, test 0.9632–0.9748, in-distribution only (README, ML-1 R2). The probe caught 153/153 test negatives and refused 0/71 test positives (analysis/ml6_p8/summary.txt).

The host. Everything trains here (ML-6 D9): 12 cores, 13 GB of RAM (about 7 GB available), no GPU, 19 GB free on /workspace (98% used). The crops take 6.4 GB. The live site runs on webappremote's GPU; its last deploy (OPS-7, 2026-10-18) had to stop the live service by hand because the smoke worker did not fit beside it (OPS-10).

3. Before and after

Now

  • 20 pool photos without a crop; every segcrop stage stale against ML-7's ported mask code.
  • Four camera pools, a patch budget per pool, the pool's index in the patch seed.
  • A camera column in five manifests; scenario tags on the negatives, which split, filter the probe's fit and group reports.
  • Augmentation pipelines built leniently: an unknown argument falls back to a default with a warning.
  • The classifier fitted on unaugmented patches, named allrigs_….
  • The live classifier trained on ML-6's data of 2026-10-13.

After

  • Every photo available on 2026-10-19 cropped once by XL0-v3 and today's code, the 20 new crops judged by the owner, every crop stage current.
  • One pool, a patch budget per class (D8), no camera or rig in any training path; ADR 0024 supersedes ADR 0010.
  • No camera column and no scenario tag anywhere; negatives split, fit and report by batch.
  • One strict builder for every pipeline, failing on an unknown argument.
  • The head fitted on 8 augmented views per patch drawn per run and held in RAM, nothing augmented stored (ADR 0025); new names without allrigs.
  • A new classifier and probe, exported to LiteRT, live on the site.

4. What does not change

  • The segmenter. XL0-v3 stays (D3); no labels pass, no fine-tune.
  • The classes. The label is the country (ADR 0012); new photos join existing classes.
  • The classifier's design. A frozen DINOv3 ViT-B/16, cls_mean, a C=0.1 logistic-regression head, no TTA (ADR 0006). Augmentation changes what the head is fitted on, not the model.
  • The split. Photos pooled per class and split 70/15/15; val and test are not augmented.
  • The probe rule. The linear probe at a fixed 0.5, fitted on the segmenter dataset's training and validation negatives, measured on its test split (ADR 0016).
  • How photos enter. Metadata stripped before dvc add (ADR 0013); the segmenter dataset stays append-only.
  • The session folders and their names. No folder under dataset/ is renamed, moved or regrouped (owner 2026-10-19, D2); renaming would move every recorded path, and the names are history, not pools.
  • The library version. albumentations 2.0.8, pinned, not upgraded (ADR 0018).
  • What the OOD reference and probe are built from. Unaugmented embeddings, as today (D12).
  • Training on PyTorch, serving on LiteRT (ML-7 D3, ADR 0019, ADR 0020).

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1The new classifier cannot be compared pairwise with exp267–269. New photos re-draw the pooled split (ML-4 R2), one pool and a new budget (D2, D8) change the patch draw, and the crops move under them. Report both, say what moved, as ML-3 and ML-6 P8 did. The effect of augmentation alone is measured on one data state (D4).
R2Augmentation may show no gain in-distribution. Val sits near ceiling and in-distribution (README; ML-1 R1, R2); augmentation's promise is robustness to new cameras, light and bags, which no split here measures. The split is by single photo (dataset.split_photos_by_class), with no burst grouping, so near-duplicate shots of one tray can sit in train and test; that rewards memorisation, and a flat or negative delta is what it would show even if augmentation helps real uploads. The owner adopted it regardless (D4); the paired print, at equal total sample weight in both arms (plan P6), says what it does to the numbers we have.
R3The re-crop's changes hide inside the retrain. ML-7's port moves masks. Before anything trains, compare the re-crop with P8's recorded crops on the photos both have: crop boxes, fallbacks, mask pixels, and what changes in the patches placed by the D17 rule.
R4Time and memory of augmentation. Each view of every training patch is a backbone pass. At K = 8 and 600 patches per class: 6,000 × 9 views at 146.5 ms, about 2.2 h per seed plus val and test, about 7 h for three seeds, during which the host runs nothing else heavy. The unaugmented arm reuses the originals' embeddings. Memory: the training split's stored patches take about 3.6 GB, the embeddings about 330 MB, and the head fits' float64 copies of about 54,000 rows about 660 MB each, against about 7 GB available; augmented images are made and embedded in batches and dropped. A smoke fit measures time per view; peak RAM is measured on one full-size training split, which the smoke's 6 patches per class cannot show (plan P6).
R5Disk. 19 GB free; a second set of crops is 6.4 GB. The re-crop replaces the stage outputs in place and the old ones stay in the DVC cache; check free space before it, and push and verify before any dvc gc.
R6The deploy takes the site down for a few minutes. OPS-10: with the live worker on the GPU, the host had 3.6 GB available against the smoke worker's 5 GB, and OPS-7's deploy stopped the live service by hand (about 2 minutes down). Either OPS-10 is fixed first or the owner accepts the same short outage at the deploy's stop.
R7The strict builder touches the live segmenter's training code. Moving seg_finetune's builder could change XL0-v3's recorded pipeline. The segmenter's seeded stream must come out byte-identical on fixtures before the stage is recorded with dvc commit (plan P2).
R8Without tags, listed segmenter-dataset groups could merge. A negative's grouping subject becomes its batch, so two tags of one batch shot within 120 s would join one group. Rebuild in memory first; if any listed split or group would change, stop for the owner (plan P3). Outcome (P3): no listed split or group value changed; compared as partitions, user_samerig's green_legume and confusable_grain groups (98 s apart) chain, both in train, and three iPhone pool photos chain under the old code too; reported at P4's stop.
R9The pinned library gets no more fixes. 2.0.8 is the last MIT release and upstream is archived. Accepted in ADR 0018; the strict builder at least makes every argument mean what it says.

7. Scope and acceptance

  • Every photo available on 2026-10-19 (D6) stripped (ADR 0013), in DVC and on dvcnewremote, every object verified by md5, and in labels/ml5/seg_dataset.yaml by an append that changes no listed entry.
  • The re-crop compared with P8's crops on the photos both have: crop boxes, fallbacks, mask pixels, patch placement (R3).
  • No code reads a negative's scenario tag: the probe fits on every training and validation negative, the segmenter dataset stratifies new negatives by batch, reports go by batch (D9).
  • Every segcrop stage current in dvc status, run once with XL0-v3 and today's code.
  • No camera column in any manifest; seg_dataset.shot_time reads file-name times on one local clock; a rebuild of labels/ml5/seg_dataset.yaml still equals the committed file (D2).
  • The owner has judged the 20 new photos' crops; declined ones are in labels/ml3/pool_exclude.csv (D11).
  • One pool replaces the four camera pools; no training path, stage or report reads a camera or rig; the patch budget is 600/160/160 per class (D8); ADR 0024 supersedes ADR 0010.
  • One strict builder makes every albumentations pipeline, the segmenter's included, and fails on an argument the pinned version does not know; the segmenter's pipeline builds identically through it (plan P2).
  • The head fit draws 8 augmented views per training patch per run through the strict builder, holds only their embeddings in memory, writes nothing augmented, and records the pipeline, the view count and the library version in the run's config and the model card; ADR 0025 records the rule.
  • Classifier runs at 42, 123 and 7 under names without allrigs (D10), with ADR 0002's paired print against the same data unaugmented, and beside exp267–269 (R1).
  • A candidate, named dino3b16_segxl0v3_country_aug8_s<seed> (D10), with its OOD reference from unaugmented embeddings and a probe refit on every training and validation negative, unaugmented (D12), measured on the test split by batch (ADR 0016).
  • Its litert_export passing ADR 0020's gates; data and models pushed to dvcnewremote and verified.
  • Deployed on the owner's go and verified on the live site (docs/agents/deploy.md).
  • README's Status, the model card, dvc.yaml's notes, dataset/ood_negatives/README.md and dataset/segmenter_positives.README.md say what is true afterwards.

Out of scope

  • Retraining the segmenter and labelling its new positives (D3): a later ticket.
  • New capture sessions for thin pools and a fresh-bag test set: ML-4. Photos added after 2026-10-19, the owner's next negatives included, wait for the next ticket (D6).
  • Renaming session folders, new classes, a new backbone or head type, moving training onto LiteRT (ML-7 D3).
  • Test-time augmentation (ADR 0006).
  • Upgrading albumentations: every version after 2.0.8 is AlbumentationsX, AGPL-3.0 or commercial (ADR 0018). Revisiting that is its own ticket.
  • Feature-space augmentation (FroFA, arXiv 2403.10519): its gains are few-shot and shrink to +0.1–0.4 points by 25 shots, and it cannot imitate a camera.
  • Fixing the deploy's memory preflight: OPS-10 (R6).
