Ticket ML-32 · coffee-vision · classifier backbone

Distil the frozen DINOv3 ViT-B/16 into a MobileNetV4 student, quality first

The classifier’s backbone takes 5.74 s of a photo’s roughly 10 s on the serving host. This ticket trains a MobileNetV4 to reproduce that backbone’s embedding on bean patches, and measures whether the student keeps the classifier’s and the guard’s results. Where quality and speed pull apart, quality wins: the larger variant, the longer schedule, the teacher run on every view. Written and planned; nothing is implemented, and it ends at the owner’s decision on the evidence, not at a release.

Owner request, 2026-11-16. “create ticket and plan how to distillate DINOv3 into MobileNetV4, prioritise quality of student result”. Earlier, 2026-11-15, on student selection: “The main goal for student selection is performance and speed optimization, not size.”

1. Summary

Today a patch goes through a frozen DINOv3 ViT-B/16, and a logistic-regression head and the OOD probe read its 1536-d cls_mean readout. After this ticket there is a MobileNetV4 student trained to reproduce that readout, with its own head and its own probe fitted on the student’s embeddings, and a paired three-seed comparison with B/16 that says what the student lost and how much time it saves on yappy. The work splits into six parts: timing the untrained variants where they would serve; the distillation code; a pilot at one seed; recipe levers screened on validation; three students at the three seeds with the paired evidence; and the conversion and timing of the best one. Releasing a student is a later ticket.

2. Background

  • The time. On yappy a 12 MP photo takes about 10 s: DINOv3 5.74 s for 40 patches in 5 calls of 8, the segmenter’s encoder 1.78 s, decoding 1.4–1.5 s (analysis/ml7_gpu/README.md §2, docs/status.md).
  • The research. analysis/distillation/research_distillation.md (2026-11-15) is this ticket’s source for every outside claim; its source numbers are given as [Sn].
    • The method the sources support is embedding distillation on the project’s own patches: in-domain data works best, the teacher and the student must see the same augmented view, and the schedule must be long; targets precomputed for one fixed view “work much worse” [S9].
    • Out-of-distribution detection is the property plain feature distillation loses [S12]. The guard reads the embedding (ADR 0016), so the guard is where a student is most likely to fall short.
    • MobileNetV4 is the one family whose paper measures CPU, mobile-GPU and accelerator latency together [S13]. In timm’s tables at 224 px Conv-M has 5.0 times ViT-B/16’s throughput on an RTX 4090 and 10.1 times on a laptop CPU; Conv-L about 2.1 times on the GPU, at 256 px. ImageNet top-1 at 256 px: Conv-M 79.9, Conv-L 82.9 [S6, S13]. Weights are Apache-2.0 [S28].
    • No source gives a time on LiteRT’s WebGPU accelerator. On yappy the one local hint is that the convolution-heavy encoder gained 3.9 times from the GPU against DINOv3’s 3.0.
  • The decision a student supersedes. ADR 0006: the backbone is B/16, frozen, never fine-tuned; the gain over ResNet18 “came from DINO’s self-supervised pretraining, not from architecture or recipe”. Whether a convolutional network can hold that embedding on bean patches is what this ticket measures; no source tests it.
  • Earlier thinking. docs/dinov3_integration_plan.md §13 proposed DINOv3 as a teacher for ResNet18, as a fallback; it was never run. Nothing in .out-of-scope/ closes distillation.
  • The baseline. exp283–285 (ML-27), seeds 42, 123, 7: val 0.9470–0.9649, test 0.9500–0.9731 patch macro-F1. Both are in-distribution and near their ceiling (docs/status.md, “Reading the numbers”).
  • Where it trains. This machine has no GPU and torch is the CPU build (pyproject.toml, docs/development.md); there is no other training machine (docs/agents/remote-sweeps.md). B/16 embeds a patch in about 0.2 s here (ML-27 P2: about 3 h 10 min a seed for 57,200 embeddings).
  • What training costs here (measured 2026-11-16, one ad hoc run: batch 16, 224 px, 6 torch threads, random weights, the machine at a load average of about 7; plan P1 repeats it as a recorded measurement).
    ModelPer viewPer pass over 54,000 views
    B/16 teacher, forward only0.225 s3.4 h
    Conv-M student, training step0.083 s1.2 h
    Conv-L student, training step0.167 s2.5 h
    Hybrid-L student, training step0.203 s3.0 h
    A student embeds faster than B/16 (forward only: Conv-M 0.024 s, Conv-L 0.056 s, Hybrid-L 0.054 s a view), so a head or probe fit on a Conv-L student is about 1 h a seed against B/16’s 3 h 10 min. About 2.9 GB of memory was available during the run.
    • The plan as written does not fit this machine. One Conv-L student at 100 passes with the teacher on every view is about 590 h, some 25 days; the plan holds about 11 such distillations (the pilot, the levers, three seeds).
    • A reduced plan does, in about two weeks of the machine doing nothing else: teacher embeddings stored once for about 540,000 views (about 34 h; one store serves the three seeds, since it is keyed by photo), each Conv-L student trained 30 passes from the store (about 75 h), a pilot and three seed students (10–12 days), no lever screening. With Conv-M the student time halves, about one week. Thirty passes over a finite store is well short of the schedule the sources recommend [S9], so it trades away the quality this ticket puts first.
    • On a GPU the full plan is days, not months: timm lists Conv-L at about 6,000 images a second for inference on an RTX 4090 [S6], against about 18 here. That is a rough guide, not a measurement of training.
  • No ready-made student exists (searched 2026-11-16). No MobileNetV4 distilled from DINOv3 is published: timm’s MobileNetV4 weights are ImageNet-trained, with no DINOv3 teacher (its model card). The published students of DINOv3 are Meta’s own, ViT-S/S+/B/L/H+ and ConvNeXt-T/S/B/L (Meta’s model card); B/16 is itself one of them. Because the backbone here is frozen, any of those is a drop-in that needs a head and a probe refitted and no distillation: ViT-S/16 is on disk (2.7 times B/16’s throughput on a desktop GPU [S6]; test 0.939 against 0.955 in the retired screen), and ConvNeXt-Tiny is a download under the DINOv3 License (1.7 times on a desktop GPU, 3.2 times on a CPU [S6]).

3. Before and after

Now

  • One backbone family in coffeecv/backbones.py: pretrained DINO weights, verified against models_pretrained/manifest.json, never trained here.
  • A checkpoint holds a head and names its backbone’s weights by digest.
  • The only training driver fits a head on frozen embeddings.
  • No measurement of any MobileNetV4 on yappy, and no student of DINOv3.

After

  • A distillation driver that trains a MobileNetV4 against B/16’s cls_mean, recorded like any run.
  • A student backbone the head fit, the probe fit and infer.py load like a frozen one, its weights a tracked artefact with a digest.
  • Three students (seeds 42, 123, 7), each with its head and probe, compared with B/16 pair by pair: macro-F1 per class, the guard’s figures, per-photo answers, agreement with B/16.
  • The best student converted, checked against its own PyTorch model and timed on yappy.
  • The owner’s decision: adopt (a release ticket and an ADR superseding 0006), iterate, or close.

4. What does not change

  • The live model. B/16 serves throughout. Nothing here deploys.
  • The pipeline around the backbone. The segmenter, the crops, patch sizing (4–7 beans), 40 patches at 224 px, softmax averaged per photo, no TTA, the head at C = 0.1, the probe at 0.5.
  • The parity rule. A student is the backbone in training too: its head and probe are fitted on its own embeddings and every reported metric is computed with it. B/16 appears only in the student’s training loss (CODING_STANDARDS.md; the research note §3.9).
  • How evidence is read. Paired, three seeds, per class, the owner decides (ADR 0002). Val selects, test is reported.
  • Weights stay private (ADR 0014): the student’s too, in the DVC remote.

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1A convolutional student cannot hold the embedding. ADR 0006 credits the pretraining, not the architecture, and no source distils DINOv3 into MobileNetV4. The pilot (plan P3) answers it at one seed before the three-seed cost is paid, with the published DINOv3 ViT-S/16 as a reference arm: a student that does not beat S/16 has no case.
R2The guard degrades while macro-F1 holds. [S12]; a student trained on bean patches is constrained only where beans are. The guard’s training and validation negatives are in the transfer set (D5), and the guard’s test-split figures are part of every comparison.
R3The metrics cannot see the loss. Val and test sit near 0.97 and come from the training photos’ bags and cameras. Beside them (D2): the student’s cosine to the teacher on validation patches, per-photo answers through the serving path, and how often its top-1 differs from B/16’s, read against patch sampling’s 0.85% (ADR 0019).
R4Leakage through the transfer set. The photo split is seeded on (seed, class) (dataset.split_photos_by_class), so one seed’s training photos are another seed’s validation and test photos. A student is distilled on its own seed’s training photos only and compared at that seed only (plan Q1); a test refuses anything else.
R5No training hardware for a long schedule. Measured 2026-11-16 (§2): the plan as written is months on this machine, and the version that fits in about two weeks cuts the schedule to 30 passes over stored teacher embeddings. D3 decides the machine; D8 decides whether a published student is tried first.
R6The speed is not there on yappy. Every published ratio is from other hardware. P1 converts and times the untrained variants on the accelerator before anything is trained, with the delegate’s placement report.
R7fp16 drifts in a convolutional graph. The segmenter’s encoder failed its fp16 bar on this GPU because convolutions accumulate in fp16 (ADR 0019). The student’s precision is measured in P1 and P6, and fp32 is the default until fp16 is shown and accepted (plan Q5).

6. Decisions

IDQuestionOptionsOwner decision
D1Which MobileNetV4 is the student? (a) The largest variant P1 shows placing on yappy’s GPU and at least twice as fast there as B/16: Conv-L expected, Hybrid-L if it places. Recommended: quality first. (b) Conv-M, the fastest of the serious ones. (c) Two students, (a)’s and Conv-M, at twice the training cost. Not decided: the ticket closed (D9).
D2What is shown to judge a student, and is there a numeric bar? (a) No numeric bar (ADR 0002). Shown per seed pair: val and test macro-F1 with per-class F1; the guard’s test-split figures; per-photo answers through the serving path; top-1 agreement with B/16; the cosine to the teacher; the time on yappy. Recommended. (b) The same, plus a bar set now, before any number exists. Not decided: the ticket closed (D9).
D3Where does the distillation train? (a) A GPU machine the owner names (rented), the teacher run on every view. Recommended: it is what the quality priority asks for. (b) This machine’s CPU, the reduced plan of §2: a finite store of teacher embeddings per (patch, view), 30 passes a student, no lever screening, about two weeks for Conv-L or one for Conv-M. Not decided: the ticket closed (D9).
D4What are the student’s initial weights? (a) timm’s ImageNet-pretrained MobileNetV4 weights (Apache-2.0), downloaded once, listed in models_pretrained/manifest.json with their digest and tracked by DVC. Recommended. (b) Random initialisation. Not decided: the ticket closed (D9).
D5What is in the transfer set? (a) Patches from the seed’s training-split photos, drawn afresh and not only the head fit’s 600 a class, plus patches from the guard’s training and validation negatives. Recommended. (b) (a) plus a generic unlabelled image set: a lever for P4 if the guard falls, and a download to choose. (c) Bean patches only. Not decided: the ticket closed (D9).
D6Is a student trained on DINOv3’s embeddings treated as a derivative under the DINOv3 License? (a) Yes: its weights stay private like every checkpoint (ADR 0014), which costs nothing. Recommended. The licence does not define “derivative works” (research note §3.9; not legal advice). (b) No. Not decided: the ticket closed (D9).
D7Where does the ticket end? (a) At the owner’s decision on the paired evidence and the converted student’s time on yappy; a release, and the ADR superseding 0006, are a ticket of their own. Recommended. (b) At a release. Not decided: the ticket closed (D9).
D8Is a published DINOv3 student tried before anything is distilled? It costs hours, not weeks (§2). (a) Yes: with P1’s timing, ViT-S/16 fitted at seeds 42, 123 and 7 on the current pool and paired with B/16, the guard’s figures included; distillation goes on only if the owner does not accept its loss, and that loss is then what a MobileNetV4 student has to beat. Recommended. (b) (a), and ConvNeXt-Tiny too: a download under the DINOv3 License. (c) No: ViT-S/16 stays the pilot’s one-seed reference arm, as planned. Not decided: the ticket closed (D9).
D9Does the ticket go on? (a) Go on with the plan. (b) Close: no distillation. (b), 2026-11-20. “Distillation is impossible on my hardware and there is no pre-trained student. So we will use different approaches to speed up things, not a distillation.” The training cost measured here (§2, R5) puts even the reduced plan at weeks of this machine, and no MobileNetV4 distilled from DINOv3 is published. Distillation is recorded as a closed idea in .out-of-scope/distillation.md.

7. Scope and acceptance

  • Each MobileNetV4 variant timed on yappy’s accelerator and on XNNPACK beside B/16, with its placement report, before any training.
  • A distillation driver, proven at toy scale, whose transfer set a test shows free of validation and test photos and of the guard’s test negatives.
  • A pilot student at seed 42 with its head and probe, beside B/16 and the published ViT-S/16.
  • The recipe chosen on validation only, each lever’s effect recorded.
  • Three students at seeds 42, 123 and 7, each compared with its B/16 pair on everything D2 names.
  • The best student converted, held to D7’s fp32 bars against its own PyTorch model, and timed per photo on yappy.
  • The owner’s decision recorded: adopt, iterate or close.

Out of scope

  • A release of a student, and the ADR that would supersede ADR 0006 (D7).
  • The segmenter: EfficientViT-SAM-L0 and any segmenter student (research note §4.2).
  • Other classifier students (LeViT, TinyViT, ConvNeXt, a distilled ViT-S/16). The published ViT-S/16 appears only as the pilot’s reference arm.
  • A student input below 224 px, fewer patches per photo, and a fully convolutional single pass over the crop: speed levers that cost quality, for after a student holds at 224 px.
  • Quantization (analysis/quantization/research_quantization.md) and the Pi 5 host (OPS-23).
  • A generalization test set of new bags and a new phone (docs/status.md): still its own ticket.
