Implementation plan: canonicalization

The order of work for ticket ML-19. Two strands: the training side (the function, the canonical store, pass14, the crop stage, the baseline), which unblocks ML-16's retrain, and the serving side (the front canonicalizes, the host takes canonical photos only), which ships with ML-16's release. The encoder is proved on the three machines first, because every later phase stores its output. The first step that changes a number is P3's re-check of the 7 downscaled photos; the first that changes a model's input for good is P4's store.

Built from a read of the code at 2eb963d and the two research notes in analysis/ml19/, not from the ticket alone. Eight things the ticket does not spell out turned up. They are folded in below, and the ones that need a decision are Q1–Q3 (§2).

  1. Crops are named *__cropped.jpg in four modules. segcrop_session.py, dataset.py (its glob and bean_mask_path), merge_rig.py (and the stems of labels/ml3/pool_exclude.csv it matches) and ood_eval.py. D32's lossless JPEG XL crops become *__cropped.jxl in all four at once (P6); the exclusion list keys by stem and stays.
  2. The fine-tune checks each photo's sha256 against labels.csv (seg_finetune.py). It reads the canonical photo from P6 on, so it checks the original's hash against the record (D33) and reads the canonical file mapped from it; a missing canonical file is an error, not a fallback to the original.
  3. pass13 holds geometry beyond its masks. Include and exclude points, fences, boxes and strokes, as fractions of the unrotated frame (189 rows with exclude points, 286 fences, 168 with strokes). pass14 turns them with the mask (D28, P5).
  4. The front's release lists its files (webapp/deploy/release_manifest.py, FRONT_CODE: the check service, the upload check, its pyproject.toml and lock). The canonicalization module, photo_io.py and the committed Rec.2020 profile join it (P9, D35).
  5. imagecodecs.jpegxl_decode does not report a file's colour encoding (research note). D35's "is canonical" check needs it. P1 finds how to read it; a small reader of the JPEG XL header's colour bundle is the fallback.
  6. libjxl's output may differ between CPUs (an approximate reciprocal instruction, research note), but not between thread counts. So the store is built on one machine, this one, as segcrop is, and D30's 1-level bar is what holds across machines.
  7. The front needs OpenCV for D43's INTER_AREA, and imagecodecs for colour and JPEG XL: its lock gains the photo stack (D35), held to training's versions by D41's check.
  8. Every remote run needs the owner's go (docs/agents/remote-sweeps.md). P1 and P8 run on the front and on yappy, in throwaway environments under /tmp, never in a release's .venv.

1. Phase order and why

P1 proves the one library on all three machines before anything stores its output: a library that fails there would redo every later phase. P2 aligns the locks, which changes nothing in training (its versions are the target, D42) and is checked on serving by the deploy's --compare. P3 writes the function and its tests and is the first to change a number (the 7-photo re-check). P4–P7 move training onto canonical photos and end with the baseline, after which ML-16's retrain can start. P8–P9, the serving side, need only P3 and can run alongside P4–P7. P10 waits for ML-16's models.

5. P3 — The canonicalization function and its tests

StatusDone 2026-11-03 (this commit); two steps moved to P9, below
Blocked byNone

One module, coffeecv/canonical.py, with no torch (the front imports it): original bytes in, canonical JPEG XL bytes out, and a canonical file in, 8-bit sRGB out. photo_io.load_rgb_image becomes the decode of a canonical photo only.

  1. canonicalize(path) -> bytes: decode the main image only (D20) at each decoder's highest depth (D36; RAW by rawpy at 16 bits into Rec.2020, use_camera_wb); upright (D28: HEIC and RAW as decoded, other formats by all eight EXIF orientations, 0 or invalid as stored); over 60,000,000 pixels to at most 50,000,000 by cv2.resize(INTER_AREA) with the size given (D27, D43); transparency over the segmenter's fill grey (D13); its own profile, or sRGB when it has none (D16), into Rec.2020 by LittleCMS on float32 in strips (D31, D38); encoded with D25's settings; no metadata (D21).
  2. The committed Rec.2020 profile, coffeecv/data/rec2020_bt709.icc, its sha256 tested (D46).
  3. is_canonical(bytes) (D35): JPEG XL, D7's colour encoding, no EXIF or XMP, no orientation, no alpha or extra channels, at most 60 MP.
  4. load_rgb_image decodes a canonical photo to 8-bit sRGB through imagecodecs (D7) and refuses anything else; pillow-jxl-plugin leaves training's and the web service's pyproject.toml (D45), and the locks are redone.
  5. D8's tests in the fast tier: synthetic patches (sRGB and P3 primaries, a P3 green outside sRGB, greys, white) through original, canonical and sRGB against independent colour maths in the test; the header (D7's encoding, no orientation); a committed golden file. Also: each of the eight orientations; the cap's two thresholds (60 MP kept, 60 MP plus one pixel downscaled); a transparent pixel; an untagged photo; is_canonical on a canonical file and on near misses.
  6. The one "Uncalibrated" photo checked by hand: Adobe RGB if its interoperability index says R03 (D16).
  7. The 7 photos over 60 MP (D4's measurement) re-run on the live models from their canonical photos, now with INTER_AREA: verdict, top-1 and mask area against analysis/ml19/downscale_answers.json.
  8. Checks: the full suite green; an answer that changes among the 7 stops the phase for the owner (D43).

As done, 2026-11-03. coffeecv/canonical.py (no torch) with canonicalize, load_canonical (refuses anything not canonical), is_canonical and canonical_problems; coffeecv/jxl_header.py (the header reader, finding 5) and coffeecv/jxl_encode.py (libjxl's chunked encoder, D52: the same bytes as jpegxl_encode, half its memory); the profile in coffeecv/data/; imagecodecs' version checked at import. Changed from the steps above:

  • load_rgb_image and pillow-jxl-plugin move to P9. Turning load_rgb_image canonical-only here would break the 38 files that call it, which read originals until P6 (training) and P9 (serving); the plugin is what they read JPEG XL with until then. P9 makes load_rgb_image canonical-only and drops the plugin once the last caller has moved.
  • A pixel ceiling before decoding: canonicalize(data, name, max_pixels) refuses a photo over it from its header (TooLarge), for D38's front; Pillow's bomb limit maps to the same.
  • Found by D8's tests: LittleCMS alone round-trips every patch to the level, but q95 moves a saturated colour's near-zero channel by up to 9 levels in 8-bit sRGB (0.003 in linear light; sRGB green reads back (9, 255, 0)), greys exact. The tests hold the conversion to the maths and the whole path in linear light.
  • The 7 photos over 60 MP from their canonical photos (upright, colour-managed, INTER_AREA): 7 of 7 the same verdict and top-1, mask area within 0.006, top-1 probability moved by up to 0.19 (analysis/ml19/p3/seven_compare.txt). No stop (D43).
  • The "Uncalibrated" photo (confusable_grain_010.jpg) has no interoperability index, so it is read as sRGB (D16).
