The order of work for ticket ML-9. Two strands: the segmenter (labels, fine-tune, comparison, re-crop) and then the classifier (three seeds, probe, export, deploy). Built from a read of the code at db0e284, not from the ticket alone. Three things the ticket does not cover turned up. They are folded in below, and the ones that need a decision are Q1–Q3 (§2).

  1. 23 photos need a label, not 3. pass3 (2026-10-12) predates ML-8's appends: 18 of their training and validation positives were never labelled, beside the 3 of 2026-10-21 and pass3's 2 drops. P1.
  2. Naming the new decoder would stale every crop. seg_predict takes only names in segment_beans.NAMED_SEGMENTERS, and segment_beans.py is a dep of all 17 segcrop stages: registering xl0_v4 there stales them all (about 2 h of re-crop) even if XL0-v3 stays. segment_beans.named_params already reads WEIGHTS:DECODER; P2 lets seg_predict take it, and the name is registered only in P5, if adopted.
  3. Roast state is not recorded per photo (ML-3 D4), so "per roast state" cannot be reported. Every photo of 2026-10-21__new_green is green: its test photos are reported on their own. The ticket's wording follows.

1. Phase order and why

The owner's label session needs only the dataset as appended, so it opens first, and P2's stages are built and tried at toy scale meanwhile. P3 trains (about 7 h) and P4 judges it blind, the owner's choice deciding whether P5 re-crops. The classifier trains once, on the crops of the segmenter chosen, so its numbers are read only once.
