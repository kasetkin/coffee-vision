1. Phase order and why

The owner's label session needs only the dataset as appended, so it opens first, and P2's stages are built and tried at toy scale meanwhile. P3 trains (about 7 h) and P4 judges it blind, the owner's choice deciding whether P5 re-crops. The classifier trains once, on the crops of the segmenter chosen, so its numbers are read only once.
