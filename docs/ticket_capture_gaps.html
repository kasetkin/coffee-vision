1. Summary

Two gaps in what the photos cover. Coverage: the iPhone pool is the thinnest, and the newest farm regions rest on a handful of photos. Independence: val and test come from the same bags as train, so every number is in-distribution (ML-1 R2). Each gap is either filled by a capture session or accepted.

  • Thin farm regions. Brazil, Sul de Minas (class_013) has 7 photos and Colombia, Antioquia Excelso (class_014) 12, all from one session. The live model's known weak spot is roasted Colombia Excelso drifting to Peru or Rwanda (model card models/allrigs_dino3b16_seg_country_s123.json).
  • The split. With photos pooled per class (ADR 0010), a thin pool can leave a split cell empty; python -m coffeecv.split_report lists them, and cam_iphone was starved at some seeds.
  • Fresh scoop (reorg plan §2e): every photo shows the same physical bags used in training. Bag-to-bag drift (roast, moisture) is the largest axis never measured, and the closest thing to what the live site meets.

3. Before and after

Now

  • cam_iphone has no Indonesia, Peru, Rwanda or Kochere photos.
  • Two farm regions under 15 photos.
  • No photo from a newly bought bag; every number is in-distribution.

After

  • The gaps the owner chooses to fill are filled, the rest accepted in D1–D2.
  • A held-out fresh-bag session measures the model on new bags, if D3 says so.

4. What does not change

  • The class list. New photos join existing countries; a new country is a separate ticket.
  • How photos enter. Stripped of private metadata before dvc add (ADR 0013), cropped by the segmenter, judged as in ADR 0002.

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1A fresh-bag session leaks into training. Then it measures nothing. Keep it out of the training pools and out of the segmenter's lists, like the OOD holdout.
R2New photos shift the split. Adding photos re-draws the pooled split, so results before and after are not paired; compare as ML-3 did, with an unpaired reference.
