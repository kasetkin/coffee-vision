1. Summary

Since ML-8 training draws from one pool of every session's crops; a camera no longer has a pool, so a camera missing a country is not a gap in training. What is left: two farm regions with few photos, and no photo from a new bag of a coffee already in the dataset. Neither was filled or accepted here.

2. Background

Photos in the pool per class folder, from python -m coffeecv.coverage_report on 2026-10-20 (after labels/ml3/pool_exclude.csv): 80–150 for twelve of the fourteen folders, and

  • Brazil, Sul de Minas (class_013): 10 photos, all from random_date_raccoon (ML-8's new photos); 12 before the exclusions.
  • Colombia, Antioquia Excelso (class_014): 19 photos, all from random_date_raccoon; 27 before the exclusions.
  • Per country (what the model predicts) every country has 90 or more, since both thin folders share a country with full ones (Brazil 200, Colombia 109).
  • Fresh bag (reorg plan §2e): every photo shows the same physical bags used in training, and the split is by single photo (ML-8 R2), so val and test say nothing about a new bag. Bag-to-bag drift (roast, moisture) is the largest axis never measured, and the closest thing to what the live site meets.

4. What does not change

  • How photos enter. Stripped of private metadata before dvc add (ADR 0013), cropped by the segmenter, merged into the pool.
  • A fresh-bag session's rule, if one is shot. It stays out of the pool and out of the segmenter's lists, like the OOD holdout, or it measures nothing; and new photos re-draw the pooled split, so results before and after are not paired.
