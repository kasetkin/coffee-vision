2. Background

The JPEGs. All 15 have a Display P3-gamut ICC profile with the sRGB curve, as the earlier Samsung photos do, and EXIF orientation. Sizes: 4000×3000 (8), 4080×3060 (4), 5352×4016, 5712×4284, and one 200 MP 16320×12240 (which libultrahdr's 8192 limit does not reach; canonicalization caps it at 50 MP, ML-19 D27). Ten carry a gain map in both forms, ISO 21496-1 and Ultra HDR, 1000×750, one channel, map max 2.3 stops (as the 50 earlier Samsung files); five carry none. Each ends in Samsung's SEF trailer: Image_UTC_Data, MCC_Data (the network's country), Camera_Scene_Info, Color_Display_P3, Photo_HDR_Info, Camera_Capture_Mode_Info.

What the strip removes (strip_metadata --dry-run, 2026-11-15): from the 15 JPEGs, GPS (60 tags), Samsung's maker notes (158) and an unknown maker note (60), the embedded thumbnail (IFD1), and the SEF trailer, as for every Samsung batch; every check passes on a copy. The 5 DNGs fail: the strip's pixel check decodes through rawpy, which cannot open them, and a photo whose pixels cannot be compared is never stripped.

The duplicate check (find_duplicates, threshold 0.97, the 15 JPEGs against class 013's other 131 readable photos): no copy; the nearest is 0.62. The DNGs could not be checked; each is the twin of the JPEG of its name (D1).

The DNGs. One read in full (20261115_175038, 25 MB; the others 24 to 41 MB): DNG 1.7 (backward version 1.1); the main image 4080×3060, three channels, 16-bit, PhotometricInterpretation LinearRaw (demosaiced, the merged picture of Samsung's processing, not sensor data), compression 52546, JPEG XL; orientation 6; profile "Embedded Galaxy RAW"; two illuminants (D50 and standard light A) with colour and forward matrices; AsShotNeutral; BaselineExposure +3.5: the data sits 3.5 stops below the picture, the highlights kept; an OpcodeList2 of flat GainMap opcodes (gain 1.0: lens shading already applied); a ProfileGainTableMap, the local tone map ML-29 D18 leaves unapplied. LibRaw 0.22.1 (rawpy 0.27.1) answers LibRawFileUnsupportedError for all five.

They can be read. imagecodecs decodes the JPEG XL main image. Developed by hand (camera white balance, the forward matrix to XYZ, Rec.2020; tmp/ml29_new_photos/develop.py, crops/): with LibRaw's auto-bright, as canonicalization develops every other RAW (ML-19), it is brighter than Samsung's JPEG; at +3.5 EV it is close to it; with the ProfileGainTableMap as well, darker.

Twins. Each DNG has a JPEG of the same stem; segcrop writes <stem>__cropped.jxl, so the second crop of a pair would overwrite the first (ML-25 R2). ML-28 D5 renamed the developed twin: 20261115_174805_… → 20261115_174805-jpg_….

5. Risks

IDRiskEvidence and mitigation
R1A hand-written development differs from LibRaw's for the same kind of file. LibRaw cannot read these, so there is nothing to equal; the risk is a brightness or colour unlike the dataset's other RAWs. Mitigation for D1 (a): follow LibRaw's steps for a LinearRaw DNG (white balance, the DNG colour matrices, auto-bright), and report the crops against the JPEG twins for the owner.
R2A DNG held outside the dataset keeps its GPS on disk. Held in tmp/ (gitignored, never in DVC): nothing is published; stripped when a decoder reads it.
