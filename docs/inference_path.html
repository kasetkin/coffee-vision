What happens between an uploaded photo and the answer it gets

Traced from webapp/app.py's POST /analyze, which runs the two steps of coffeecv/infer.py:classify_one, segment_decoded and classify_segmented, with the preview sent in between (ticket OPS-7, ADR 0023). The CLI runs classify_one itself, so the two callers share one code path. Logging and JSON shaping are left out; everything from the upload to the two streamed parts is included.

model: dino3b16_segxl0v3_country_aug8_s123 segmenter: EfficientViT-SAM XL0 + xl0_v3 decoder classifier: DINOv3 ViT-B/16, frozen, + linear head runtime: LiteRT on the host's GPU, no torch as of 2026-10-21
On "votes": there is one frozen DINOv3 backbone and one linear head, no ensemble. What plays the role of voting is averaging: 40 patches cut from the photo's bean region, each scored once, their softmax probabilities mean-averaged into one vector over the 10 countries. The answer ranks all 10 by that vector.

Four mechanisms worth reading in full

The rest of the pipeline is fairly linear; these four points are where a decision actually gets made.

1

One request, two parts

webapp/app.py · analyze(), _analyze_stream()

The page uploads the photo once and reads the answer as application/x-ndjson, one JSON object per line (ticket OPS-7, ADR 0023). The photo is decoded and segmented before the response starts, so a photo that cannot be decoded is still a plain 400 and a segmenter error the error handler's 500. Then the 200 goes out with the preview: a JPEG thumbnail (so the browser never has to decode HEIC or RAW itself), the whole photo's bean mask as a 1-bit PNG, the crop box as fractions of the photo, and whether the D18 fallback fired. The classification follows as the second line. A failure after the 200 cannot change the status, so it becomes an error part; a photo whose pitch cannot be measured becomes a refused_unreadable classification part. "Use full photo instead" on the page re-sends the photo with skip_crop=1: the segmenter does not run, the whole photo is classified, and the preview has no mask and no box. The server never takes a box from the client.

photo = load_rgb_image(path)                   # 400 if it cannot be read
seg = segment_decoded(photo, cfg, skip_crop=skip_crop, segmenter=segmenter)
yield json.dumps(_preview_part(seg)) + "\n"     # part 1
entry = classify_segmented(seg, cfg, class_ids, class_labels, classifier, ref,
                           n_patches=40, tta=TTA, probe=probe)
yield json.dumps({"part": "classification", **body}) + "\n"   # part 2
2

Bean region and bean pitch — patches measured in beans, on the mask

coffeecv/infer.py · segment_decoded(), sample_patches()

The segmenter is the one the model's training crops were cut with (XL0-v3, ticket ML-6), named and sha-pinned by the model's own config through seg_params(cfg); a mismatch fails the worker's boot, not a request. It sees the photo at 1024 px on its long side, prompted with one box over the whole photo, and its mask is scaled back to the full photo. Non-bean pixels in the crop are filled with ImageNet's mean colour, and the crop keeps at least 95% of the bean pixels (D3, D4). A mask covering less than 6.45% of the photo is treated as no mask: the whole photo is classified, unfilled, every patch accepted (D18).

Bean pitch is then measured on the crop by FFT, not assumed from a zoom level, so each patch is 4–7 beans on a side at any distance, exactly as in training. A patch with less than 80% bean region under the mask is redrawn; after 20 × 40 attempts the best rejected boxes top the photo up to 40 (D17). There is no scale refusal: beans_across, mask_area_frac, bean_frac_in_crop, retained_frac, seg_fallback and patches_below_share are logged on every request so a future guard can be calibrated on real traffic (docs/scale_guard_plan.md).

pitch = estimate_bean_pitch(grayscale_like_training(rgb), **pitch_kwargs(cfg))
boxes, clamped, below = sample_masked_bean_unit_boxes(
    rng, region, n_patches, pitch, cfg.patch_beans_min, cfg.patch_beans_max, mask,
    cfg.patch_min_bean_share, cfg.patch_max_attempts_factor, 0.0)   # no rotation at inference
diag["beans_across"] = room / pitch   # logged, never gated on
3

The "vote", on LiteRT

coffeecv/litert.py · LiteRtClassifier; coffeecv/infer.py · classify_segmented()

The service runs the model's LiteRT conversion (models/<name>.litert/), not the PyTorch checkpoint, and imports no torch (ticket ML-7). Its manifest names each graph's accelerator and precision: the segmenter's encoder and decoder on the GPU at fp32, DINOv3 on the GPU at fp16 with a batch of 8 (ADR 0019). A conversion serves only after it matches PyTorch's answers within the D7 bar (ADR 0020), and the manifest must name the checkpoint the service was pointed at, or the boot stops. The 40 patches go through DINOv3 in 5 calls; the linear head and softmax run in numpy. The 40 softmax vectors are mean-averaged and all 10 countries ranked by the result. TTA is off: the model's card does not ask for it, and the service refuses to boot a model whose card does.

Before the first request each worker runs a fixed photo through this whole path (ADR 0021), so no visitor pays for graph setup; on the GPU the warm-up also has to show the GPU ran it, or the worker does not serve (ADR 0020).

probs, embeds = classifier.probs_and_embeddings(x, tta=False)   # 5 calls of 8
mean = probs.mean(axis=0)              # the final "vote"
ranked = sorted(zip(class_ids, mean), key=lambda t: -t[1])
4

Out-of-distribution guard — the linear probe at 0.5

coffeecv/infer.py · probe_score(), classify_segmented()

Softmax confidence alone was found to be useless here (0.97 on a genuinely out-of-rig photo, indistinguishable from 0.997 on an in-distribution one), so the guard asks a different question: does each patch's embedding look like a bean patch? A logistic probe, fitted in this model's own 1536-d space on genuine photos against non-bean negatives (ADR 0005), gives P(not beans) per patch; the mean over the 40 patches is the photo's score. It refuses above its own decision boundary, 0.5, fixed in code (PROBE_THRESHOLD, ADR 0016) rather than read from the probe file. The live probe carries no conformal thresholds, so it has no warn band: the live model's answer is either "predicted" or refused, never "warned". On a refusal the ranked list is computed but not sent.

The centroid distance of the older guard is still computed and logged on every request, so the long-run log stays comparable; it decides only for a checkpoint without a probe file (refusing above 1.4). The live model's probe caught 160/160 test negatives and refused 0/73 bean photos (docs/status.md); docs/ood_guard_eval.md and docs/dinov3_integration_plan.md §9.3 have the guard's history.

def probe_score(embeddings, probe):
    z = ((embeddings - mu) / sd) @ w[:-1] + w[-1]   # per patch
    return mean(sigmoid(z))                      # P(not beans), per photo

if score > PROBE_THRESHOLD:                     # 0.5
    verdict = "REFUSED (out of distribution)"      # no ranked list sent
