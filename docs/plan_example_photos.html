Built from a read of the code at 3c008a8, not from the ticket alone. Five things the ticket does not cover turned up. They are folded in below, and the ones that need a decision are Q1–Q3 (§2).

  1. The front's nginx needs no new route. Its root is the release's webapp/static and location / serves any file there; a path the release has no file for is a 404. The examples only have to be in the front's release: STATIC in webapp/deploy/release_manifest.py, which feeds FRONT only, so they never cross to the inference host. Folded into P2.
  2. fixtures.txt has exactly three columns. release_probe.read_fixtures splits each line into role, sha256 and path, and the deploy's fixtures() reads $3; an expected answer cannot go there as a fourth. Q1.
  3. A failed --verify rolls a full deploy back (on_verify_failure → revert_both, scripts/deploy_webapp.sh). So D11's "fails" means a wrong example reverts the release, as a wrong smoke top-1 does today. Q2.
  4. send_fixtures sends every fixture to the inference host, over its slow link (all five examples: about 10.8 MB, about 45 s on the first deploy). --verify posts them from the deploying machine, through the domain, so the host never needs them. Folded into P5: the example role is not sent.
  5. The JPEG XL encoder writes the image's EXIF back unless told not to (pillow-jxl-plugin's _save calls im.getexif() when no exif is given). Folded into P1: encode with exif=b"" after ImageOps.exif_transpose, so the file carries no metadata and no orientation tag.

Working in parallel with ML-16. A second session takes this plan; the ML-16 session keeps the data.

5. P3 — The 10 MB cap

StatusDone 2026-11-03, 502a112 on ops16-example-photos
Blocked byNone

D8. Every place the 5 MB cap is, read at 3c008a8 (grep -rn "5 MB\|5184\|MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES").

  1. webapp/front/check_service.py: MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES 10 MB and its comments; INFERENCE_TIMEOUT_S 60 → 120 (it bounds the connect, the whole upload and each read of the answer: a 10 MB photo crosses in about 40 s at 2.1 Mbit/s). Done as two: one constant at 120 s would give each read of the answer 120 s too, up to 240 s before its first byte, past nginx's 190 s. So UPLOAD_TIMEOUT_S = 120 bounds the connect and the whole upload, and ANSWER_TIMEOUT_S = 60 each read after it: 120 + 60 s, as the next line assumes. A test holds nginx's wait above their sum.
  2. webapp/deploy/coffee-cv-front.nginx.conf.template: client_max_body_size 5184k → 10304k (10 MB + the 64 KB FORM_ALLOWANCE), proxy_read_timeout 130s → 190s (the check service's longest wait before the answer's first byte goes from 60 + 60 s to 120 + 60 s, and nginx outwaits it), the 413 message, the comments.
  3. webapp/static/index.html: MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES, and the comment "about 20 s for 5 MB".
  4. The docs: webapp/static/llms.txt (three places), webapp/static/openapi.json (two), webapp/README.md (two), docs/inference_path.html, ADR 0031's consequence line ("Until then the front caps uploads at 5 MB", now 10 MB with OPS-16 D8 as its source).
  5. The tests: tests/test_front_check_service.py (the exactly-5 MB photos and the "5 MB" messages), tests/test_front_site.py, tests/test_agent_files.py (holds the page's cap equal to the check service's). tests/test_inference_site.py reads app.py's 20 MB, which stays.
  6. The check service's peak memory with a 10 MB upload in flight, measured as OPS-15's plan P1 did (40 MB at 5 MB), written in the ticket's Activity. MemoryMax=100M (webapp/deploy/coffee-cv-front.service.template, and its comment) rises only past about 70 MB. Measured: 65.5 MB VmHWM at 10 MB (44.6 MB at 5 MB, same machine); it stays.
  7. Checks: the full suite; a 10 MB photo passes the front and a 10 MB + 1 byte one gets the 413.
