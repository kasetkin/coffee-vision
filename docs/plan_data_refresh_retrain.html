Four strands: the data's bookkeeping (no camera column, no tags), a strict augmentation builder, the crops (one re-crop, one pool), and the classifier (augmented head fit, probe, LiteRT, deploy). Built from a read of the code at 81e1ac0 plus the uncommitted ingest of 2026-10-18, not from the ticket alone. Five things the ticket does not cover turned up. They are folded in below, and the ones that need a decision are Q1–Q3 (§2).

  1. An unknown augmentation argument is silently ignored. In albumentations 2.0.8, ColorJitter(brightness_range=(0.5, 0.6)), the 2.2 docs' spelling, only warns "Argument(s) 'brightness_range' are not valid" and runs with the default brightness=(0.8, 1.2). seg_finetune._transform catches exceptions, not warnings. The live seg_ft.augment builds with no such warning (all 9 transforms checked 2026-10-19), so XL0-v3 trained as configured. Folded into P2.
  2. 2.0.8 is the last release of albumentations. PyPI has nothing after it (2025-05-27, MIT). Every later version, 2.2 included, is the package albumentationsx (2.5.1, 2026-10-11), AGPL-3.0-only or commercial, which "collects usage statistics by default" and installs under the same import name. ADR 0018 rejected it; this plan stays on 2.0.8, where every transform it uses exists (PhotoMetricDistort, which the online guide names, does not).
  3. Patches are held in RAM already. MultiPhotoPatchDataset keeps every patch as a 448×448×3 uint8 array (602 KB), and fit_one_seed builds and embeds one split at a time. At D8's 600 training patches per class that is about 3.6 GB for the training split; augmented views can be drawn from those arrays batch by batch, so only their embeddings (6 KB each) accumulate: 9 × 6,000 × 6 KB, about 330 MB. Folded into P6.
  4. The run and model names say allrigs. fit_frozen_head archives runs as allrigs_dino3b16_frozen_clsmean_…, and the shipped models carry the same prefix, a rig word D2 retires. Q1.
  5. Dropping the tags can touch the segmenter dataset's groups. seg_dataset.groups chains shots within 120 s per subject, and a negative's subject is its (batch, tag). With no tag, two tags of one batch shot within 120 s of each other would join one group. P3 measures it against the committed file before writing.

A data-science review of 2026-10-19, checked against the code and against albumentations 2.0.8 on this host, found four more. Each is folded in below; none changes a ticket decision.

  1. The paired print changed two things at once. The head is sklearn LogisticRegression, whose loss is C times the sum over rows, so 9× the rows at the same C = 0.1 is about 9× weaker regularisation. The augmented arm now fits with a sample weight of 1/9, so both arms carry the same total weight and augmentation is the only difference (ADR 0002, docs/agents/experiments.md). P6, P7.
  2. Affine(scale=(0.9, 1.1)) filled its borders with black. The 2.0.8 defaults are border_mode=BORDER_CONSTANT and fill=0; at scale 0.9 the corner pixel came out [0, 0, 0], the filled corners this plan leaves rotation out for. It now reflects (BORDER_REFLECT_101). CoarseDropout fills with the ImageNet mean, as the ResNet-era random erasing did, instead of black. P6.
  3. Most of the camera-pipeline family did not reach the model. Patches are stored at 448 px and the model sees 224 (patch_resize). Downscale(0.5–0.9) at 448 never drops below 224, so the resize undoes it; what survived was the default INTER_NEAREST aliasing, which no phone makes. Blur and noise were halved by the 2× resize. The family now runs on the patch already resized to 224, so its strengths are in the model's input pixels, and Downscale uses area down and linear up. P6.
  4. P5 missed what reads the camera pools. coffeecv_dino/reference.py's REFERENCE_RIG is data/segcropped/cam_iphone, and the DINOv3 parity and real-crop tests are skipUnless those crops exist: once cam_iphone goes they skip silently and check.sh stays green. The fixture regeneration that docs/agents/experiments.md requires after a re-crop was in no phase. P5.

1. Phase order and why

P1 commits what is already done. P2 and P3 change code and records only, each proved against today's outputs: the segmenter's pipeline builds identically through the strict builder, and the segmenter dataset rebuilds equal without the camera column and the tags. P4 re-crops and is compared with ML-6's crops before anything trains (R3). P5 merges one pool. P6 adds augmentation to the head fit, tried on a smoke subset. P7 is the first step that changes a number the owner reads: the classifier at three seeds. P8 converts and deploys.

8. P6 — augmentation in the head fit

StatusDone 2026-10-19: the commit ML-8 P6; full size measured: peak RSS 5.5 GB of about 7 GB available, both fits 4.4 min, about 2.8 h per seed (Activity)
Blocked byP2, P5

Ticket D4. params.yaml gains head_augment: views: 8 and the pipeline below, built by P2's builder. For the training split, fit_frozen_head embeds each patch as stored and 8 augmented views of it, drawn from the in-memory array in batches, seeded by (run seed, patch index, view), and keeps only the embeddings. Validation and test go through the eval transform alone. The same run also fits a head on the originals alone, the unaugmented arm of ADR 0002's print.

Both arms fit at C = 0.1 with the same total sample weight: each of the 9 rows of a training patch (the original and 8 views) weighs 1/9 in the augmented arm, 1 in the unaugmented one. Without it the augmented arm would also be regularised about 9× less (sklearn's loss is C times the sum over rows), and the print could not tell augmentation from the change in C (finding 6). Each arm's diagnostic val-picked C is recorded beside it.

Geometry, occlusion and colour act on the stored 448 px patch. The camera-pipeline family acts on the patch after the resize to 224 that the eval transform makes, so its strengths are in the model's input pixels (finding 8); the eval transform then leaves the size as it is.

FamilyTransform, albumentations 2.0.8Why
GeometrySquareSymmetry, p 1Beans have no "up"; the 8 flips and quarter turns are exact.
ScaleAffine(scale=(0.9, 1.1), border_mode=cv2.BORDER_REFLECT_101), no rotation, p 0.5Patch size follows the measured bean pitch; tighter than the guide's 0.8–1.2. Reflected, not filled: the default fills a shrunk patch's border with black (finding 7).
OcclusionCoarseDropout(num_holes_range=(1, 8), hole_height_range=(0.05, 0.15), hole_width_range=(0.05, 0.15), fill=(124, 116, 104)), p 0.5"High impact" in the library's guide. Filled with the ImageNet mean, as the ResNet-era random erasing was. That paired evidence was on a fine-tuned ResNet18 and does not carry over to a frozen DINOv3 head; here it is adopted under D4, not on evidence.
ColourColorJitter(brightness=0.2, contrast=0.2, saturation=0.2, hue=0), p 0.8The guide's ImageNet example, hue at 0: colour carries origin and roast. Brightness, saturation and white balance still move roast colour; P7's per-class F1 is where a cost would show.
White balancePlanckianJitter, p 0.3The guide's phone-upload case.
Camera pipeline, at 224 pxImageCompression(quality_range=(60, 95)) p 0.3; OneOf[GaussianBlur(blur_limit=(3, 5)), MotionBlur, Defocus] p 0.2; GaussNoise(std_range=(0.01, 0.04)) p 0.2; Downscale(scale_range=(0.5, 0.9), interpolation_pair={downscale: INTER_AREA, upscale: INTER_LINEAR}) p 0.2Other phones, compression, light. At 224 the downscale really loses resolution, and area/linear interpolation does not add the default's nearest-neighbour aliasing (finding 8).

Left out: ToGray, ChannelDropout and hue shifts (they remove the colour signal), rotation by any angle (filled corners no real patch has), RandomResizedCrop (the patch draw already places crops at random).

  1. head_augment in params.yaml and RunConfig; the run's config.json records the pipeline, views and the albumentations version, and the model card carries them.
  2. fit_frozen_head: the augmented embedding of the training split; nothing augmented written; both heads fitted and both archived in the run's metrics (augmented as the run's head, unaugmented beside it).
  3. The ResNet-era knobs (color_jitter_strength, brightness_jitter_strength, illum_gradient_strength, zoom, patch scale) stay for run_all_rigs only; the frozen path refuses them non-zero.
  4. A smoke fit (--smoke) on one seed: views drawn and embedded, ms per view measured, the estimate for P7 written down. Expected: about 2.3 h of embedding per seed (6,000 × 9 views at 146.5 ms, plus val and test), about 7 h for three. The smoke also prints, per transform applied alone, the mean cosine between a view's embedding and its original's: a transform that barely moves the embedding costs a backbone pass and buys nothing, and is reported to the owner before P7.
  5. Peak RAM at full size, which the smoke's 6 training patches per class cannot show: hold one full training split (about 6,000 patches, 3.6 GB), embed its 9 rows per patch and fit both heads, including the val-picked C diagnostic on about 54,000 rows, whose float64 copies are about 660 MB each. Measure peak RSS and the time of both fits against about 7 GB available, before the three-seed run.
  6. ADR 0025: a frozen head fit augments its training patches per run, in memory, through the strict builder, and stores nothing augmented. The ticket's D4 links it.
  7. Tests: the seeded view stream repeats for one seed and differs across seeds; val and test are never augmented; the augmented arm's total sample weight equals the unaugmented arm's; a shrunk Affine view has no constant-filled border; a smoke fit leaves no file outside the run's archive.

Explicitly not in this change

  • Everything in the ticket's "Out of scope".
  • Upgrading albumentations: 2.0.8 is its last MIT release, and every later version is AlbumentationsX under AGPL-3.0 or a commercial licence, with usage statistics on by default (finding 2, ADR 0018).
  • Feature-space augmentation (FroFA, arXiv 2403.10519): its gains are few-shot and shrink to +0.1–0.4 points by 25 shots, and it cannot imitate a camera.
