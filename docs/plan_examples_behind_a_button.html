Built from a read of the code at d78a684, not from the ticket alone. Five things the ticket does not cover turned up. They are folded in below. None of them needs a decision.

  1. hidden does not hide the chips. .formats-hint sets display: flex, which wins over the hidden attribute's user-agent display: none. The folded list needs a rule of its own, .formats-hint[hidden]. Folded into P1.
  2. The example list can arrive after the first pick. loadExamples() runs on load and unhides the row when examples.json is in. On a slow line a visitor may pick a photo first, and the row would then open under the folded line. Once a pick has happened, a late list shows the "Examples" button and leaves the row folded. Folded into P1.
  3. The fold belongs to the two pick handlers, not to choose(). An example reaches choose() again once it has downloaded. Folding there would close a list the visitor opened during the download, and choose() also runs, and clears the photo, for a change with no file. So the fold sits in the file inputs' change handler, only when a file came (D10), and at the top of chooseExample() (D2, D10). "Use full photo instead" calls analyze() directly and folds nothing. Folded into P1.
  4. The refusal's words are not a constant. The front (webapp/front/check_service.py, twice) and the app (webapp/app.py) each write "could not read this as an image: ..." inline. The page's D6 constant is held to all three in tests/test_agent_files.py, as its OFFLINE_ERROR is. The front and the app are not changed (the ticket's "What does not change"). Folded into P1.
  5. The page ships only with a full release. As for OPS-19, the front's files are built per release, so P2 is a full two-host deploy of the live model. Photo answers stop for under 2 minutes (ADR 0026). Folded into P2.
