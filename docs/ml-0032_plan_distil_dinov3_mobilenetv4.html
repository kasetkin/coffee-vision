Implementation plan: distil DINOv3 into MobileNetV4

The order of work for ticket ML-32. One strand, cheapest proof first: time the untrained variants where they would serve, build the distillation code, pilot one student at seed 42, screen the recipe on validation, then train three students and compare each with its B/16 pair, and convert the best. Quality decides every tie: the larger variant, the teacher on every view, the longer schedule. The first step that changes a number is P3’s pilot.

Built from a read of the code at 8b862d3, not from the ticket alone. Five things the ticket does not cover turned up. They are folded in below, and the ones that need a decision are Q1–Q6 (§2).

  1. The photo split differs by seed. dataset.split_photos_by_class shuffles on (seed, class), so seed 42’s training photos include seed 123’s validation and test photos. One student cannot be scored at three seeds; each seed needs its own student, distilled on that seed’s training photos (Q1, P5). A photo is in all three training splits with probability about 0.34, too few to distil one shared student on.
  2. A checkpoint holds no backbone. dino_classifier.save_frozen_checkpoint stores the head and the digest of a file under models_pretrained/; is_frozen_model accepts the dinov3 family only; backbones.features reads CLS and patch tokens. A student needs a home for trained backbone weights, a family and a readout of its own (Q2, Q3, P2).
  3. The export is written for DINOv3. litert_forms.DinoReadout and the litert_export stage wrap FrozenBackbone.features with rewrites made for a ViT; a convolutional graph needs its own entry, its own range scan (ADR 0022) and its precision decided again (ADR 0019) (Q5, P1, P6).
  4. No training hardware. torch is the CPU build and the machine has 12 threads and 13 GB of memory. Measured ad hoc on 2026-11-16 (the ticket’s §2): the teacher 0.225 s a view, a Conv-L training step 0.167 s, so a 100-pass student with the teacher on line is about 25 days here and the plan as written is months. P1 repeats the measurement as a record; the ticket’s D3 decides the machine, and on this one P3–P5 shrink to the ticket’s reduced plan (a stored teacher, 30 passes, P4 dropped).
  5. The pool is moving. ML-31’s intake is in progress (8b862d3). A student and its B/16 pair must read the same pool; exp283–285 are the baseline only while the pool is theirs (Q6).

1. Phase order and why

P1 trains nothing and can end the ticket: if no variant places on yappy’s GPU well ahead of B/16, there is nothing to distil for. P2 is code, proven at toy scale. P3 is the first number, at one seed, and answers the ticket’s R1 before the three-seed cost. P4 spends training time only on validation. P5 is the evidence under ADR 0002. P6 is the only phase that says what the site would gain.

3. P1 — Time the untrained variants, and a training step stop

StatusOpen not done, 2026-11-20: the ticket closed (D9)
Blocked byNone

Weights do not change a graph’s time, so this needs no training, no download and no licence decision. It replaces every published ratio with a number from the host that serves, and it is the proof ADR 0020 asks of any graph: the delegate’s own report of where each op ran. Scripts and records go to analysis/ml32/, in the shape of analysis/quantization/gpu_quantized_ops.{py,sh}.

  1. Read docs/agents/deploy.md and analysis/ml7_gpu/README.md for how a graph is timed on yappy beside the live worker (memory headroom, the shared power limit), and agree the time of day with the owner.
  2. Convert, in convert/.venv, at batch 8 and 224 px, with random weights: mobilenetv4_conv_medium, mobilenetv4_conv_large, mobilenetv4_hybrid_medium, mobilenetv4_hybrid_large, each ending at its pooled feature. Record per graph: whether it converts, its op list, and whether layout transposes remain.
  3. On yappy: each graph on the WebGPU accelerator in fp32 and fp16 and on XNNPACK, beside the live model’s dino_b8 graph in the same session: the placement report, the time for 5 calls of 8, the MemAvailable drop, and for fp16 any NaN or Inf and the cosine to the fp32 output on real patches.
  4. On this machine: the time of one training step (forward, backward, update) for Conv-M and Conv-L at 224 px and batch 32, and of the B/16 teacher on the same batch; from them, the hours per pass over 54,000 views with the teacher on line and from a store.
  5. Checks: a table in analysis/ml32/README.md with every cell filled or marked failed with its error; the records committed.
  6. If D8 is (a) or (b): the published student fitted at seeds 42, 123 and 7 by fit_frozen_head on the current pool (Q6), with its probe and ood_eval, and paired with B/16 in analysis/ml32/published_students.txt; its untrained graph timed with the others.
  7. Stop for the owner: the variant (D1), the machine (D3), and D2, D4, D5; with D8, whether the published student is accepted in place of a distillation. If no variant is at least twice as fast as B/16 on the accelerator, whether the ticket goes on.

4. P2 — The student backbone and the distillation driver

StatusOpen not done, 2026-11-20: the ticket closed (D9)
Blocked byP1, D1, D3, D4, D5, Q3

Two pieces, written test-first and proven at toy scale (CODING_STANDARDS.md, “Proving a change”).

The student as a backbone. A mobilenetv4 family in coffeecv/backbones.py beside dinov3: built through timm, its weights verified by digest, features(x) returning its readouts from one forward pass, frozen and in eval mode once distilled. is_frozen_model, load_frozen_checkpoint and fit_frozen_head --backbone accept it, so the head fit, the probe fit, ood_eval and infer.py run on a student through the code they run on B/16 today. That is what keeps the parity rule.

The driver, coffeecv/distill.py, the second training driver beside fit_frozen_head, with its refusals (dirty tree, stale stages) and its archive. The baseline recipe, each choice the quality-first one:

  • Target. The teacher’s cls_mean, 1536-d, from the B/16 the live model uses, in fp32.
  • Loss. Cosine distance plus smooth-L1 between the student’s projected embedding and the target, both standardised with the teacher’s statistics over the transfer set, so no dimension dominates. One linear projector. No label enters.
  • Same view. The teacher and the student receive the same tensor: one augmentation draw, then both forwards. Views come from head_augment’s pipelines (ADR 0025, ADR 0027), so the student is matched on the views the head is fitted on.
  • Transfer set (D5 (a)). Patches drawn afresh each pass from the seed’s training-split photos by the D17 rule the head fit uses, not a fixed 600 a class; and patches from the guard’s training and validation negatives, a fixed share of each batch.
  • Teacher on line or stored (D3). On a GPU the teacher runs on every view. On the CPU the driver first builds a store of teacher embeddings keyed by (photo, box, view seed) and replays each view from its seed, as many views as the budget allows; a stored embedding is never paired with a view it was not computed on.
  • Student. D1’s variant from D4’s ImageNet weights, every layer trained, at 224 px. AdamW, warm-up then cosine decay, an exponential moving average of the weights; the average is what is evaluated and saved.
  • Watched during training. The mean cosine to the teacher on the seed’s validation patches and on held-back negatives, unaugmented. It is logged, and the last weights are kept, not the best on validation: picking a checkpoint on validation would spend it.
  1. The initial weights downloaded once into models_pretrained/mobilenetv4/, listed in manifest.json with their digest and source, added to DVC and pushed (docs/agents/dvc.md).
  2. Tests for the backbone family: its readouts’ shapes; frozen and in eval mode after loading; a checkpoint whose student digest does not match is refused; the existing DINOv3 tests unchanged and green.
  3. Tests for the driver: the transfer set holds no validation or test photo of its seed and no test-split negative (R4); the teacher and the student are called on identical tensors; a replayed view reproduces the tensor its stored embedding was computed on; two runs at one seed give the same weights on this machine; a run resumed after an interruption gives what an uninterrupted one gives.
  4. A --smoke run end to end: distil for a few steps, save the student, fit a head and a probe on it, classify one photo with infer.py.
  5. On D3’s machine, if it is not this one: docs/agents/remote-sweeps.md followed, the environment synced, the smoke run repeated there.
  6. Checks: scripts/check.sh --full green; the driver and its checkpoint format described in docs/agents/experiments.md; “student”, “teacher” and “transfer set” in GLOSSARY.md.

5. P3 — The pilot at seed 42 stop

StatusOpen not done, 2026-11-20: the ticket closed (D9)
Blocked byP2, D2, Q6

One student, the baseline recipe, a schedule sized from P1 (on a GPU, at least 100 passes over a 54,000-view draw). It answers R1: can this architecture hold the embedding well enough to be worth three seeds.

  1. The baseline settled (Q6): exp283, or B/16 refitted on the current pool.
  2. The reference arm: the published DINOv3 ViT-S/16 through fit_frozen_head at seed 42, with its probe. No distillation; the weights are on disk.
  3. The pilot distilled as a recorded run, committed code, the config archived.
  4. The head and the probe fitted on the student, on each candidate readout (Q2), by fit_frozen_head and fit_ood_probe unchanged.
  5. The report, analysis/ml32/pilot.txt, three columns (B/16, ViT-S/16, the student): val and test macro-F1 and per-class F1; the guard’s test-split figures; per-photo top-1 through the serving path on the seed’s validation and test photos; the student’s top-1 agreement with B/16 per patch and per photo; its cosine to the teacher on validation patches and on negatives, with the distribution’s low tail, not only the mean.
  6. The 20 validation patches with the lowest cosine opened at full size, to see what the student misses.
  7. Stop for the owner: the pilot beside B/16 and ViT-S/16; go on to P4, change the variant (D1), or close. Q2 and Q4 answered here.

6. P4 — Recipe levers, on validation

StatusOpen not done, 2026-11-20: the ticket closed (D9)
Blocked byP3, Q2, Q4

Each lever is one distillation at seed 42 that differs from the best so far in one thing, judged on validation macro-F1, the guard’s validation-side figures and the cosine; test is not read until P5. One seed cannot separate small effects (ADR 0002), so a lever is kept only when it moves validation by more than the head fit’s own seed spread or repairs the guard; otherwise the simpler recipe stays. In this order, within Q4’s budget:

  1. Schedule length, three times the pilot’s: the main lever in the sources [S9].
  2. A spatial term: the student’s last feature map (7×7 at 224 px) matched, through a 1×1 projector, to the teacher’s patch tokens averaged 2×2 to the same grid [S7]. It gives the student 49 targets a view where the baseline gives one.
  3. A logit term: the teacher’s head output on its own embedding as a soft target for the student’s projected embedding through the same frozen head [S10, S11]. It weights the 10-class directions; the head is still refitted afterwards.
  4. Mixed views: mixup or CutMix of two patches with the teacher run on the mixed image [S9]. Teacher on line only. Mixup was closed for the fine-tuned ResNet18 (.out-of-scope/resnet18-recipe-levers.md), which allows a lever again on the DINOv3 pipeline as a paired screen; here it is a distillation input, never a label mix.
  5. Only if the guard’s figures fall: a similarity-preserving loss in place of the plain match [S12], then a generic unlabelled image set in the transfer set (D5 (b), the owner’s choice of set).
  6. Checks: analysis/ml32/levers.txt with every run, kept or not, and the recipe P5 uses stated in full.

  • P4: no test figure appears in levers.txt.
  • P5: each student’s archived transfer set is disjoint from its seed’s validation and test photos and from the guard’s test split; each config differs from its pair’s in the backbone only.
