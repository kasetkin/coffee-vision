1. Summary

Before: the live model was ML-9's dino3b16_segxl0v4_country_aug8_s42, its crops cut by XL0-v4, which was fine-tuned on the pass4 labels. After: XL0-v5, fine-tuned on pass7 by XL0-v4's recipe, cuts every crop; the classifier is retrained on them and live. The work ran in five parts: fine-tune three seeds; judge XL0-v5 against XL0-v4 and the seeds against each other; adopt one and re-derive the tiny-mask threshold; re-crop and retrain; ship, convert and deploy.

2. Background

  • The labels. On 2026-10-25 the owner re-reviewed every label of the segmenter dataset with seg_review label --recheck (pass7; e1f5af2, merge da7dfae), and 6 pool-excluded photos moved to segmenter_positives. pass7 trains 219 positives and 368 negatives and validates on 53 + 85.
  • The recipe. XL0-v4's (ML-9): params.yaml's seg_ft at lr 1e-4 (ML-9 D8), 8 threads, one run at a time; each card equals models/seg/xl0_v4.json's but for the labels and the seed (de2af06).
  • The tiny-mask threshold (seg_min_area_frac) is half the smallest test-positive mask the owner accepted (ML-5 D21); a mask under it counts as none (D18). XL0-v4's was 0.039049.
  • New photos meanwhile. 15 Brazil Sul de Minas photos of 2026-10-26, added by another session: the owner accepted 5 masks (to random_date_raccoon) and declined 10 (to segmenter_positives, appended to the segmenter dataset, 976 to 996 photos; b56b8bd, ab61da2).

3. Before and after

Before

  • Segmenter XL0-v4 (pass4 labels), threshold 0.039049.
  • Test positives passed blind: XL0-v4 64 of 91.
  • Test negatives exactly empty: 156 of 160; empty or tiny 156.
  • Live dino3b16_segxl0v4_country_aug8_s42 (exp273), pool 1,326 photos.

After

  • Segmenter XL0-v5 (xl0_v5, seed 123, pass7 labels), threshold 0.021008.
  • Test positives passed blind: XL0-v5 80 of 91 (exact McNemar p = 0.0015).
  • Test negatives exactly empty: 119 of 160; empty or tiny 154. The OOD probe catches all 160.
  • Live dino3b16_segxl0v5_country_aug8_s42 (exp276), pool 1,376 photos.

4. What does not change

  • The classifier's recipe. Frozen DINOv3 ViT-B/16 cls_mean, C = 0.1, 600/160/160 patches per class, 8 augmented views per training patch (ADR 0025): exp276–278's config differs from exp273–275's only in the decoder and the threshold.
  • The pool's exclusions. labels/ml3/pool_exclude.csv unchanged (D5).
  • The OOD guard. A probe at the fixed 0.5 (ADR 0016), refit on the shipped checkpoint.
  • Serving. LiteRT on the host's GPU; /analyze takes about 8.4 s server-side, its preview 2.1 s, as before.

7. Scope and acceptance

  • XL0-v5 fine-tuned on pass7, three seeds: best epochs score 0.964 (seed 123, epoch 15 of 23), 0.973 (42, epoch 18 of 26) and 0.960 (7, epoch 16 of 24); 5.2–6.0 h of epochs each (c9f2b35, 014d1c1, 16c9da4).
  • XL0-v5 judged blind against XL0-v4 by the owner on the test split: 80 against 64 of 91 positives passed; pool 43 against 34 of 44, segmenter positives 37 against 30 of 47 (58233de, analysis/xl0v5/paired_report.txt).
  • The seeds compared and one adopted (D2, D3); the threshold re-derived (D4).
  • Every session re-cropped (17 segcrop stages, then merge_segpool): 1,379 photos, no D18 fallback, median coverage change 0 (5d7aeb2, analysis/xl0v5/recrop_report.txt); the DINOv3 fixture regenerated (a04ccdb).
  • Classifier exp276–278: val 0.9614–0.9718, test 0.9694–0.9813 patch macro-F1; paired with exp273–275, val −0.0040 to +0.0032 (mean 0.0000), test −0.0113 to +0.0113 (mean +0.0016) (55b0026, EXPERIMENTS_LOG.md).
  • Beside the live model on the photos it did not train on: patch macro-F1 0.9603–0.9803 against 0.9453–0.9807, photo accuracy 0.983–1.000 against 0.967–1.000 (analysis/xl0v5/live_baseline.txt).
  • Shipped exp276 as dino3b16_segxl0v5_country_aug8_s42 with a refit OOD probe: 160/160 test negatives caught, 0/95 bean photos refused (9e7b5bc, analysis/xl0v5/ood_eval.txt).
  • LiteRT export: D7's check passed on 13 fixtures (4014af9); tests, docs and the webapp's default moved to the new model (e64d151, 5014384); check.sh --full passed.
  • DVC pushed and every object verified on dvcnewremote; main pushed to origin.
  • Deployed and verified on the domain: --compare 12 photos, no verdict or top-1 change (p95 largest change 0.0034); latency_ms 8,408–8,428, preview_ms 2,092–2,103.
