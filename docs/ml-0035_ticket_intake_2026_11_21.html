2. Background

The files (exiftool, 2026-11-21). The Pixel 9 Pro and OnePlus 7 Pro JPEGs, 30 in all, carry an Ultra HDR gain map (hdrgm 1.0); the Pixel ones a Display P3 profile, the OnePlus 7 Pro ones sRGB. The KB2005 JPEGs have no profile and Orientation 0, as 27 of the phone's 28 earlier photos do. The Sony G8441 JPEGs are rotated by EXIF (90°, 180°, 270°). Two are large: a 50 MP Pixel JPEG (20261116_122259, 6144×8160) and a 48 MP KB2005 JPEG (20261120_104254, 8000×6000), both under canonicalization's 50 MP cap (ML-19 D27). The DNG (20261116_122301) is DNG 1.6, a colour filter array with JPEG compression, like the dataset's 67 other Pixel 9 Pro DNGs, which LibRaw reads.

What the strip removes (strip_metadata --dry-run on copies in tmp/ml35_intake/new/, 2026-11-21): 46 checked, 46 would be stripped, 0 failing. GPS (433 tags, in every file but the Sony ones), the embedded thumbnail (IFD1, 345 tags), Sony's maker notes (110), Google's HDR+ maker note in extended XMP (30 XMP-GCamera and 30 XMP-xmpNote tags), one IFD0 tag. The DNG's RAW preview stays (owner, 2026-10-05).

The duplicate check (find_duplicates, threshold 0.97, each folder's new photos against every earlier photo of its class in dataset/: 151 for class 013, 226 for class 001): no copy; the nearest are 0.77 (class 013) and 0.76 (class 001). Within the batch, one pair above the 0.85 look threshold: the Pixel JPEG 20261116_122259 and DNG 20261116_122301, 0.88, two seconds apart (D1).

Twins. No two new files share a stem, so no crop overwrites another (ML-25 R2) and no rename is needed (ML-28 D5). The segmenter dataset groups shots within 3 s (ADR 0029), so the D1 pair goes to one split by itself, as earlier Pixel RAW+JPEG pairs did (20261112_144002 and 20261112_144003).
