## The live model

`dino3b16_segxl0v7r_country_aug8_ml37lin_s123` (exp293, ticket [ML-37](ml-0037_ticket_linear_canonical_photo.html)), adopted by the owner 2026-11-28 and live from ML-37's P8 release (not deployed yet): ML-29's recipe and segmenter, the head refitted on crops of the store's photos in ML-37's canonical form, linear Rec.2020 (ADR 0042), read back once to 8-bit sRGB.

- **Pipeline:** the XL0-v7r segmenter (`xl0_v7r_s123`) marks the bean region and crops to it (a mask under 2.85% of the photo counts as none); patches of the crop go through a frozen DINOv3 ViT-B/16 (`cls_mean` readout); a logistic-regression head at C = 0.1 (fixed, ML-1 D3, ADR 0006) scores the 10 countries; patch scores are pooled per photo. No TTA. The OOD probe refuses photos that do not look like the training data (threshold 0.5, ADR 0016). Since ML-37 a canonical photo is a 16-bit linear Rec.2020 JPEG XL (ADR 0042), read back to 8-bit sRGB once (`coffeecv/readback.py`) before both models; the models' resizes run in float (ML-37 D11). A photo's gain map is applied as since ML-29 (ADR 0041).
- **The live input is not training's** (ticket [OPS-22](ops-0022_ticket_front_fast_canonicalization.html), ADR 0036): the front canonicalizes visitors' photos with its own settings (effort 3, quality 90, over 18 MP area-averaged to at most 15 MP), while the model was trained and scored on the store's (effort 7, quality 95, kept up to 60 MP). The scores below describe the store's photos; no metric was measured on the front's beyond the deploy's four examples (OPS-22 D5). Since ML-37 the front also makes the page's preview and sends it first (ADR 0043): on 2 CPUs a photo takes it 0.3-19.4 s to canonicalize and 0.05-1.1 s more for the preview, at most 537 MB (`analysis/ml37/p4_front_cost.jsonl`).
- **Training data:** one pool, `data/segcropped/pool`, every session's crops cut by the segmenter from the store re-canonicalized in ML-37's form (P5, P6: the same 1,811 photos, the same 15 D18 fallbacks, no photo in or out), split 70/15/15 within each class, 600/160/160 patches per class. The head is fitted on each training patch and 8 augmented views of it (ADR 0025). The label is the country (ADR 0012).
- **Scores:** in-distribution val 0.9606, test 0.9493 patch macro-F1 at seed 123; over seeds 42/123/7 (exp292-294) val 0.9443-0.9606, test 0.9382-0.9542. Against today's pipeline refitted on the same pool (exp295-297, ML-37 D29), paired by seed: test -0.0004 on average (-0.0031 to +0.0026), 95% photo-bootstrap interval -0.0026 to +0.0017; val -0.0013 (-0.0021 to -0.0005); per class on test no mean moves more than 0.003 (Guatemala -0.003). No test photo's answer changed at any seed; 0.6-1.0% of test patches changed prediction. ML-37 D13's bar (nothing lost) was met on its three parts (`analysis/ml37/p7_compare_exp292_294.txt`). Not comparable with exp289-291's numbers, which are on the pool before ML-35's and ML-36's photos.
- **OOD guard:** its own probe, refitted on it (ML-37 P7, P8): on the segmenter dataset's test split, 164/164 negatives caught and 2/308 bean photos refused, the same two as ML-29's probe (2/295, on the split before ML-35's and ML-36's photos), both never trained on (`analysis/ml37/p7_ood_eval.txt`, `analysis/ml29/p11_ood_eval.txt`).
- **Known weakness:** `xl0_v7r_s123` finds no beans in a few photos XL0-v6 cropped, green beans and a plain pile among them (ML-16's re-crop: 11 D18 fallbacks against 5); such a photo is classified on the whole frame. The page's Vietnam example left the list for this (ML-16 D12).
- **Serving:** the model's LiteRT conversion on the web host's GPU, without torch (ticket ML-7, ADR 0019, ADR 0020); about 10 s server-side per 12 MP photo since ML-21's release (2026-11-10: 9.7-10.0 s, of it 1.4-1.5 s decoding its canonical JPEG XL on four threads; the 50 MP grinder example 14.6 s, 5.7 s of it decoding). ML-16's release had made it about 12 s (a 12 MP photo 12.1 s, 3.7 s of it decoding, ML-19; the 50 MP example 24.8 s, 15.9 s decoding), against 8.4 s before. [inference_path.html](inference_path.html) traces a photo through it step by step; [webapp/README.md](../webapp/README.md) covers running and deploying it.

`models/` also holds the eight models before it, `dino3b16_segxl0v7r_country_aug8_ml29gm_s123` (exp290, ticket ML-29, live 2026-11-18 to ML-37's release), `dino3b16_segxl0v7r_country_aug8_ml27_s42` (exp283, ticket ML-27, live 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-18), `dino3b16_segxl0v7r_country_aug8_s42` (exp280, ticket ML-16, live 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-12), `dino3b16_segxl0v6_country_aug8_s42` (exp279, ticket ML-13, live 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-08), `dino3b16_segxl0v5_country_aug8_s42` (exp276, live 2026-10-27 to 2026-10-29), `dino3b16_segxl0v4_country_aug8_s42` (exp273, ticket ML-9, live 2026-10-24 to 2026-10-27), `dino3b16_segxl0v3_country_aug8_s123` (exp271, ticket ML-8, live 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-24) and `allrigs_dino3b16_segxl0v3_country_s42` (exp267, ticket ML-6, live 2026-10-13 to 2026-10-20), not retired.

## Reading the numbers

Quote ranges over seeds, not a single run: seed-to-seed spread is wider than most of the effects being measured, which is why adoptions require a *paired* multi-seed check (ADR 0002). Val patch macro-F1 selects adoptions; test is reported.

**Every val and test score this pipeline now produces is in-distribution, not an estimate of real-world accuracy.** Val and test photos come from the same bean bags and the same cameras as the training photos, so they say nothing about how a model does on a new camera or a new scoop of beans (ML-1, R2). They also sit near ceiling (~0.97) and barely separate choices (R1). Never compare one with a fold-era cross-camera number: 0.887 and 0.975 for the same shipped recipe answer different questions (R3), and `experiments/index.csv` keeps the two kinds of column apart (`xrig_*` is blank for runs since ML-1, meaning "not measured"). Until a generalization test set exists (fresh scoops, a new phone -- its own ticket), camera- or scoop-level drift shows up only in manual spot-checks and the OOD guard, and neither gates a change.

Roast state is uneven across countries (pool photos, `data/segcropped/pool/merge_manifest.json`): Brazil (class 013, 10 photos), Colombia (class 014, 19) and Guatemala (26) have roasted photos beside their green ones, Ethiopia and Vietnam one roasted photo each; Kenya and Costa Rica are green only; Indonesia, Peru and Rwanda are roasted only. For the one-state countries roast state alone narrows the answer, and no number above says how a model does on them in the other state.

Those counts are ML-8's pool. ML-9's added the 2026-10-21 photos, green Indonesia (class 015, a coffee of its own) and green Peru (class 011) among them, and 33 more Brazil photos, less the owner's exclusions ([ML-9](ml-0009_ticket_green_origins_retrain.html)); it was the first model to see Indonesia and Peru green. XL0-v5's added 32 more Brazil photos (2026-10-24 to 2026-10-26) and 18 the owner readmitted from the exclusions on XL0-v4's masks (2026-10-24). XL0-v6's was the same 1,376 photos. ML-16's added the owner's photos of 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-06 (87 on 2026-11-05 and 2026-11-06 alone: Ethiopia, Brazil, Indonesia, Colombia), less the five example photos (ADR 0032) and the owner's exclusions, 1,493 photos in all, re-cropped by `xl0_v7r_s123`; `merge_manifest.json` has the counts per class and session.

## How it got here

- **Fine-tuned ResNet18** (the first model): bean-unit patch sizing at 4-7 beans, MixStyle p=0.5, random erasing p=0.5, 100 epochs / patience 20, TTA. Cross-camera patch macro-F1 0.7908-0.8025 over seeds 42, 123 and 7 (exp200-203, exp232-239). No ResNet18 model ships any more, and its code was removed on 2026-10-20.
- **Frozen DINOv3 ViT-B/16 + linear head** (exp220-231, exp240-254): cross-camera patch macro-F1 **0.8873** (exp240-251, 3 seeds × 4 folds), 0.9489 pooled per photo. The owner selected it on those folds; live since 2026-09-27 (`docs/dinov3_integration_plan.md`).
- **ML-1** (2026-09-29): the leave-one-camera-out folds retired; all cameras equal, none held out (ADR 0010). Cross-camera numbers above come from those folds.
- **ML-2** (live 2026-10-03): a learned bean segmentation stage (EfficientViT-SAM-L0) in front of the classifier (ADR 0011).
- **ML-3** (live 2026-10-06): country classes instead of one class per coffee.
- **ML-5** (live 2026-10-09) and **ML-6** (live 2026-10-13): the XL0-v2, then XL0-v3, segmenter.
- **ML-7** (live 2026-10-17): serving on LiteRT instead of PyTorch.
- **ML-8** (live 2026-10-20): one pool for every session and an augmented head.
- **ML-9** (live 2026-10-24): the XL0-v4 segmenter, and green Indonesia and Peru in training.
- **XL0-v5** (live 2026-10-27): the XL0-v5 segmenter, fine-tuned on the pass7 labels.
- **ML-13** (live 2026-10-29): the XL0-v6 segmenter, fine-tuned on the pass10 labels at seed 42.
- **ML-16** (live 2026-11-08): the XL0-v7r segmenter (XL0-v7 refined at seed 123), canonical photos (ML-19), 87 new photos.
- **ML-27** (live 2026-11-12): the head refitted on the pool with ML-25's 188 new photos.
- **ML-29** (live 2026-11-15, 2026-11-16 and 2026-11-18): photos read by their colour declarations, then HDR main images tone-mapped (ADR 0040); then gain maps applied (ADR 0041) and the head refitted on the gain-map crops.
- **ML-37** (adopted 2026-11-28): the canonical photo linear Rec.2020, read back once; the preview made at the front; the head refitted on the re-cropped pool, the live model above.

Every other model is retired; each one's card, sidecars and `.pt.dvc` are in git history and its `.pt` in the DVC cache and remote. `EXPERIMENTS_LOG.md`'s exp270-272 entry lists the last ones retired.

## Where runs are written up

`EXPERIMENTS_LOG.md` holds the experiment history through exp279; exp280-282 (ML-16) are written up in `analysis/ml16/` and the ML-16 ticket, exp283-285 (ML-27) in `analysis/ml27/`, exp286-291 (ML-29) in `analysis/ml29/README.md` (P10). exp292-297 (ML-37) in `analysis/ml37/p7_compare_exp292_294.txt` and the ML-37 plan's P7. exp264-269 (ML-5, ML-6) are summarised there by pointer only; their write-ups are in `docs/ml-0005_plan_segmenter_dataset.html` and `docs/ml-0006_plan_segmenter_retrain.html`. Each ML-n ticket's plan (`docs/ml-*_plan_*.html`) has the fuller record of its runs; `experiments/index.csv` has every run.

In the log, phases 1-14 (through exp105) were written contemporaneously and are authoritative prose. Phases 15-17 (exp106-175) were **reconstructed on 2026-09-03** from `index.csv` and the archived configs — the numbers are recomputed and exact, but the reasoning-as-it-happened is genuinely lost for those runs, and the section says so. Per-experiment metrics, configs, curves and predictions are archived in `experiments/` — see `experiments/README.md`; `experiments/index.csv` is the one-row-per-run summary and is regenerated from the archive directories by `archive_experiment.rebuild_index()`, never hand-edited.

Ticket ML-1 and its implementation plan (`docs/ml-0001_ticket_retire_cross_rig.html`, `docs/ml-0001_plan_retire_cross_rig.html`) record how and why the fold protocol was retired. `docs/dataset_training_reorg_plan.md` records the dataset and rig reorganisation behind the camera-rig numbers.
