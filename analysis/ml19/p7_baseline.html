Read this first: the test split is not exp279's

Two changes at once P6's re-crop changed the pool: random_date_raccoon's crops were stale (133 of its photos renamed since, 50 added) and three photos moved to example_photos/, so the pool holds 1,429 photos against 1,379. The split is drawn per class from the pool, so it changed too: of exp279's 209 test photos, 103 are in this test split; 114 of its 217 are new to it. The whole-split numbers mix the two changes. The paired section separates them.

The photos with the older names score as the shared ones do. The 25 dated photos hold 34 of the split's 45 new errors. They come from the session's mix of phones (KB2005, G8441, OnePlus, Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Pixel 7, Pixel 9 Pro), with two Pixel 9 Pro photos of Ethiopia wrong on every patch (20261103_110934, 20261103_110947). The Ethiopia and Colombia recall drops are mostly those photos.

Of the 89, 20 were in exp279's val split and the other 69 in neither its test nor its val, so in its train split. They are easier than truly held-out photos. That makes the drop, if anything, an underestimate of what the new photos cost.
