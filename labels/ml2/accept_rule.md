Accept the mask only if all of these hold: (1) nothing inside the outline is tray, rim, table, hand or reflection, apart from a few pixels along the edge; (2) no part of a coffee-bean pile bigger than about one bean lies outside the outline; (3) the outline covers each pile as a whole, including the gaps, shadows and anything lying inside it, and doesn't trace individual beans; (4) if the photo has no pile of mostly whole coffee beans (green or roasted; not other seeds, grains, legumes, ground coffee, cherries, parchment or mostly broken beans), the mask is empty or covers at most a few beans. Otherwise, decline.
